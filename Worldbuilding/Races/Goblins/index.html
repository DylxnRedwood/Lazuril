Goblins¶

Goblins are small, quick, and stubbornly adaptable people found across the rougher margins of Arkhaven, especially in jungles, ruins, tunnels, marshes, frontier towns, and places larger powers do not fully control.

Clan and Warren Peoples¶

Batiri goblins are one of the recognised peoples of the Zul’Akari Clans, where they live among the jungle, marsh, lake, and ruin territories of the north-west. Other goblin groups exist in caves, old fortifications, abandoned mines, city underworks, battlefield ruins, and hidden camps across the continent.

Goblins rarely hold formal power in large states, but they are difficult to remove once established. Their communities survive through numbers, cunning, salvage, traps, scavenging, bargaining, and a willingness to make homes in places others consider worthless or cursed.

Reputation and Survival¶

Most larger peoples view goblins with suspicion, often reducing them to pests, thieves, raiders, or servants of stronger monsters. This reputation is not always fair, but goblin bands are practical enough to use fear and confusion when it keeps them alive.

In modern Arkhaven, goblins may be enemies, guides, miners, smugglers, tinkers, scouts, relic-looters, labourers, or uneasy members of mixed frontier communities. Their greatest strength is not power. It is refusal to disappear.
