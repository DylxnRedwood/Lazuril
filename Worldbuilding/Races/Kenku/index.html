Kenku¶

Kenku are a sharp-eyed, quick-handed people found in cities, ports, clan territories, caravan camps, and northern jungle settlements across Arkhaven.

Mimics and Messengers¶

Kenku are known for mimicry, memory, scavenging, trade talk, coded speech, and survival in crowded or dangerous places. In the northern regions they appear among the Veylrath Clans, Myruun Clans, and Zul’Akari Clans. They are also common enough in frontier settlements, dock districts, and travelling camps to be recognised across much of the continent.

Many kenku serve as messengers, scouts, lookouts, translators, thieves, performers, spies, shrine attendants, market runners, and record-keepers of spoken things. Their ability to repeat voices, warnings, passwords, bird calls, and overheard conversations gives them a reputation that is both useful and unsettling.

Reputation and Survival¶

Kenku communities are usually small, practical, and socially flexible. They often live under the protection of stronger groups, but this does not make them weak. Kenku survive by knowing what others forget, hearing what others miss, and leaving before trouble closes around them.

In modern Arkhaven, they are treated with mixed suspicion and respect. A kenku can be dismissed as a street mimic or valued as the only witness who remembers exactly what was said.
