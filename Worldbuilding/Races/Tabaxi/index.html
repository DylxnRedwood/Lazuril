Tabaxi¶

Tabaxi are a feline humanoid people found primarily in the northern territories of Arkhaven, especially within the tropical rainforests, dense jungle, river basins, and coastal forest regions.

Northern Clanlands¶

They are most strongly associated with the clan-held lands of the north, including the Veylrath Clans, Jhakur Clans, Myruun Clans, and Zul’Akari Clans. These regions are not ruled as single centralised kingdoms, and many tabaxi communities remain organised around kin-groups, treetop settlements, river clans, shrine families, and travelling bands.

Tabaxi culture is shaped by movement, curiosity, oral tradition, sign language, hunting, trade secrecy, and survival in difficult forest terrain. Many settlements are built high in the canopy, with hidden platforms, rope bridges, rain collectors, lookout perches, and concealed nursery spaces.

Reputation and Survival¶

Outsiders often know tabaxi as agile scouts, guides, traders, messengers, and storytellers. Their knowledge of northern forest routes is valuable, but rarely given away freely. A tabaxi guide may lead travellers through the jungle, but most will never reveal the full location of a clan home or canopy road.

Tabaxi communities are cautious because they have a long history of defending themselves from predators, raiders, traffickers, and those who attempted to capture young tabaxi for sale. This has made secrecy a cultural necessity rather than simple suspicion.

Some tabaxi live in port towns, trade cities, or mixed frontier settlements, but most remain culturally tied to the hidden communities of the northern forests. Their central concern is survival without captivity, trade without exposure, and freedom without surrendering the safety of their young.
