Dwarves¶

Dwarves are one of the major civilised peoples of Arkhaven. They are most strongly associated with the south-eastern mountain ranges of the continent, where their oldest kingdoms, deepest mines, and largest strongholds are located.

Mountain Realms¶

Their principal homeland is The Throne of Khuldovar, centred on the great mountain-capital of Khuldovar. The Dornhal Empire is also dwarven, though culturally and politically distinct, having split from Khuldovar many centuries ago.

Large dwarven holds, mining enclaves, fortress-cities, tunnel stations, and trade halls exist throughout the mountain regions of Arkhaven. Smaller dwarven communities are also found near human cities, major construction projects, river routes, strategic passes, and mixed states such as the Windmere Assembly.

Stone, Craft and Memory¶

Dwarven civilisation is shaped by stone, metal, pressure, heat, and depth. Their settlements are commonly built within mountains rather than upon them, and many dwarven societies measure wealth, prestige, and history through craftsmanship, engineering, oath-keeping, and the endurance of their halls.

Dwarves are widely respected as miners, masons, smiths, armourers, engineers, bridge-builders, and fortification experts. Human and mixed states often rely on dwarven skill for roads, walls, tunnels, weapons, locks, and stonework.

Their societies are usually organised around clans, guilds, ancestral lineages, and sworn obligations. Political disputes between dwarven powers can be severe, but dwarven contracts are generally treated across Arkhaven as among the most reliable in the world.

In modern Arkhaven, dwarves remain economically powerful beyond their population size. Their mountains provide metal, stone, engineering knowledge, military strength, and deep historical memory. To many dwarves, the deep earth is not only a source of wealth. It is history itself.
