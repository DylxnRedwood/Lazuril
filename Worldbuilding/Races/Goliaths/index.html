Goliaths¶

Goliaths are a large, powerful people most strongly associated with the harsh highlands, dry plains, and mountain edges of the Kharad Wildlands and the wider Thornbelt.

Wildland Tribes¶

Goliath communities are tribal, mobile, spiritual, and honour-bound. They live in mountain camps, highland routes, dry river valleys, cliff shelters, and seasonal gathering grounds where survival depends on endurance, strength, memory, and respect for the land.

They are common in the colder southern mountains of the Kharad Wildlands, though many roam down into the plains. Some goliath bands trade with Loxodon tribes, dwarven prospectors, or nomadic human and halfling groups, but they rarely accept foreign rule easily.

Honour and Spirit¶

Goliath culture values personal honour, physical trial, ancestral memory, spiritual signs, oathkeeping, and proven usefulness. Boasting is common, but empty boasting is despised. A person’s worth is measured by what they endure, what they protect, and whether they carry their obligations without complaint.

To settled peoples, goliaths are often seen as wildings, raiders, guides, mountain warriors, or spiritual nomads. To themselves, they are keepers of hard places, bound to winds, stone, beasts, ancestors, and the old laws of survival.
