Vaelcairn¶

Vaelcairn is a coastal city of council houses, vine-covered balconies, sea gardens, music halls, and open public squares.

It is one of the main gathering places of the Houses of Bârathanaear, where noble families argue, vote, feast, and negotiate with more charm than honesty. Wealth is visible here, but it is softened by civic ceremony and the expectation that even old houses must answer to public reputation.
