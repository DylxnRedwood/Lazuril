Vaelornith¶

Vaelornith is a city of sacred groves, healing houses, prophecy halls, and magical instruction linked to House Vaelorn.

Queen Lúthariel Vaelorn’s patronage gives the city great prestige among mages, artists, healers, and keepers of old rites. Its gardens are treated as libraries written in leaf, water, and moonlit stone.
