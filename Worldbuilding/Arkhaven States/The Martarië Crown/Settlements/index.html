Settlements of the Martarië Crown¶

Maerithal¶

Maerithal is one of the great royal cities of the Martarië Crown, associated with House Maereth, royal law, military command, and the formal memory of elven rulership.

White stone halls stand beneath ancient trees, and public spaces preserve roots, springs, and old stones rather than clearing them away. Decrees sealed in the name of King Aurelion Maereth are witnessed here by magistrates, archive-keepers, and noble retainers.

Vaelornith¶

Vaelornith is a city of sacred groves, healing houses, prophecy halls, and magical instruction linked to House Vaelorn.

Queen Lúthariel Vaelorn’s patronage gives the city great prestige among mages, artists, healers, and keepers of old rites. Its gardens are treated as libraries written in leaf, water, and moonlit stone.

Silvaren Bay¶

Silvaren Bay is a coastal harbour city of shipwrights, fleet-commanders, pearl markets, glass-roofed halls, and sea-facing estates.

Its ships carry Martarië goods, envoys, musicians, and scholars along the coast, guarded by elven captains who treat seamanship as both craft and ceremony.

Lethar Grove¶

Lethar Grove is a smaller inland settlement built around a surviving warded grove from the time of the Green Silence.

It is quiet, formal, and watched by druids and record-keepers. Weddings, funerals, and oaths taken here are considered especially difficult to break.
