Aldmere¶

Aldmere is a wealthy assembly city set among broad river roads, civic halls, public gardens, guildhouses, and granaries.

It is one of the principal meeting places of the Grand Assembly of Windmere. Humans, dwarves, gnomes, halflings, and elves all maintain formal districts here, each proud of their own customs and equally convinced the others need reminding how government works.
