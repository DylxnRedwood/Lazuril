Settlements of the Myruun Clans¶

Shellroot¶

Shellroot is one of the main clan-house settlements of the Myruun, built on raised root-platforms above black-water channels and mangrove ground.

Tortle elders meet beneath shell-marked halls to settle fishing rights, marriage obligations, shrine duties, and matters tied to the Rootbound Pact. Guests are welcomed carefully, watched closely, and expected to understand that patience is not the same as weakness.

Varn Tulo¶

Varn Tulo is a forest gnome root-village hidden beneath leaves, mushroom groves, disguised doors, and warning charms.

It is known for trapcraft, concealed storehouses, herbal work, illusion magic, and small devices that keep larger enemies guessing.

Redcliff Shrine¶

Redcliff Shrine stands on a scar-facing cliff where Myruun tide-readers watch strange waters and mark changes in the Godscar.

Relics found nearby are brought here before being traded, sealed, or destroyed according to clan law.

Caltha Cove¶

Caltha Cove is a coastal settlement of fishers, canoe docks, shell-dyers, storm shrines, and treaty goods.

It deals more openly with Crown merchants than most Myruun places, though every bargain is remembered longer than outsiders expect.
