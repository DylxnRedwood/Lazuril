Shellroot¶

Shellroot is one of the main clan-house settlements of the Myruun Clans, built on raised root-platforms above river channels and mangrove ground.

Tortle elders meet beneath shell-marked halls to settle fishing rights, shrine duties, trade terms, and matters tied to the Rootbound Pact.
