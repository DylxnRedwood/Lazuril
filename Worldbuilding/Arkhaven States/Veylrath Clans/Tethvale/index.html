Tethvale¶

Tethvale is one of the major river-trade settlements of the Veylrath Clans, built high among the rainforest canopy above the broad bends of the Saelun River.

It is small by human city standards, but large by Tabaxi and Veylrath standards. It is not a city in the Crown sense. There are no straight roads, stone avenues, or walls pretending the jungle is something to be conquered. Tethvale is made from suspended platforms, rope bridges, carved living branches, watch-nests, woven lifts, river stairs, and timber halls built around trees too old for anyone sensible to cut.

The settlement sits where several canopy trails meet the Saelun, making it one of the safest and most important navigation points in the north-eastern Veylrain Expanse.

Kelshara lies to the north-east along older canopy paths, Vurokai lies south-west where the rainforest thins toward savannah, and Nareth Lo sits further downriver where the Saelun feeds into warmer wetland channels near the Lo-Varin Pools.

Canopy and River¶

Tethvale’s upper settlement belongs to the trees. Families, traders, navigators, storytellers, scouts, and river-speakers live among layered platforms hidden by leaves, moss, and hanging flowers.

Below, the riverfront is busy with narrow boats, ferry lines, fish traps, cargo nets, raft pens, and guarded landing stages. Goods move upward by rope lift and pulley, while travellers are watched carefully before being allowed into the higher walkways.

The Saelun River is treated as road, boundary, memory, and warning. Its seasonal floods decide when trade moves quickly and when the jungle closes around everyone.

Trade and Navigation¶

Tethvale is known for river pilots, map-keepers, ferry families, quiet carriers, and traders who understand which waters are safe after heavy rain.

Foreign merchants are allowed here more often than in deeper Veylrath settlements, though never without guides. They trade metal tools, salt, cloth, medicines, carved goods, preserved fruit, feathers, river pearls, rare woods, and stories worth repeating.

The Veylrath do not sell maps freely. A route learned from Tethvale is usually given as a spoken chain of warnings, landmarks, spirits, smells, and turns, because a drawn line does not tell you what is waiting beneath the water.

Clan Life¶

Tethvale is loud, watchful, and alive at nearly every hour. Children learn knots before letters, hunters sleep in shaded hammocks above the docks, and elders sit near the bridge-posts telling travellers which questions are foolish.

The settlement is open by Veylrath standards, but it is not careless. Ruin relics are declared before entering, weapons are bound at the river stairs, and anyone travelling deeper into the Expanse leaves with a guide or not at all.
