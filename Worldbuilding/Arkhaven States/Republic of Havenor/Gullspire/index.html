Gullspire¶

Gullspire is a sea-facing town beneath the lone coastal mountain used by sailors as a landmark.

Its slopes hold signal towers, goat farms, shrines to safe arrival, and lookout posts watching the bay. The town is poorer than Valewick, but its warnings have saved more ships than its people are ever paid for.
