Valewick¶

Valewick is the capital city of the Republic of Havenor, built around the protected harbour where the Ark rests at Founding Quay.

The Mariner’s Senate meets here, close to the Ark, the First Manifest, the Oath Deck, and the working docks that still define Havenori politics. It is not a polished capital in the style of the Vermillion Crown. It is a busy harbour city of shipwrights, fishers, senators, clerics, guilds, old Ark families, ropewalks, timber yards, shrines, and hard public argument.
