Settlements of the Republic of Havenor¶

Valewick¶

Valewick is the capital city of the Republic of Havenor, built around the protected harbour where the Ark rests at Founding Quay.

The Mariner’s Senate meets here, close to the Ark, the First Manifest, the Oath Deck, and the working docks that still define Havenori politics. It is not a polished capital in the style of the Vermillion Crown. It is a busy harbour city of shipwrights, fishers, senators, clerics, guilds, old Ark families, ropewalks, timber yards, shrines, and hard public argument.

Keldmere¶

Keldmere is an inland lake town south of the bay, built around fisheries, irrigation channels, ferry routes, and safer freshwater settlement.

It feeds the republic, sends fish and grain north, and provides a calmer settlement zone away from the danger of the bay islands.

Thornwatch¶

Thornwatch sits near the northern frontier, where timber camps, old watch posts, and contested wilds begin to press against Havenori claims.

It is a tense place of militia musters, logging contracts, frontier shrines, and families who know that maps drawn in the capital do not make the jungle safer.

Gullspire¶

Gullspire is a sea-facing town beneath the lone coastal mountain used by sailors as a landmark.

Its slopes hold signal towers, goat farms, shrines to safe arrival, and lookout posts watching the bay. The town is poorer than Valewick, but its warnings have saved more ships than its people are ever paid for.
