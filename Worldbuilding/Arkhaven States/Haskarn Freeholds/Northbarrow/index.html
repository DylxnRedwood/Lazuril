Northbarrow¶

Northbarrow sits near the pressure lines between Haskarn expansion, Zul’Akari territory, and the Kharad Wildlands.

It is a frontier town of watch posts, survey stakes, militia musters, and farms built with one eye on the fields and one eye on the tree line.
