Settlements of the Leridian Concord¶

Caldris Vey¶

Caldris Vey is an academy city of the Leridian Concord, built around colleges of abjuration, divination, coded speech, and military engineering.

Its streets are warded, its towers are numbered rather than named, and its public squares are watched by both magistrates and counter-scrying offices. Students, officers, scribes, spies, and artificers all pass through the city before being assigned to war offices or border work.

Eastwatch Mere¶

Eastwatch Mere guards one of the inland lake routes that once connected Leridian trade to wider southern waters.

Since the Vermillion blockade, the town has become more military than commercial. Warehouses have become storehouses, ferry towers have become signal stations, and every boatman knows which questions not to ask near the docks.

Halvern Gate¶

Halvern Gate is a fortified border town facing the Crown-held routes of the Crimson Chain.

It is a place of engineers, scouts, wardens, bridge plans, false merchants, intercepted letters, and soldiers trained to fight under spellfire.

Veyrglass¶

Veyrglass is a quieter college town known for forensic spellcraft, illusion detection, and archive courts.

Religious claims, forged documents, strange relics, and magical residue from battlefields are often brought here for examination before the Concord decides whether something is evidence, propaganda, or a weapon.
