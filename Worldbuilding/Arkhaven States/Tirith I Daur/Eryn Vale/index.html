Eryn Vale¶

Eryn Vale is a small valley settlement of hunters, herbalists, bowyers, and beast-keepers.

Its halls are built into living wood and mossy slopes rather than cleared ground. Outsiders usually pass through without ever seeing the true village, as the paths turn away unless a guide chooses otherwise.
