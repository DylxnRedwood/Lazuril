Caer Oryn¶

Caer Oryn is the capital city of The Vermillion Crown, and one of the wealthiest and most politically important cities in Arkhaven.

The city sits on the River Oryn, with its lower districts built along the water and its seat of power raised farther inland, where the main keep stands on a hilltop above the city.

Capital of the Crown¶

Caer Oryn sits at the heart of Vermillion power, serving as the seat of government, taxation, military authority, trade control, and noble administration. Its influence reaches far beyond the city because the Crown controls movement through the Godscar and the Crimson Chain.

The city is walled, but not entirely enclosed. Its strongest walls protect the main approaches, higher districts, military roads, and administrative centres. The riverfront is more open, but heavily watched through quays, chain gates, patrol posts, customs houses, and Crown officials who monitor what enters and leaves by water.

The lower districts contain the docks, warehouses, markets, river labour offices, workshops, taverns, modest housing, and most of the working life of the city. These areas are busy, useful, and tightly managed rather than left to rot.

Farther from the river, the land rises into wealthier and more controlled districts. The main keep stands on the hilltop above them, removed from the noise of the waterfront and visible from many of the roads leading into Caer Oryn.

Caer Oryn is known for guarded gates, controlled river access, regulated districts, wealthy high wards, and a political culture built around permission, rank, debt, favour, and surveillance.

Current Status¶

Caer Oryn will be developed as the primary capital settlement of The Vermillion Crown.
