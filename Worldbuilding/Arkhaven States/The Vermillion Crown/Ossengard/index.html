Ossengard¶

Ossengard is a fortress city on the hard road between the Crown’s interior estates and the scar-facing military corridors.

It is built in stepped rings around a weather-beaten drill plateau. The lower ring holds armour stores, mule yards, oath houses, repair sheds, and inns for soldiers in transit. The upper ring belongs to officers, instructors, magistrates, and families whose children are being prepared for state service.

Military Schooling¶

Ossengard is known for the Crown’s harshest officer schools.

Cadets come here to learn logistics, siege discipline, map reading, winter marching, bridge defence, and the art of holding a road after retreat would be easier. The city is full of practice towers, measuring posts, mock gates, signal fires, and old battle diagrams carved into classroom walls.

Graduating from Ossengard carries prestige, but failing there leaves a mark that can follow a family for years.

City Character¶

Ossengard is orderly without being elegant.

Its people prize punctuality, spare speech, polished boots, clean weapons, and written proof of authority. Taverns close early near the academy ring, and even private feasts tend to sound like briefings.

Tensions¶

The city produces loyal officers, but also ambitious ones.

Some instructors believe Maeron Cadris saved the Crown. Others quietly worry that a state trained to obey a single ruler may forget how to survive him.
