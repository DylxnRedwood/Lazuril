Rhalstoke¶

Rhalstoke is an industrial riverworks city where the Crown builds the chains, winches, bridge teeth, pump housings, and pressure wheels used across its controlled waterways.

The city sits beside a fast bend of the River Oryn, where water power, imported ore, timber shipments, and state contracts meet in a constant grind of labour. Its skyline is not graceful. It is stacks, gantries, hoists, cranes, slate roofs, furnace sheds, and signal horns.

Riverworks¶

Rhalstoke does not simply make weapons.

Its workshops produce the practical machinery that lets the Vermillion Crown control passage: lock gears, chain gate teeth, flood braces, wagon axles, river cranes, bridge pins, harbour pulleys, and iron fittings for military roads.

The Armature Directorate keeps inspectors in the city at all times. Poor work is treated as sabotage if it reaches a bridge, gate, or crossing.

Workers and Wards¶

The people of Rhalstoke are skilled, blunt, and proud of useful craft.

Guild families live beside contracted labourers, veteran machinists, charcoal burners, barge-haulers, apprentice engineers, and state auditors. The city has wealth, but it smells of hot metal, wet timber, oil, coal dust, and river mud.

Tensions¶

Rhalstoke knows how much of the Crown depends on its mechanisms.

Workers grumble about long hours and Directorate oversight, but few are careless. A failed chain gate can drown a district, open a route to enemies, or ruin a family name overnight.
