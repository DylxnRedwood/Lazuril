Karthrest¶

Karthrest is a Crown lock-city built where a set of engineered water stairs forces river traffic to slow, register, and pay before continuing through the central passage routes.

Its streets are narrow, paved in dark stone, and numbered by district rather than named by local custom. Barges enter under painted signal arms, while customs officers record cargo marks from balconies above the lock basins.

Function¶

Karthrest exists to turn movement into revenue.

The city houses toll courts, cargo ledgers, confiscation yards, bond houses, licensed pilot guilds, and barracks for the Passage Directorate. A merchant can lose a season here over a damaged seal, an unpaid fee, or a single missing name from a charter.

Unlike Caer Oryn, Karthrest does not pretend to be the heart of the Crown. It is a counting machine with walls, bells, floodgates, clerks, chain crews, auditors, and soldiers.

Civic Mood¶

People in Karthrest speak in docket numbers, cargo weights, canal times, and family permits. Children grow up learning the sound of lock-bells before they learn old songs.

The city is prosperous, but tense. Delayed boats crowd the holding quays, foreign captains curse in rented rooms, and locals know that small mistakes can become legal traps.

Tensions¶

Karthrest is hated by smugglers and quietly feared by honest traders.

The Passage Directorate claims the city keeps the heartlands safe. Rivermen say it keeps the Crown rich. Both are true.
