Tharnok Gate¶

Tharnok Gate is a fortified surface town guarding one of the great roads descending from the Anvilspine Range.

Merchants, soldiers, mule trains, foreign envoys, and tunnel wardens pass through its gates under the eyes of stone captains and toll clerks.
