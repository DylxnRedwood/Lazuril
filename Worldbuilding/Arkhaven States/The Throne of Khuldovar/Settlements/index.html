Settlements of the Throne of Khuldovar¶

Khuldovar Hold¶

Khuldovar Hold is the royal mountain seat of King Barund Khargrim Anvilthane, built deep within Khar Dûmvar.

Its halls contain royal courts, clan vaults, oath-stones, forge-shrines, and roads cut through the living mountain. It is old, formal, and heavy with ancestral law. Nothing important is agreed there until it has been spoken, witnessed, and carved.

Tharnok Gate¶

Tharnok Gate is a fortified surface town guarding one of the great roads descending from the Anvilspine Range.

Merchants, soldiers, mule trains, foreign envoys, and tunnel wardens pass through its gates under the eyes of stone captains and toll clerks.

Barak Vuld¶

Barak Vuld is a mining settlement known for gemcutters, deep iron, and old disputes between clan claims.

Its workshops are wealthy, but every new vein risks argument between guild, temple, crown, and family.

Kharund Bridge¶

Kharund Bridge stands above a cold mountain river that feeds the lowlands below.

It is both a settlement and a piece of dwarven pride: a stone bridge-town of brewers, masons, river wardens, and shrine-keepers who treat the water as useful, dangerous, and legally accounted for.
