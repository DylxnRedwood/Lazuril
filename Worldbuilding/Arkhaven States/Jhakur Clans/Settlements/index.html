Settlements of the Jhakur Clans¶

Ssar Varkhun¶

Ssar Varkhun is a hidden lake settlement on the northern waters of Lake Varkhuna, built among reeds, mud islands, half-submerged platforms, and false shorelines.

It belongs to the scaled and marsh-born peoples of the Jhakur. Fishing, ritual, hunting, and border watching all happen here, though outsiders usually mistake the settlement for empty wetland until it is too late to leave politely.

Korrash Teeth¶

Korrash Teeth is a defensive clan settlement marked by carved bone, hanging warnings, and narrow river approaches.

War-leaders, brood elders, and venom shamans gather here when Havenori movement threatens old nesting rights.

Naga’s Low¶

Naga’s Low lies near forbidden pools and vine-covered stonework claimed by serpent-touched families and ruin priests.

Its people treat the old ruins as living obligations, not treasure. Objects taken without permission are considered stolen even if found lying in the mud.

Gruth Mire¶

Gruth Mire is a grung and lizardfolk settlement deep in hot marshland.

It is known for poison craft, wetland ambush paths, frog-song warnings, and healers who know which venoms cure and which ones teach obedience.
