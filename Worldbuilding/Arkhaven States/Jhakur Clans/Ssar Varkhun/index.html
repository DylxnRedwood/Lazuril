Ssar Varkhun¶

Ssar Varkhun is a hidden lake settlement on the northern waters of Lake Varkhuna, built among reeds, mud islands, half-submerged platforms, and false shorelines.

It belongs to the scaled and marsh-born peoples of the Jhakur Clans. Fishing, ritual, hunting, and border watching all happen here, though outsiders usually mistake the settlement for empty wetland until it is too late to leave politely.
