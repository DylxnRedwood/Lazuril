Settlements of the Eldermarch Court¶

Calen-Êlwen¶

Calen-Êlwen is the ceremonial heart of the Eldermarch Court, named for the realm itself and guarded by the six elder houses.

It is less a city of crowds than a court of ancestry, law, and old claim. Family halls stand beneath jungle trees and high stone arches, with bloodline records, land charters, and druidic circles preserved as proof of ancient authority.

Vaeranthil¶

Vaeranthil is a highland estate town controlled by one of the older noble families.

Its terraces overlook dry woodland and mountain roads, and its people are trained from childhood to understand rank, inheritance, and the quiet violence of legal precedent.

Thir Aelorn¶

Thir Aelorn lies in the northern jungle, where Eldermarch druids tend living boundaries and old hunting paths.

The settlement is beautiful, but not kind. Its groves are shaped, measured, and guarded according to elven design rather than left wild.

Elandor March¶

Elandor March is a southern border settlement facing the Houses of Bârathanaear.

It is full of patrol lodges, claim-stones, surveyors, retainers, and minor nobles trying to turn old maps into present power.
