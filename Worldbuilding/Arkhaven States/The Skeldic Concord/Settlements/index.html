Settlements of the Skeldic Concord¶

Cairnmeet¶

Cairnmeet is not a capital, because the Skeldic Concord has no single capital.

It is one of the most common Oathmoot grounds: a high, flood-safe settlement near the Cairnback Range where oath-speakers, shield-companies, caravan-houses, and hill clans gather when the Concord needs one voice.

Varkhuna Stilt¶

Varkhuna Stilt is a lake settlement raised above the dangerous eastern waters of Lake Varkhuna.

Fishing is profitable here, but never safe. Houses stand high on timber legs, boats are tied with quick-cut ropes, and every child learns which ripples mean wind and which mean something below.

Frostgale Hold¶

Frostgale Hold sits below the storm-heavy southern mountains.

It is a defensive settlement of exiles, mountain families, goat herders, smiths, and fighters watching the passes toward Dornhal.

Reedshield Camp¶

Reedshield Camp is a moving floodland settlement of wagons, raised tents, livestock pens, and oath-marked banners.

It shifts with water, grazing, and danger. A guest is fed, questioned, and protected while under roof-oath, even if that roof is only canvas for the night.
