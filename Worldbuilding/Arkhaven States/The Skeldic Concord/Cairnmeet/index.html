Cairnmeet¶

Cairnmeet is not a capital, because the Skeldic Concord has no single capital.

It is one of the most common Oathmoot grounds: a high, flood-safe settlement near the Cairnback Range where oath-speakers, shield-companies, caravan-houses, and hill clans gather when the Concord needs one voice.
