Akari Tava¶

Akari Tava is a major council settlement of the Zul’Akari Clans, hidden among jungle roots, reed channels, and lake-fed marshland.

Elders, shamans, oracles, and clan speakers gather here when outside threats force the clans to speak with one voice. Outsiders rarely see more than the landing platforms, painted stones, and watching eyes beyond the trees.
