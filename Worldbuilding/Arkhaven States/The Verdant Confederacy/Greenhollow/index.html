Greenhollow¶

Greenhollow is a large market town of mixed human and elven governance, surrounded by pasture, orchards, boundary groves, and low stone roads.

It is typical of the Verdant Confederacy at its best: practical human expansion checked by elven land stewardship. Its assembly hall has two doors by tradition, one for town delegates and one for grove speakers.
