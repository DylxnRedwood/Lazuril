Elmsbridge¶

Elmsbridge stands on a broad river crossing used by farmers, merchants, militia columns, and elven wardens.

The bridge itself is half stone and half living wood, maintained by dwarven masons, human toll clerks, and elven root-mages who all argue about repairs in different languages.
