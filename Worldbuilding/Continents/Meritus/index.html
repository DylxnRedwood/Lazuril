Meritus¶

Meritus is the eastern continent from which the first Humans and Aasimar came to Arkhaven aboard the Ark.

In the oldest human records, the continent is sometimes called Meridian. The name reflects the belief that Meritus was the centre of the known world, the fixed point from which all lands, seas, kingdoms, roads, and peoples were measured.

That belief did not end when the Arkborne reached Arkhaven. Many early human settlers continued to treat Meritus as the true heart of civilisation and Arkhaven as a promised refuge, frontier, or inheritance.

The Angel’s Command¶

The departure from Meritus began with a vision given to a human oracle. The event is remembered in human religious history as The Angel’s Command.

According to Arkborne tradition, a celestial messenger appeared to the oracle and warned that Meritus would face a disaster no army could defeat and no crown could command away. The oracle was instructed to build a single Ark, gather those who could preserve human civilisation, and sail west across the ocean toward a scarred land beyond the sea.

The command was specific enough to be obeyed, but vague enough to be disputed for centuries. It did not clearly define who deserved to be saved, whether Meritus would fall completely, or whether those left behind were doomed.

The Chosen Families¶

The Aasimar noble houses later claimed that their celestial blood proved they were meant to interpret and fulfil the Angel’s Command.

They took leading roles in funding, organising, guarding, and blessing the Ark. Priests, officers, shipmasters, scholars, craftspeople, soldiers, nobles, and selected families were gathered for the crossing as part of the Ark’s 1,000 passengers.

After the landing in Arkhaven, many of the first land grants went to families who had been aboard the Ark, especially those who could claim descent from Ark captains, oracle-guardians, priests, officers, and Aasimar patrons.

To their supporters, these families preserved human civilisation. To their critics, they used a divine warning to save themselves first.

The Unknown East¶

The current state of Meritus is unknown.

No confirmed voyage has returned with reliable news. Some believe the continent was destroyed. Some believe it survived but changed beyond recognition. Others claim the foretold disaster never came, and that the Arkborne abandoned their homeland because frightened nobles mistook prophecy for certainty.

Churches in Arkhaven preserve fragments of Meritian scripture. Noble houses keep genealogies, seals, coins, maps, ship records, and old titles from the eastern homeland.

Sailors still tell stories of lights on the eastern horizon, impossible storms, dead ships, and voices speaking old human dialects over empty water. None of these stories have been confirmed.

Meritus remains central to human origin, Aasimar legitimacy, and the contested meaning of the Arkfall.
