The Ashen Vow¶

Description¶

The Ashen Vow is a militant holy order made up of paladins, priests, exorcists, temple guards, battlefield confessors, monster hunters, and soldiers who have seen what faith can become when it is used badly.

They were shaped by the memory of the War of the False Saints. To them, false miracles are not harmless lies. They are the first step toward mobs, purges, broken kingdoms, and another continent-wide disaster.

The Ashen Vow are not part of any single church or state, though many temples tolerate them and many rulers use them when it suits. They are called when cults gather, saints are declared too quickly, relics start speaking, the dead do not stay buried, or a village begins worshipping something it found beneath the ground.

They often cross paths with the Scriptor Compact, but they are not the same kind of people. Scriptors preserve doctrine and investigate records. The Ashen Vow are the ones sent when a door needs breaking.

Purpose¶

The Ashen Vow exists to stop faith being turned into a weapon again.

They investigate false saints, miracle cults, undead outbreaks, fiendish influence, cursed relics, forbidden shrines, and religious movements that begin gathering swords before answers.

At their best, they protect ordinary people from monsters wearing holy language.

At their worst, they start seeing monsters everywhere.

Rules¶

No miracle is above examination.

No prophet is above law.

No temple may raise a private army without scrutiny.

Do not destroy evidence unless leaving it intact would cause immediate harm.

Accept surrender from the deceived. Show no mercy to those who knowingly manufacture miracles, saints, or relics to control the desperate.

The Vow does not exist to punish doubt. It exists to punish corruption.

Personality¶

The Ashen Vow are severe, brave, suspicious, and difficult to relax around.

They are respected by common folk who have seen cults take root, feared by priests with secrets, and watched carefully by rulers who do not like armed holy orders getting ideas.

A good member of the Vow is disciplined, patient, and painfully honest.

A bad one is only one bad vision away from a purge.

That tension is what makes them useful and dangerous in equal measure.
