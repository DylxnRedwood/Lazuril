Black Keel Brotherhood¶

Description¶

The Black Keel Brotherhood is a loose maritime network of captains, smugglers, salvage crews, wreckers, privateers, dockside brokers, river pilots, and sailors who understand that law becomes flexible once land is out of sight.

They are not quite pirates, not quite merchants, and not quite a navy. That is why they survive.

On the surface, they move cargo, crew ships, recover wrecks, guide vessels through dangerous waters, and offer passage when legal routes are closed. In truth, they also smuggle goods, hide fugitives, broker ransoms, sell information, and move things that no official harbour wants recorded.

They are strongest in Republic of Havenor, around coastal trade routes, island coves, lake settlements, river mouths, and the waterways of The Godscar.

Purpose¶

The Black Keel Brotherhood exists to keep people and cargo moving by water when law, war, tolls, blockades, or politics get in the way.

They are useful to merchants, criminals, refugees, spies, nobles, pirates, and desperate towns that need food more than they need clean paperwork.

A Brotherhood ship may be the safest passage available.

It may also be the reason the harbour master has stopped asking questions.

Rules¶

A captain’s word given over water matters.

A marked Brotherhood vessel is not to be raided by another Brotherhood crew.

Spoils are divided by crew law, not noble title.

Shipwrecked crews are ransomed, recruited, or released unless they have earned worse.

Do not sell passengers to slavers, cults, or experimental mages.

Never ask too much about the cargo unless the answer will raise the price.

Personality¶

The Black Keel Brotherhood is rough, funny, dangerous, superstitious, and loyal in its own way.

Its members drink with pirates, sell news to admirals, lie to customs officers, and still insist they are honest sailors.

They respect nerve, competence, and anyone who pays on time.

They do not respect people who think a seal, crown, or temple stamp means the sea will obey them.
