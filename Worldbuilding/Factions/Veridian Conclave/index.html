Veridian Conclave¶

Description¶

The Veridian Conclave is a loose order of druids, rangers, wardens, shamans, herbalists, beast-speakers, ruin-keepers, and land-priests who protect the living places of Arkhaven.

They are not against civilisation. That would be too simple. They accept farms, roads, towns, trade, hunting, timber, and settlement when it is done with sense.

What they oppose is stupidity. Poisoned rivers, sacred ruins treated as treasure houses, forests cut until the soil dies, mines dug into cursed seams, marshes drained without understanding them, and nobles who mistake ownership for permission.

They are strongest in the Veridian March, the Veylrain Expanse, the Silent Weald, the Floodlands, the Kharad Wildlands, and around older elven territories where the land still remembers things people do not.

Purpose¶

The Veridian Conclave exists to stop civilisation and wilderness destroying each other.

They watch for blight, poisoned waters, unnatural silence, dangerous growth, altered beasts, cursed ruins, and places where old magic has begun moving again.

They do not protect nature because it is gentle. They protect it because when it breaks, people usually break with it.

Rules¶

The land is to be listened to before it is used.

Sacred ruins are not abandoned property.

Water, soil, forest, herd, and harvest matter more than the pride of a lord.

A monster is not always an enemy. Sometimes it is a warning.

Do not destroy what you do not understand unless it is already killing people.

A Conclave warden serves the balance of a place before the politics of a border.

Personality¶

The Veridian Conclave is practical, suspicious, weather-beaten, and blunt.

Its members are often calm in ways that annoy everyone else. They will let a council argue for hours and then say the river has already answered.

They are patient with farmers, children, animals, and old roads.

They are less patient with treasure hunters, careless mages, and rulers who think a map is the same thing as knowing a place.
