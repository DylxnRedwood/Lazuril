Foundry Quarter: NPCs & Locations¶

Player-facing reference for NPCs met and locations visited in the Foundry Quarter of Caer Oryn. Updated after Session 4 - The Hart of the Matter.

NPCs Met¶

NPC First Encountered What the Party Knows
Foundry Quarter Gate Guards Quarter entrance Two guards refused to accept Teclis' Inquisitor-Scriptor authority without the correct papers from Arch-Lector Voss. Teclis distracted them with an illusion involving one guard's wife and the other's supposed betrayal, allowing the party to slip into the restricted quarter.
Local Foundry Worker 17 Magpie Lane Confirmed that number 17 had once been a shift manager's office but had stood unused for months.
Halwin Magpie Lane The blacksmith working a few doors from number 17. He saw a man whom the party believe may have been Edrin Vaal enter the derelict building, followed shortly afterwards by several cloaked figures. He heard a scuffle and said anyone leaving without passing his forge must have used the sewer drain or the scaffolding at the closed end of the lane.
Garron Duskholt Rooftops above Magpie Lane A human agent of the Obsidian Register who shot Cassian and was captured after a rooftop chase. He carried the crying-eye brand on the back of his neck. Under interrogation, he revealed the Gilded Hart room 13 drop, Odran Kesh's name and the use of the Magpie Lane drain as a transport route. He was killed in the basement beneath number 17.

Mentioned, Not Met¶

NPC Connection
Odran Kesh Named by Garron Duskholt as his superior within the Obsidian Register. Garron said Kesh could be found at the Counting House in the Foundry Quarter.


Locations Visited¶

Location What Happened There
Foundry Quarter Entrance The quarter was under tighter control during preparations for Cadris' Day. The party were refused entry without formal papers and slipped through while the guards were distracted by Teclis' illusion.
Magpie Lane A smoke-stained working lane containing number 17, Halwin's nearby blacksmith's forge and a closed cul-de-sac at its far end. The party followed Edrin Vaal's courier route here, questioned local workers and discovered possible escape routes through a large sewer drain and rooftop scaffolding.
17 Magpie Lane A derelict former shift manager's office. The front room was dusty, while the rear showed signs of a struggle, including broken furniture and a concealed doorway disguised by an illusion of brick mortar. The party later brought Garron Duskholt back here for interrogation.
Hidden Basement beneath 17 Magpie Lane Contained barrels of old vegetables, a trapped gilded chest, Veylrain cloth and a courier tag linking Edrin Vaal to a small green lizard figure. Blood seeped between the floorboards and dragged marks suggested an injured person had been moved through the room. Garron Duskholt was interrogated and killed here.
Scaffolding and Rooftops The party investigated the lane's upper escape route after Cassian was struck by a crossbow bolt. Nakoa pursued the attacker across the rooftops, aided by Teclis and Alekthar, until Garron fell into a barrel below and was captured.

Known Open Threads¶

  • Odran Kesh was said to be based at the Counting House and has not yet been confronted.
  • Whether the man Halwin saw entering 17 Magpie Lane was Edrin Vaal remains unconfirmed.
  • Edrin Vaal was taken through the Magpie Lane drain, but the destination beyond the drain remains unknown.
  • Blood and drag marks beneath number 17 suggest another injured captive or victim was moved through the basement.
  • The small green lizard figure was not recovered at Magpie Lane and was also gone when room 13 of the Gilded Hart was later searched.
  • The Obsidian Register's wider structure, purpose and operations within the Foundry Quarter remain unresolved.
