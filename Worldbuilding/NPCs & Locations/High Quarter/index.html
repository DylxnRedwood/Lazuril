High Quarter: NPCs & Locations¶

Player-facing reference for NPCs met and locations visited in the High Quarter of Caer Oryn. Updated after Session 4 - The Hart of the Matter.

NPCs Met¶

NPC First Encountered What the Party Knows
Marcelline Orras The Gilded Hart The immaculate proprietor of the Gilded Hart, maintaining a watchful presence over the hotel's staff and wealthy guests.
Selda Morn The Gilded Hart reception The receptionist who confirmed that room 13 was registered to Derren Kerr and had been kept almost permanently available even when its occupant was absent.
Gilded Hart Waiter Gilded Hart dining room A reluctant but helpful member of staff who told the party about Resha, a surviving maid from House Vaelisar who could often be found at Starflux.
Vaelisar Gate Guards House Vaelisar Two pike-carrying men stationed outside the sealed manor. They gave Alekthar little information but named Roderick Valeborne as the person overseeing the property. An experienced veteran later suggested that the men behind the gate did not move like trained soldiers.
Starflux Proprietor Starflux The owner and principal barista of the coffee shop. Teclis disturbed her after closing with a loud magical music box. She confirmed that Resha was due to begin work there the following morning.

Mentioned, Not Met¶

NPC Connection
Resha A former maid of House Vaelisar and apparent survivor of the disaster within the manor. She suffered a severe head injury, lost her position and was due to begin work at Starflux.
Roderick Valeborne Named as the official overseeing the sealing of House Vaelisar. The party have not yet approached him directly.
Derren Kerr The name used to maintain room 13 at the Gilded Hart. The registration form listed him as a travelling metals broker from Karthrest, but the name had been written over earlier text and is believed to be false.


Locations Visited¶

Location What Happened There
Hartglass Row A polished High Quarter street lined with expensive establishments, brass lamps and clipped trees. The Gilded Hart, Starflux and House Vaelisar all stand along or near this route. The party observed hurried Civic Directorate movement towards the manor from here.
The Gilded Hart The party booked rooms 12 and 14 on either side of room 13, ate in the hotel's dining room and used the balcony as a vantage point over Hartglass Row and House Vaelisar. Staff provided information about Resha, the manor and Derren Kerr.
Room 13, The Gilded Hart Nakoa entered the apparently empty room and found disturbed floorboards, fine green dust, Veylrain wrappings and a note reading Hart / 13 / 3rd bell. A personal information form identified the occupant as Derren Kerr, though the name appeared altered. The lizard relic was no longer present.
Starflux A coffee shop on the corner of Hartglass Row. Teclis visited after closing and spoke with its proprietor through an upstairs sash window. He learned that Resha was due to begin her first shift the following morning.
House Vaelisar Alekthar first scouted the sealed and badly damaged manor before the party later climbed the outer wall and entered through the rear. Inside, they followed a low humming sound to the ruined central hall, where a fractured black orb hung suspended between the floors with dim red energy moving beneath its surface.

Known Open Threads¶

  • Derren Kerr is almost certainly a false identity, and the name originally written on his registration form remains unknown.
  • Room 13 appears to be a permanent meeting point, hiding place or drop location rather than ordinary accommodation.
  • The meaning and timing of Hart / 13 / 3rd bell remain unresolved.
  • The fine green dust found beneath the floorboards may be another form of veinstone but has not been confirmed.
  • Resha survived the events inside House Vaelisar and has not yet been questioned.
  • Roderick Valeborne is overseeing the manor's sealing but has not yet been contacted.
  • The supposed guards around House Vaelisar may not be genuine soldiers.
  • The origin, purpose and danger of the black orb inside House Vaelisar remain unknown.
  • The fate of the Vaelisar family and most of the household remains unconfirmed.
