Lazuril World Information

The calendar systems of Lazuril vary between cultures, kingdoms, churches, and historical traditions.

Most modern systems used in Arkhaven now measure time using the beginning of The Green Silence as the primary historical reference point.

Under this system, the first recorded year of the Green Silence is recognised as 1 GS.

The current historical period is generally understood to be slightly more than twelve centuries after the beginning of the event.

Overview¶

Most major calendars are based upon:

  • Solar cycles
  • Lunar observation
  • Seasonal change
  • Astronomical events
  • Religious observance

Although regional systems still exist, the GS dating system is widely used by:

  • Churches
  • Archive Houses
  • Scriptors
  • Trade institutions
  • Historians
  • Major governments

Seasons¶

Most regions recognise four major seasons.

Seasonal intensity varies significantly depending on geography and climate.

Northern rainforests, desert regions, mountain territories, and glacial elevations often experience seasonal conditions very differently from the central grasslands.

Religious Observances¶

Many churches maintain holy days, fasts, pilgrimage periods, and commemorative festivals tied to:

  • Solstices
  • Equinoxes
  • Historical events
  • Saints
  • Celestial alignments
  • Agricultural cycles

Religious calendars do not always align between institutions.

Astronomy and Navigation¶

Astronomical observation remains important for:

  • Navigation
  • Agriculture
  • Religious ritual
  • Maritime travel
  • Seasonal prediction
  • Historical record keeping

Elven observatories and maritime powers maintain some of the most accurate astronomical records.

Historical Usage¶

The adoption of the GS system became increasingly widespread after the Age of False Saints and the expansion of institutional record keeping under the Scriptor Compact.

Many historians considered the Green Silence a more stable historical reference point than earlier disputed events associated with the Godscar.

Older records may therefore use different chronological systems.

Current Status¶

Although no single calendar system dominates all of Lazuril, the GS dating system is now the closest equivalent to a continental standard throughout much of Arkhaven.

Regional variations remain common, especially in isolated territories and older religious traditions.
