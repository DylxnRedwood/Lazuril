## Astaavi

_Private NPC file for Alekthar's campaign continuity._

Astaavi Amakiir is the youngest son of [Akaavi](./Akaavi.md) and [Miriam](./Miriam.md), and one of the seven survivors of the Night of Golden Tears. He did not carry the Bound Radiance.

Astaavi survived while his parents and siblings [Aldric](./Aldric.md), [Aliana](./Aliana.md), [Alysse](./Alysse.md), and [Aleron](./Aleron.md) died. He is the last surviving child of Akaavi and Miriam's branch.

## Aftermath

After the massacre, Astaavi was raised alongside [Amalina](./Amalina.md) and [Avaris](./Avaris.md) under the care of [Ahkotan](./Ahkotan.md) and [Catrina](./Catrina.md). He grew up inside the diminished version of House Amakiir, shaped by absence, grief, and the knowledge that his father's pride is remembered as one of the reasons the house fell.

## Relationship to Alekthar

Astaavi and Alekthar share survivor status, but not the same burden. Alekthar became heir and left to seek the truth. Astaavi remained as one of the living remnants of a ruined branch. He may admire Alekthar, resent him, depend on him, or feel abandoned by him.

## DM Use

Astaavi can represent the consequences Alekthar left behind at Cael Tharos. He may become a future claimant, a bitter survivor, a loyal cousin, or someone tempted by anyone promising restoration of House Amakiir's lost greatness.
