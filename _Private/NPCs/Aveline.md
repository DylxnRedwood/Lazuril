## Aveline

_Private NPC file for Alekthar's campaign continuity._

Aveline Amakiir was an Aasimar daughter of [Araenia](./Araenia.md) and [Hadvar](./Hadvar.md), sister to [Aaldar](./Aaldar.md) and [Avaris](./Avaris.md). She carried the Bound Radiance and died during the Night of Golden Tears.

Aveline was one of the ten bearers targeted by the cult at Cael Tharos. Her death, alongside Aaldar's, left Avaris as the surviving child of Araenia and Hadvar.

## Bound Radiance

Aveline's blood was opened into the ritual circle and her inner light was drawn toward the runecarved bowl. She died hollowed by celestial fire when the gathered Radiance became unstable through Amari.

## Relationship to Alekthar

Aveline can be used as another echo of the family's divided childhood. She may have been close to Amari through shared preparation, making her death emotionally linked to the failed Continuance rather than merely to the wider massacre.

## DM Use

Aveline's records can help reveal how young female bearers were prepared, and whether Amari's preparation differed from the others. If there is a clue that Amari was unusually powerful or unstable, Aveline's comparison point may matter.
