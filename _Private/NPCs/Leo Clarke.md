## Leo Clarke

_Private NPC working page for Leo Clarke, created as part of Cassian Clarke's private PC development._

Leo Clarke is the son of [Cassian Clarke](../PCs/Cassian%20Clarke.md) and [Rayna Clarke](Rayna%20Clarke.md), and the older brother of [Lyra Clarke](Lyra%20Clarke.md). At the beginning of the campaign he is 9 years old.

Leo is currently staying in [Seabarrow](../../docs/Worldbuilding/Arkhaven%20States/Windmere%20Assembly/Seabarrow.md), in the [Windmere Assembly](../../docs/Worldbuilding/Arkhaven%20States/Windmere%20Assembly.md), with Rayna and Cassian's parents, [Percy Clarke](Percy%20Clarke.md) and [Meri Clarke](Meri%20Clarke.md), while Cassian travels across Arkhaven on pilgrimage.

## Role in Cassian's Backstory

Leo represents one of Cassian's most important personal stakes. Cassian's decision to leave his family was not made lightly, and Leo is old enough to understand absence, danger, and the idea that his father has gone away because something is wrong in the wider world.

He should not be treated as a plot device only. Leo is part of what Cassian is trying to protect: ordinary family life, safety, continuity, and the possibility that children might grow up under a sky that still guides rather than threatens.

## Personality Direction

Leo is still intentionally open for future development. As a starting point, he may be curious, watchful, and old enough to imitate his father's seriousness without fully understanding it. He may admire Cassian's duty, resent his absence, or move between both feelings depending on the scene.

Given both Cassian and Rayna's interests, Leo may have some familiarity with constellations, simple star names, sailor's sky-lore, or the idea that the sun's accretion disk is something his parents used to study.

## DM Use

Leo can be used to show the emotional consequence of Cassian's pilgrimage. A letter from Leo, a child's drawing of strange stars, or a report that he has started having odd dreams under moonlight could all connect the family thread to the larger celestial mystery without immediately making him a target.

If the old god's influence begins to manifest through dreams, children may notice patterns adults dismiss. Leo should be handled carefully. He is a stake, not a disposable shock tool.
