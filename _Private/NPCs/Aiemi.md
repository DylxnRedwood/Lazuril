## Aiemi

_Private NPC file for Alekthar's campaign continuity._

Aiemi was Alekthar Amakiir's Aasimar grandmother, the wife of [Oberyn](./Oberyn.md), and one of the senior bearers of the Bound Radiance within House Amakiir. She died during the Night of Golden Tears.

Among the family, Aiemi carried the old burden with solemn tenderness. Visiting priests held their tongues around her. Old retainers showed her reverence. She took part in the private instruction of [Amari](./Amari.md) before the Continuance.

At Amari's celebration, Aiemi was seen dabbing away a golden tear before smiling at Oberyn. She understood the Continuance as blessing, burden, inheritance, and risk.

## Bound Radiance

Aiemi carried the Bound Radiance and was one of the ten targeted by [The Horned Tiefling](./The%20Horned%20Tiefling.md). During the ritual, her blood was drawn into the circle and her inner light was extracted. She died hollowed by celestial fire after Amari drew the gathered Radiance back into herself.

## Relationship to Alekthar

Alekthar saw how others reacted to Aiemi, but he was not allowed to know why. Her secrecy contributes to his unresolved anger toward the dead. She loved the family and guarded its burden, but her generation also helped maintain the ignorance that left Alekthar unable to understand the catastrophe that followed.

## DM Use

Aiemi can appear through memory, old prayer books, family portraits, marginal notes, hidden instructional texts, or surviving retainers who understood that she was more than an honoured grandmother. If any ancestral echo of House Amakiir remains, Aiemi is a strong candidate for a voice that knows more than it can safely say.
