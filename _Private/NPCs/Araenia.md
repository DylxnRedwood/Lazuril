## Araenia

_Private NPC file for Alekthar's campaign continuity._

Araenia Amakiir was Arnemia's sister, wife to [Hadvar](./Hadvar.md), mother to [Aaldar](./Aaldar.md), [Aveline](./Aveline.md), and [Avaris](./Avaris.md), and a bearer of the Bound Radiance. She died during the Night of Golden Tears.

At Amari's Continuance celebration, Alekthar noticed Araenia studying Amari with an expression he could not read. That detail should matter. Araenia may have recognised something unusual about Amari's state before the ritual began, or she may have feared that the Continuance was being delayed too long.

## Bound Radiance

Araenia carried the Bound Radiance and was one of the ten targeted in the ritual. Her children included two known bearers: Aaldar and Aveline. Her youngest daughter, Avaris, survived and did not carry the marked inheritance.

## Relationship to Alekthar

Araenia is part of the pattern Alekthar resents: adults who understood enough to be afraid but not enough, or not free enough, to tell him the truth. Her unreadable attention on Amari can become a memory Alekthar returns to when trying to reconstruct what the family knew.

## DM Use

Araenia can be used through private letters, maternal notes, or warnings addressed to Avaris. She may have suspected that Amari's Continuance would behave differently. She may have left traces because she doubted the family's rules more than Arnemia did.
