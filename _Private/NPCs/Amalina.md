## Amalina

_Private NPC file for Alekthar's campaign continuity._

Amalina Amakiir is the daughter of [Ahkotan](./Ahkotan.md) and [Catrina](./Catrina.md), sister of [Aldoran](./Aldoran.md), and one of the seven survivors of the Night of Golden Tears. She did not carry the Bound Radiance.

Amalina survived the massacre and remained at Cael Tharos during the years of managed decline. She was raised alongside [Astaavi](./Astaavi.md) and [Avaris](./Avaris.md) under the care of her parents.

## Relationship to Alekthar

Amalina is one of Alekthar's living family ties to the house he left behind. She is not just a name in a survivor count. She is someone whose adult life is being formed inside the ruins of choices made before she was old enough to understand them.

## DM Use

Amalina can be used as a living reminder that House Amakiir still exists. She may write to Alekthar, preserve estate traditions, fear the return of the cult, or begin asking her own questions about why some relatives carried the Radiance and others did not.
