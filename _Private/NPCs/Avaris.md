## Avaris

_Private NPC file for Alekthar's campaign continuity._

Avaris Amakiir is the youngest daughter of [Araenia](./Araenia.md) and [Hadvar](./Hadvar.md), sister to [Aaldar](./Aaldar.md) and [Aveline](./Aveline.md), and one of the seven survivors of the Night of Golden Tears. She did not carry the Bound Radiance.

Avaris survived while her parents and two siblings died. She remained at Cael Tharos and was raised with [Amalina](./Amalina.md) and [Astaavi](./Astaavi.md) under the care of [Ahkotan](./Ahkotan.md) and [Catrina](./Catrina.md).

## Relationship to Alekthar

Avaris mirrors Alekthar in important ways. She was an Aasimar child of the house who survived without carrying the marked Radiance. She may understand Alekthar's exclusion better than most, especially if she too felt the divide between bearers and non-bearers.

## DM Use

Avaris can become a powerful emotional pressure point. She may be the survivor most likely to ask Alekthar what right he had to leave, or the one most likely to help him because she also wants the truth. She may also hold a childhood memory of Araenia or Aveline that Alekthar has overlooked.
