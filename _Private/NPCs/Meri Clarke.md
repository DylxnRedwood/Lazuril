## Meri Clarke

_Private NPC working page for Meri Clarke, created as part of Cassian Clarke's private PC development._

Meri Clarke is the mother of [Cassian Clarke](../PCs/Cassian%20Clarke.md), wife of [Percy Clarke](Percy%20Clarke.md), and grandmother of [Leo Clarke](Leo%20Clarke.md) and [Lyra Clarke](Lyra%20Clarke.md). At the beginning of the campaign she is 75 years old and lives in [Seabarrow](../../docs/Worldbuilding/Arkhaven%20States/Windmere%20Assembly/Seabarrow.md), in the [Windmere Assembly](../../docs/Worldbuilding/Arkhaven%20States/Windmere%20Assembly.md).

Meri is a herbalist and a devoted worshipper of Selune. In Seabarrow she is known less as a formal priestess and more as a practical religious presence: someone who helps, heals, listens, prepares remedies, and remains involved in the needs of the community.

## Role in Cassian's Backstory

Meri gave Cassian his first understanding that faith is not only spoken in prayer. It is practised in care. From her, Cassian learned basic first aid, patience with the frightened and injured, and the belief that the gods are honoured through service as much as ceremony.

Where Percy taught Cassian to read the sky, Meri taught him to read people: pain, fear, fever, grief, pride, and the quiet signs that someone is pretending to be stronger than they are.

## Personality

Meri should feel kind but not soft. She has spent too many years treating wounds, sickness, and desperate people to be naive. Her compassion is practical, disciplined, and sometimes blunt. She is likely to worry about Cassian's pilgrimage, but she would not try to shame him out of doing what he believes is necessary.

She may be the family member most able to see when Cassian is carrying more than he admits.

## Current Position

Meri remains in Seabarrow with Percy. After Cassian began his pilgrimage, [Rayna Clarke](Rayna%20Clarke.md), Leo, and Lyra returned to live with Cassian's parents for safety.

Meri therefore acts as one of the guardians of Cassian's family while he is away. She is also a useful point of contact for rumours about illness, strange dreams, moonlit rites, or community unease in Seabarrow.

## DM Use

Meri can be used to show the human and domestic effects of the wider celestial disturbance. If the accretion disk weakens and the quality of sunlight or moonlight changes, Meri may notice symptoms before scholars name causes: sleep disruption, unusual fevers, plants behaving strangely, remedies losing potency, children drawing wrong stars, or patients speaking of dreams they cannot explain.

She should also remain a moral mirror for Cassian. When his story becomes cosmic, Meri can remind him what protection actually means at ground level.
