## Percy Clarke

_Private NPC working page for Percy Clarke, created as part of Cassian Clarke's private PC development._

Percy Clarke is the father of [Cassian Clarke](../PCs/Cassian%20Clarke.md), husband of [Meri Clarke](Meri%20Clarke.md), and grandfather of [Leo Clarke](Leo%20Clarke.md) and [Lyra Clarke](Lyra%20Clarke.md). At the beginning of the campaign he is 79 years old and lives in [Seabarrow](../../docs/Worldbuilding/Arkhaven%20States/Windmere%20Assembly/Seabarrow.md), in the [Windmere Assembly](../../docs/Worldbuilding/Arkhaven%20States/Windmere%20Assembly.md).

Percy is an old sailor, shaped by the coast, the tides, hard weather, and the long habit of trusting the stars when land has vanished from sight. He worships Selune with the sincerity of a man who has survived darkness, storms, and distance because the heavens gave him bearings when nothing else could.

## Role in Cassian's Backstory

Percy gave Cassian his first practical relationship with the night sky. To him, stars were not abstract theology or scholarly objects. They were tools of survival, signs of season and route, and part of the inherited discipline of those who live by the sea.

Through Percy, Cassian learned that celestial knowledge is not separate from ordinary life. A constellation can be a prayer, a map, a warning, and a promise of home all at once. This remains one of the foundations of Cassian's worldview.

## Personality

Percy should feel steady, weathered, warm, and quietly stubborn. He is not likely to speak in grand religious abstractions unless pressed, but he understands faith through lived experience. He has seen enough of the sea to respect danger and enough of the sky to believe that guidance matters.

He is likely proud of Cassian, though concerned by the danger of his pilgrimage. He may not fully understand the theological or astronomical stakes, but he understands why Cassian feels compelled to go.

## Current Position

Percy remains in Seabarrow with Meri. After Cassian left on pilgrimage, [Rayna Clarke](Rayna%20Clarke.md), Leo, and Lyra returned to live under Percy and Meri's protection in the Windmere Basin.

This makes Percy one of Cassian's strongest emotional anchors. Any threat to Seabarrow or Cassian's family will immediately draw Percy into the story, even if he is not physically present.

## DM Use

Percy can be used to ground Cassian's cosmic arc in human cost. He represents the old, practical relationship between mortals and the heavens: sailors looked up because they needed to live, not because they wanted to unravel divine machinery.

If the night sky becomes unreliable, Percy is one of the first ordinary people who would understand the practical danger. Wrong stars mean wrong routes. Wrong routes mean wrecks, hunger, lost ships, and families left waiting on the shore.
