## Alysse

_Private NPC file for Alekthar's campaign continuity._

Alysse Amakiir was an Aasimar daughter of [Akaavi](./Akaavi.md) and [Miriam](./Miriam.md). She died during the Night of Golden Tears.

Unlike Aldric, Aliana, and Aleron, Alysse is not recorded as a bearer of the Bound Radiance. This made her more like Alekthar within Akaavi's branch: Aasimar by blood, part of the family, but not counted among those entrusted with the hidden inheritance.

## Relationship to Alekthar

Alysse can mirror Alekthar's excluded position. Her death shows that being spared the secret did not mean being spared the consequences. She may have shared Alekthar's frustration, curiosity, or quiet resentment toward the private lessons given to other relatives.

## DM Use

Alysse is useful if Alekthar ever searches for evidence that other non-bearers questioned the family's rules. A diary, letter, or childhood note from Alysse could reveal how the excluded children interpreted the secrecy around them.
