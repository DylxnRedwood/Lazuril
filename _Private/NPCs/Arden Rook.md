## Arden Rook

_Private NPC working page for Arden Rook. This page supports [Nakoa](../PCs/Nakoa.md), The Veiled Path, and the hidden ruin-history of Ithrava._

Arden Rook is known publicly as a travelling scholar, map-reader, relic investigator, and difficult man to surprise. He has a tired face, dry humour, and a habit of noticing exits before introductions. He appears to be the kind of scholar who has spent too long in unsafe places and has learned to travel light, speak carefully, and trust locks only when he placed them himself.

That public identity is only partly true.

Arden was once an operative of **The Veiled Path**, the hidden network widely known across Arkhaven as a thieves' guild. That reputation is too simple. The Veiled Path moves stolen goods, relics, letters, prisoners, poisons, names, maps, forbidden books, forged permits, and secrets through safehouses, coded routes, criminal brokers, dockside runners, and false identities. Most people think they are thieves. Most thieves hope that is all they are.

Arden was respected inside that world before abandoning it years ago. The break was not clean. He left after uncovering something in the ancient ruins of Ithrava, something he has never fully explained. Whatever he found made him quieter, more cautious, and far less amused by treasure hunters, amateur relic seekers, and clever young fools who treat old places as puzzles built for their entertainment.

## Relationship to Nakoa

Arden first met [Nakoa](../PCs/Nakoa.md) near Tethvale, after arriving with locked cases, sealed parcels, map rolls, and enough careful silence to attract every rumour in the settlement. Nakoa attempted to sneak into his temporary dwelling and was caught almost immediately.

Rather than punish him, Arden recognised familiar traits: boldness, intelligence, restlessness, and the fatal inability to leave a mystery alone. He took an interest in Nakoa because the boy reminded him of the sort of person who either becomes useful, becomes dangerous, or dies believing those are the same thing.

Arden taught Nakoa how to move unseen, open locks without force, read people as carefully as maps, listen for what was not being said, and survive when cleverness had already failed. More importantly, he tried to teach Nakoa when not to reach.

This is Arden's central frustration. He gave Nakoa tools, but he also gave him warnings. Nakoa learned both. Only one set reliably shaped his behaviour.

## Personality

Arden should be played as controlled, dry, wary, and difficult to impress. He does not posture like a villain or boast like a rogue. He has survived too much by being underestimated to waste effort proving what he can do.

He is not heartless. His mentorship of Nakoa proves that. But his affection expresses itself as correction, preparation, and blunt warnings rather than warmth. He is capable of kindness, but he distrusts sentiment when danger is nearby.

He dislikes amateurs in dangerous places. He dislikes people who touch relics before asking what touched them first. He dislikes locked doors that are too easy to open. He dislikes ruins that seem abandoned but are not dusty enough. He dislikes The Veiled Path being discussed by people who know only the tavern version.

## Skills and Use in Play

Arden can serve as an informant, mentor, reluctant protector, old contact, unreliable source, or warning sign. If Arden is worried, the players should pay attention.

He understands coded routes, false identities, smuggling chains, dead drops, relic movement, forged permits, safehouses, black-market scholarship, criminal etiquette, and the political usefulness of secrets. He also has enough ruin experience to know that not every hidden chamber is meant to be entered and not every relic is inert because it fits in a hand.

Arden should not solve problems for Nakoa. His function is to complicate them. He may provide a route but refuse to explain why it is safer. He may recognise a symbol but warn against researching it in public. He may destroy evidence if he believes preservation would put the wrong eyes on it.

## Secrets and Future Development

Arden may know more about the black-and-gold feline relic than he has admitted.

Arden may have seen similar golden dust, living markings, or relic residue during the event that caused him to leave The Veiled Path.

Arden may still be hunted, watched, or quietly claimed by old Veiled Path contacts.

Arden may have come to the Veylrain Expanse specifically because he believed something beneath the rainforest was waking.

Arden may be protecting Nakoa from knowledge that would make him easier to find.
