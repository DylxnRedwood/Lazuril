## New Meritus

**New Meritus** is the capital city of the [Republic of Havenor](../Republic%20of%20Havenor.md), built around the protected harbour where the Ark made landfall and now rests at **Founding Quay**.

The city was named by the first Arkborne settlers in memory of [Meritus](../../Continents/Meritus.md), the eastern homeland they left behind after the Angel’s Command. To its defenders, the name honours survival, duty, and the preservation of human civilisation. To its critics, it reveals the ambition of the first settlers: they did not merely seek refuge in Arkhaven, they intended to plant a new version of the old world upon it.

New Meritus is the civic heart of Havenor. The **Mariner’s Senate** meets here, close to the Ark, the First Manifest, the Oath Deck, and the working docks that still define Havenori politics.

It is not a polished capital in the style of the Vermillion Crown. It is a busy harbour city of shipwrights, fishers, senators, clerics, guilds, old Ark families, ropewalks, timber yards, shrines, fish markets, taverns, public votes, and hard argument.

Founding Quay is both shrine and workplace. Pilgrims come to see the preserved Ark, senators swear oaths within sight of it, guilds trace their authority back to the first professions aboard it, and ordinary dockworkers move cargo beneath its shadow.

The city’s greatest tension is memory. New Meritus teaches every citizen that Havenor began with courage, divine warning, and impossible survival. Older peoples of Arkhaven often tell the same story differently, seeing the city as the first stone of human expansion, land claim, and unequal settlement.
