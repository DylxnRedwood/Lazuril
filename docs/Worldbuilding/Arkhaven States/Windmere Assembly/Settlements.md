# Settlements of the Windmere Assembly

## Aldmere

Aldmere is a wealthy assembly city set among broad river roads, civic halls, public gardens, guildhouses, and granaries.

It is one of the principal meeting places of the Grand Assembly of Windmere. Humans, dwarves, gnomes, halflings, and elves all maintain formal districts here, each proud of their own customs and equally convinced the others need reminding how government works.

## Millharth

Millharth is a rich farming town surrounded by orchards, grain fields, mills, and halfling-managed food markets.

Its public feasts are famous, but so are its land disputes. Good soil creates wealth, and wealth creates arguments over inheritance, leases, irrigation, and voting weight.

## Cogwell

Cogwell is a gnomish workshop town of clockwork halls, counting houses, alchemy shops, magical craft guilds, and experimental civic conveniences.

Its inventions are useful often enough that the Assembly tolerates the occasional smoke cloud.

## Seabarrow

Seabarrow is a fortified coastal town on the eastern cape, known for fisheries, shipyards, lighthouse towers, sea walls, and mixed-race harbour guilds.

It links Windmere’s inland abundance to wider sea trade.
