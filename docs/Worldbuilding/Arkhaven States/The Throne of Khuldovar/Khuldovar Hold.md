## Khuldovar Hold

Khuldovar Hold is the royal mountain seat of King Barund Khargrim Anvilthane, built deep within Khar Dûmvar.

Its halls contain royal courts, clan vaults, oath-stones, forge-shrines, and roads cut through the living mountain. It is old, formal, and heavy with ancestral law. Nothing important is agreed there until it has been spoken, witnessed, and carved.
