## Maerithal

Maerithal is one of the great royal cities of [The Martarië Crown](../The%20Martari%C3%AB%20Crown.md), associated with House Maereth, royal law, military command, and the formal memory of elven rulership.

White stone halls stand beneath ancient trees, and public spaces preserve roots, springs, and old stones rather than clearing them away. Decrees sealed in the name of King Aurelion Maereth are witnessed here by magistrates, archive-keepers, and noble retainers.
