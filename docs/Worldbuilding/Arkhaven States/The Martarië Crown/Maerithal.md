## Maerithal

Maerithal is one of the great cities of [The Martarië Crown](../The%20Martari%C3%AB%20Crown.md), most strongly associated with **House Sílorion**, a powerful elven house known for civic law, military patronage, archive discipline, and the formal memory of elven rulership.

White stone halls stand beneath ancient trees, and public spaces preserve roots, springs, and old stones rather than clearing them away. Royal decrees may still be witnessed here in the name of King Aurelion Maereth, but the city’s character, institutions, and old civic authority are shaped most visibly by House Sílorion.
