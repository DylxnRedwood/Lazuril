# Settlements of the Kharad Wildlands

## Kharuun Well

Kharuun Well is one of the best-known gathering places of the Kharad Wildlands, built around deep water, shade-stones, and old trade ground.

It is not a capital. The Wildlands do not have one. But caravan leaders, loxodon elders, goliath speakers, plains clans, and foreign merchants often meet here when disputes need witnesses and water rights need renewing.

## Marrow Ford

Marrow Ford sits on a mountain-fed river crossing used by herders, traders, and travelling clans.

Its banks are lined with hitching posts, hide tents, stone cairns, and seasonal markets. A promise made at the ford is expected to survive the dry season.

## Glasswind Camp

Glasswind Camp is a moving settlement of tents, wagons, animal pens, and windbreaks that shifts with grazing and weather.

It is known for animal handlers, scouts, centaur riders, and dryland guides who can cross bad country with little more than a skin of water and a memorised star-path.

## Redstone Rise

Redstone Rise is a rocky highland settlement used by dwarven prospectors, goliath families, and independent miners.

Its wealth is uncertain, but every rumour of metal brings new arguments over claim markers, clan rights, and who found the vein first.
