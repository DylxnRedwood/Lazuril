## Nareth Lo

Nareth Lo is a Veylrath settlement further downriver from [Tethvale](./Tethvale.md), hidden in wet rainforest where the **Saelun River** feeds into warmer channels near the **Lo-Varin Pools**.

The settlement is known for medicine, alchemy, fungi, rare jungle plants, antidotes, salves, poultices, and quiet healers who know which leaf saves a life and which one stops it.

It belongs to the [Veylrath Clans](../Veylrath%20Clans.md), but it draws visitors from many settlements because the jungle wounds everyone eventually.

## The Living Stores

Nareth Lo is built around living gardens rather than open streets.

Raised walkways pass between moss houses, drying racks, shaded pools, bark sheds, spore huts, and groves where plants are tied with coloured thread to mark their use and danger.

Nothing here is treated as merely useful. A medicine may also be poison. A fungus may feed a fever, numb a wound, stain a spirit, or mark a path that should not be followed twice.

## Medicine and Alchemy

The healers of Nareth Lo work with roots, flowers, bark, venom, mould, fruit, insects, river clay, and old Veylrath rites.

They make fever draughts, antivenoms, wound pastes, sleep-smoke, climbing salves, rot cures, fungal lanterns, scent masks, and medicines used by hunters and river travellers across clan lands.

Their alchemy is not clean or academic. It stains fingers, smells strongly, changes with rainfall, and depends on knowing when a plant was gathered, what grew beside it, and what was sung before it was cut.

## Clan Life

Nareth Lo is quiet, careful, and deeply respected.

Children are taught early not to touch bright caps, silver vines, sweet-smelling sap, or water that reflects the wrong colour.

Visitors are watched closely, not out of hostility, but because ignorance kills quickly here. Those who listen are treated well. Those who steal from the gardens are rarely invited to explain themselves twice.

The settlement trades medicine, preserved herbs, alchemical mixtures, healing charms, and specialist knowledge to other Veylrath communities, but some plants are never sold outside clan law.
