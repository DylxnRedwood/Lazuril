## Vurokai

Vurokai is a border settlement of the [Veylrath Clans](../Veylrath%20Clans.md), built where the heavy rainforest thins toward savannah, open scrub, and long grass.

It stands near the eastern reaches of the **Mavren Flats**, where jungle trails, hunting paths, and grassland routes meet. The settlement is smaller than Tethvale and less scholarly than [Kelshara](./Kelshara.md), but few places in the Veylrain Expanse are more respected by hunters.

Vurokai is known for trackers, monster slayers, hide-workers, bowyers, scouts, and clan families who spend half their lives reading mud, broken grass, claw marks, feathers, dung, and silence.

## Edge of the Rainforest

Vurokai is built low compared with most Veylrath settlements. Some homes sit in trees, but many are raised on heavy posts above the damp ground, facing outward toward the savannah rather than inward toward the deep jungle.

Watch platforms stand above the grassline. Smoke signals, carved trail posts, bone charms, and hidden warning bells mark the approaches.

The borderlands are rich hunting ground, but they are also dangerous. Beasts move between jungle and grassland with the seasons, and not all of them are natural.

## Hunters and Monster Slayers

The hunters of Vurokai are not merely providers of food. They are scouts, wardens, guides, and the first defence against things that wander too close to clan settlements.

They track river cats, horned grazers, venomous packs, canopy-stalkers, burrowing things, and older monsters that come out of ruined ground after heavy rain.

A kill is respected, but a warning given in time is respected more. Vurokai teaches that the best hunter is not the one who spills the most blood, but the one who brings everyone home.

## Clan Life

Vurokai is practical, blunt, and wary of soft hands.

Visitors are judged by how they walk, how they listen, and whether they notice when the insects stop singing. Outsiders who boast rarely stay welcome for long.

The settlement trades hides, teeth, bone tools, smoked meat, venoms, hunting charms, and monster signs to other Veylrath communities. It also sends trackers to settlements that cannot survive without them.
