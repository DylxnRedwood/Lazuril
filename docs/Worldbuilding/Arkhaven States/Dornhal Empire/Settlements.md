# Settlements of the Dornhal Empire

## Dornhal

Dornhal is the imperial seat of Queen Marzanna Duskhammer and the Matrons of Mithril.

The city is cut into ash-grey cliffs and severe mountain terraces, with bronze reliefs, black iron gates, law courts, administrative halls, and straight roads that leave little room for accident or softness. It is less ancestral than Khuldovar and far more openly imperial.

## Mithrilward

Mithrilward is a council town of forge-mistresses, law-speakers, clan-mothers, archive keepers, and imperial clerks.

Many disputes over inheritance, mining privilege, house rank, and provincial office pass through its chambers before reaching the queen.

## Ashgate

Ashgate guards one of the northern roads descending toward dry frontier lands.

It is a hard place of military stores, caravan inspections, water ledgers, and tunnel surveyors who know how quickly useful roads become invasion routes.

## Vorn Thuldren

Vorn Thuldren is a deep industrial hold known for rune licenses, siege works, heat channels, and state-directed forgecraft.

Its people measure honour in production, reliability, and service to the empire rather than old clan boasting.
