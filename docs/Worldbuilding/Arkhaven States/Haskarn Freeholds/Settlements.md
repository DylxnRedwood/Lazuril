# Settlements of the Haskarn Freeholds

## Haskarn Cross

Haskarn Cross is a growing road town where settlement charters, land claims, grain contracts, and Crown-recognised trade rights are argued over in public halls.

It has no royal beauty and little patience for old titles. Its power comes from roads, warehouses, cattle yards, militia barracks, and families who arrived with nothing and now own enough land to be difficult.

## Reedmere

Reedmere is a lake settlement built on fishing, ferry work, reeds, boat repair, and wetland farming.

It is useful to the Freeholds because lake access means food, movement, and trade without relying fully on Crown roads.

## Gullfen

Gullfen is a rough coastal town of fishers, timber yards, salt sheds, and small shipwrights.

It is poorer than Havenori ports and less orderly than Crown towns, but its people are stubborn, practical, and quick to defend local rights.

## Northbarrow

Northbarrow sits near the pressure lines between Haskarn expansion, Zul’Akari territory, and the Kharad Wildlands.

It is a frontier town of watch posts, survey stakes, militia musters, and farms built with one eye on the fields and one eye on the tree line.
