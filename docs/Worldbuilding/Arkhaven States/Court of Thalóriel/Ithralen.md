## Ithralen

Ithralen is a scriptor town within the [Court of Thalóriel](../Court%20of%20Thal%C3%B3riel.md), built around copy-halls, oath courts, dormitory cloisters, and **Lanthir Súranel**.

Lanthir Súranel is the public elven name of the town’s scriptor college, usually translated into Common as **the College of the Witnessed Word**. Its name carries the sense of speech heard under oath, written before witnesses, and preserved without adornment.

The college trains elven scriptors appointed through the Scriptor Compact. Students learn languages, temple law, religious history, miracle investigation, anti-forgery methods, archival magic, and the discipline of recording what was said rather than what would be easier to believe.

Ithralen is quiet, exacting, and difficult to impress. A careless claim in the morning can become an examination by afternoon, and every chapel bell, lecture hall, and ink-room exists to remind students that poor records can become wars.
