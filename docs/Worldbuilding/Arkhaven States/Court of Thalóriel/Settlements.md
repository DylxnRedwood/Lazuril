# Settlements of the Court of Thalóriel

## Thalóriel

Thalóriel is the southern seat of the High Court, where the realm’s archives, religious colleges, magical halls, and scriptor orders are governed.

The city is quiet by design. Its streets are lined with lecture courts, temple libraries, sealed vaults, copying houses, and debate chambers where doctrine, history, and magic are treated with the same caution.

## Veyrscript

Veyrscript is a scriptor town built around training halls, oath courts, and dormitories for young elven record-keepers.

Students learn languages, temple law, religious history, miracle investigation, and the discipline of writing exactly what was said rather than what would be easier to believe.

## Oranthir Crossing

Oranthir Crossing sits along one of the Court’s central road and river corridors near the Godscar.

It is heavily warded, with bridge chapels, watch archives, shrine courts, and patrol houses used by scholars travelling between the northern and southern holdings.

## Lethvarn

Lethvarn is a northern archive settlement near the Vermillion border.

It is fortified, suspicious, and full of sealed rooms. Reports from Crown lands, religious disputes, relic movements, and strange Godscar activity are copied here before being sent south.
