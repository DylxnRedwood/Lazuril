# Settlements of the Verdant Confederacy

## Greenhollow

Greenhollow is a large market town of mixed human and elven governance, surrounded by pasture, orchards, boundary groves, and low stone roads.

It is typical of the Confederacy at its best: practical human expansion checked by elven land stewardship. Its assembly hall has two doors by tradition, one for town delegates and one for grove speakers.

## Elmsbridge

Elmsbridge stands on a broad river crossing used by farmers, merchants, militia columns, and elven wardens.

The bridge itself is half stone and half living wood, maintained by dwarven masons, human toll clerks, and elven root-mages who all argue about repairs in different languages.

## Barleymere

Barleymere is a farming town known for grain stores, cavalry pasture, harvest fairs, and public militia drills.

It is wealthy enough to be comfortable, but close enough to Crown ambition that its people take defence more seriously than visitors expect.

## Thalan Grove

Thalan Grove is an older elven enclave within Confederacy territory.

It protects sacred trees, old boundary stones, and land-rites that predate many of the surrounding human towns. Disputes over nearby roads often end up here before they reach the central assembly.
