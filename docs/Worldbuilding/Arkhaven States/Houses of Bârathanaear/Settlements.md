# Settlements of the Houses of Bârathanaear

## Vaelcairn

Vaelcairn is a coastal city of council houses, vine-covered balconies, sea gardens, music halls, and open public squares.

It is one of the main gathering places of the Houses of Bârathanaear, where noble families argue, vote, feast, and negotiate with more charm than honesty. Wealth is visible here, but it is softened by civic ceremony and the expectation that even old houses must answer to public reputation.

## Merith Vale

Merith Vale is an inland estate town surrounded by vineyards, orchards, and warm grassland.

It is known for fine wines, long lunches, house alliances, poetry contests, and landowners who prefer debate to war unless the northern border forces the matter.

## Calenport

Calenport is a sheltered harbour town used for coastal trade, pleasure ships, fishing, and diplomatic arrivals.

Foreign visitors often see Bârathanaear first from its clean docks, painted boats, and terraces of white stone and flowering vines.

## Thalanor Rise

Thalanor Rise stands near the mountain-protected northern frontier.

It is less relaxed than the coastal towns, with watch estates, mounted patrols, and house militias trained for disputes with the Eldermarch Court.
