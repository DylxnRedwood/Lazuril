# Settlements of the Zul’Akari Clans

## Akari Tava

Akari Tava is a major council settlement of the Zul’Akari Clans, hidden among jungle roots, reed channels, and lake-fed marshland.

Elders, shamans, oracles, and clan speakers gather here when outside threats force the clans to speak with one voice. Outsiders rarely see more than the landing platforms, painted stones, and watching eyes beyond the trees.

## Vash Koru

Vash Koru is a lake-shore settlement used by fishers, lizardfolk guides, and shrine-keepers who know the old water paths.

The lake is treated as food, border, ancestor, and danger. Boats move quietly here, and foreign vessels are watched long before they find the shore.

## Sul Marak

Sul Marak is a ruin-ward settlement built around vine-choked stone and forbidden lower chambers.

The clans do not treat its ruins as abandoned. Relic hunters are warned once if they are lucky, and not at all if they are not.

## Naza Ruun

Naza Ruun lies in wet jungle where batiri paths, serpent shrines, and hidden trade routes meet.

It is a tense place of bargaining, omens, masks, poison craft, and clan politics that make little sense to anyone expecting a single Zul’Akari law.
