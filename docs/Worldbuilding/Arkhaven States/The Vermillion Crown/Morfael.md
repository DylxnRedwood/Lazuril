## Morfael

Morfael is a riverside town attached to the outer edge of [Caer Oryn](./Caer%20Oryn.md), capital of [The Vermillion Crown](../The%20Vermillion%20Crown.md).

It is close enough to feel the capital’s weight, but not close enough to share fully in its wealth or protection. Morfael exists because Caer Oryn needs river labour, ferry crossings, customs records, cheap storage, fish markets, repair yards, pauper burials, and people willing to do work the upper city prefers not to see.

Morfael should feel wet, lived-in, useful, and watched. It is not lawless, but it is not cleanly ordered in the way Caer Oryn’s higher districts are.

## Outside the Walls

Morfael sits beyond the strongest sections of Caer Oryn’s walls. It has watch posts, toll points, river chains, customs sheds, ferry yards, and patrol routes, but it does not have the same polished order as the capital proper.

People come here when they need work, passage, cheap lodging, quiet trade, river access, or a place where the Crown is watching but not always listening closely.

The town is crowded with boatmen, washerfolk, dock labourers, fishmongers, cart drivers, repair workers, petty merchants, shrine-keepers, guards, pilgrims, widows, ferrymen, and people who cannot afford to live properly inside Caer Oryn.

## The Waterways

The **River Oryn** is the town’s reason for existing.

Goods, passengers, ferries, barges, market boats, and official cargo all pass through Morfael’s stretch of the river. The same waterways also carry runoff, rumours, lost things, and whatever the capital would rather send downstream.

Morfael is useful to the Crown, useful to merchants, useful to ferry crews, and useful to people who prefer not to pass through the most obvious gates.

## Known Establishments

### Chapel of the Last Lantern

The **Chapel of the Last Lantern** is a small, practical chapel connected to **The Final Journey**. It serves drowned sailors, paupers, dock accidents, unidentified dead, and families too poor to pay for formal rites inside Caer Oryn.

Its bell is old and cracked. Locals say it sounds different when rung for someone whose passing is uncertain.

The chapel is not grand. It is a place of last prayers, body-washing, funeral candles, death records, shrouds, and quiet comfort for those who have nowhere more formal to go.

### The Lumenwell Shrine

The **Lumenwell Shrine** is a small Selûnite alcove built into an old flood wall. A shallow basin holds river water, and locals leave candles, names, travel tokens, and prayers for safe passage.

Sailors, ferrymen, widows, messengers, and travellers visit the shrine before night crossings or long roads. It is also a place where families quietly remember those who have not returned.

### The Black Eel Market

The **Black Eel Market** is a cramped fish and salvage market near the lower river steps. Its stalls sell river fish, smoked eel, cheap knives, netting, hooks, repaired boots, recovered river goods, and things no one asks too many questions about.

It is not openly advertised as a criminal place, but most locals know where it is, when to go, and which questions not to ask loudly.

### The Flood Steps

The **Flood Steps** are broad stone steps where Morfael meets the river. Fisherfolk, laundresses, children, dockhands, ferrymen, and paupers use them every day.

During flood season, the lower steps vanish beneath brown-green water. When the river is low, the stones are slick with algae, old rope marks, fish scales, and river mud.

### Harrowchain Ferry

The **Harrowchain Ferry** links Morfael to the opposite bank and the road network beyond. It is used by labourers, cart drivers, messengers, discreet couriers, and those who would rather not pass through the most formal Crown gates.

It is watched, but not always as closely as the capital’s main approaches.

### The Hook and Bell

The **Hook and Bell** is a low tavern near the quay. It serves fish stew, sour beer, dockside gossip, and cheap beds.

It is not safe, but it is useful.

### Vellum House

**Vellum House** is a narrow records office where cargo manifests, letters, permits, witness statements, dockside contracts, and other papers are written for people who cannot read, cannot write, or cannot safely use more official channels.

It is a useful place for those who need paperwork, copied records, or someone who understands how Morfael’s trade actually moves.

### Cairnwell Yard

**Cairnwell Yard** is a repair yard for carts, barges, cracked wheels, pulley frames, chain posts, dock hoists, ferry fittings, and warehouse doors.

It is one of Morfael’s practical working yards: loud, muddy, busy, and essential.

### Low Lantern Row

**Low Lantern Row** is a narrow line of lodging houses, cheap rooms, widows’ rooms, rented storage, and small apothecary rooms.

People stay here when they are passing through, hiding from debt, working temporary river jobs, or living too close to poverty to afford better rooms.

### Marrowgate Road

**Marrowgate Road** climbs from Morfael toward the controlled outer approaches of Caer Oryn. City watch, Crown agents, tax carts, labour traffic, pilgrims, and official processions all use it.

For Morfael locals, it is both a road to opportunity and a reminder that the capital is always uphill.
