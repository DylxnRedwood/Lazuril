## Caer Oryn

Caer Oryn is the capital city of [The Vermillion Crown](../The%20Vermillion%20Crown.md), and one of the wealthiest and most politically important cities in [Arkhaven](../../Continents/Arkhaven.md).

## Capital of the Crown

The city sits at the heart of Vermillion power, serving as the seat of government, taxation, military authority, trade control, and noble administration. Its influence reaches far beyond its walls because the Crown controls movement through the [Godscar](../../Arkhaven%20Lore/The%20Godscar.md) and the Crimson Chain.

Caer Oryn is known for layered districts, guarded gates, controlled river access, wealthy inner wards, and a political culture built around permission, rank, debt, favour, and surveillance.

## Current Status

Caer Oryn will be developed as the primary capital settlement of The Vermillion Crown.
