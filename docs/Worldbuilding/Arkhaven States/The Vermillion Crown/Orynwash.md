## Orynwash

Orynwash is a river town attached to the outer edge of [Caer Oryn](./Caer%20Oryn.md), close enough to feel like part of the capital, but far enough outside the main walls to be treated differently.

It sits downstream of the city, where the **River Oryn** runs west toward **Lake Orynmere**. The lake has not always been called that on older maps, but the name is now commonly used by Crown officials, river workers, and the people who live along its eastern shore.

Orynwash grew where the capital did not want to look too closely. Smaller channels, drainage cuts, old sewer mouths, service roads, spillways, and poorer river land all collect here before the water reaches the lake.

## Outside the Walls

Orynwash is not lawless, but it is not Caer Oryn either.

The town sits beyond the strongest sections of the capital’s walls. It has watch posts, toll points, river chains, customs sheds, and patrol routes, but it does not have the same clean order as the higher districts inside the city.

People come here when they need work, passage, cheap lodging, quiet trade, river access, or a place where the Crown is watching but not always listening.

The town is crowded with boatmen, washerfolk, dock labourers, fishmongers, cart drivers, sewer workers, petty merchants, smugglers, shrine-keepers, beggars, guards, pilgrims, and people who cannot afford to live properly inside Caer Oryn.

## The Waterways

The River Oryn is the town’s reason for existing.

Goods move through Orynwash before reaching Lake Orynmere, while waste, runoff, and hidden things move out of Caer Oryn through the same channels.

The sewer mouths are old, ugly, and heavily watched in theory. In practice, no one watches every grate, outflow, culvert, and half-sunk arch all the time.

This makes Orynwash useful to the Crown, useful to merchants, and useful to people who prefer not to use official gates.

## Known Establishments

### The Final Journey Church

Orynwash contains a church run by **The Final Journey**. It serves as a place of funeral rites, last prayers, care for the dying, and quiet comfort for those who cannot afford grander temples inside Caer Oryn.

It is also one of the important locations connected to the opening campaign premise.

### The Black Eel Market

The **Black Eel Market** is a known part of Orynwash’s lower river life. It is not openly advertised, but most locals know where it is, when it appears, and which questions not to ask too loudly.

It deals in cheap goods, river salvage, food, tools, rumours, stolen items, smuggled wares, and things pulled from places people would rather not discuss.

The Black Eel Market is also connected to the campaign premise, but its deeper details should remain with the adventure material rather than this settlement overview.

## Current Status

Orynwash should be developed as the rough outer settlement of Caer Oryn.

Further detail should define its river lanes, sewer outflows, lower docks, market streets, lakeward roads, small shrines, patrol routes, and the daily divide between people who live under the Crown’s shadow and those who live inside its walls.
