# Settlements of Tirith I Daur

## Tauronel

Tauronel is a hidden warden-hall of Tirith I Daur, set beneath old branches and concealed ridgelines.

It serves as one of Prince Thalion Maerethion’s principal seats when he is away from the formal courts of his parents. Rangers, elder druids, huntmasters, and forest captains gather here to renew patrol oaths and judge trespass near sacred woodland.

## Eryn Vale

Eryn Vale is a small valley settlement of hunters, herbalists, bowyers, and beast-keepers.

Its halls are built into living wood and mossy slopes rather than cleared ground. Outsiders usually pass through without ever seeing the true village, as the paths turn away unless a guide chooses otherwise.

## Moonwell Rise

Moonwell Rise is a sacred settlement built around an ancient spring used for rites of healing, mourning, and forest memory.

Weapons are bound before entering, and even elven wardens speak softly beneath the silver-leafed trees.

## Thornlark Watch

Thornlark Watch is a border settlement near the more open lands of the Verdant Confederacy.

It is practical and watchful, used by scouts, messengers, and wardens who manage road agreements, forest boundaries, and signs of outside intrusion.
