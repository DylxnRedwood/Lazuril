## Firbolgs

Firbolgs are quiet, powerful forest and wildland people found in scattered communities across [Arkhaven](../Continents/Arkhaven.md). They are most often associated with deep woods, old groves, mountain forests, hidden valleys, and places where the boundary between settlement and wilderness remains thin.

## Hidden Stewards

Firbolgs are known in the [Kharad Wildlands](../Arkhaven%20States/Kharad%20Wildlands.md), the northern rainforest clans, and the older forest edges of elven territory. Some live among the [Veylrath Clans](../Arkhaven%20States/Veylrath%20Clans.md), while others keep to remote groves, abandoned shrines, high meadows, and concealed communities that rarely appear on maps.

Their culture values patience, restraint, hospitality, spiritual duty, and the protection of natural places. Many firbolgs avoid cities not because they fear civilisation, but because they see settled peoples as loud, hurried, wasteful, and dangerous when left unwatched.

## Reputation and Role

Firbolgs are often treated as gentle giants by outsiders, though this reputation is only partly true. They prefer peace, but they are not harmless. A firbolg community can be terrifying when defending a sacred grove, hidden spring, burial mound, or forest boundary.

In modern Arkhaven, firbolgs are usually encountered as druids, shamans, wardens, healers, hermits, guides, or quiet members of frontier communities. They rarely seek power, but they often remember old boundaries better than the rulers who claim to own the land.
