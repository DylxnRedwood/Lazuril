## Halflings

Halflings are a small, adaptable people spread across [Arkhaven](../Continents/Arkhaven.md). They existed on the continent before the arrival of [Humans](./Humans.md), though they were rarely organised into large kingdoms of their own.

## Scattered Communities

Halflings are found throughout farming villages, river settlements, orchard towns, caravan routes, trade ports, and mixed states such as the [Windmere Assembly](../Arkhaven%20States/Windmere%20Assembly.md). Small nomadic or dryland halfling groups are also known in the [Kharad Wildlands](../Arkhaven%20States/Kharad%20Wildlands.md).

Their communities tend to be local, practical, and family-centred rather than imperial. Many survived human expansion by adapting quickly, joining new towns, negotiating land rights, or becoming indispensable as farmers, cooks, traders, scribes, guides, and mediators.

## Reputation and Culture

Halflings are often underestimated by larger peoples, which has helped them survive. They are widely regarded as sociable, stubborn, lucky, observant, and difficult to fully uproot once they have made a place home.

In modern Arkhaven, halflings rarely dominate politics, but they are present almost everywhere. Their influence is quiet, domestic, commercial, and agricultural, built through kinship networks, market trust, hospitality, and deep knowledge of local land.
