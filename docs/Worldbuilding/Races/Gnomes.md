## Gnomes

Gnomes are a small, clever, and widely scattered people of [Arkhaven](../Continents/Arkhaven.md). Like [Halflings](./Halflings.md), they existed on the continent before the arrival of [Humans](./Humans.md), though most gnomish communities were too dispersed or hidden to form large states of their own.

## Hidden Settlements

Gnomes are found across the continent in workshops, burrows, river towns, university districts, forest enclaves, and trade cities. Forest gnomes are especially important in the [Myruun Clans](../Arkhaven%20States/Myruun%20Clans.md), where their hidden paths, trapcraft, herbalism, illusion magic, concealed shelters, and small-scale invention help protect clan settlements.

Other gnomish communities live in the [Windmere Assembly](../Arkhaven%20States/Windmere%20Assembly.md), dwarven mountain towns, human cities, and isolated woodland settlements. They are rarely politically dominant, but their technical and magical skill makes them difficult to ignore.

## Craft and Curiosity

Gnomish culture is shaped by curiosity, invention, careful observation, humour, and the protection of hidden places. Many gnomes favour illusion, clockwork, alchemy, mapping, trapmaking, banking, scholarship, or magical craft.

In modern Arkhaven, gnomes are usually seen as useful, strange, secretive, and brilliant in equal measure. Their small settlements often survive by being hard to find, harder to understand, and too valuable for neighbouring powers to casually threaten.
