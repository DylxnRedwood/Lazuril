## Tieflings

Tieflings are a scattered people found throughout [Arkhaven](../Continents/Arkhaven.md), marked by infernal or fiendish traits that set them apart from the communities into which they are born.

## Rumours of Origin

No single explanation for tieflings is accepted. Some people believe tieflings are born from families touched by the [Godscar](../Arkhaven%20Lore/The%20Godscar.md), especially those who drank too deeply from scar-tainted waters or lived too close to red lakes and old veinstone deposits.

Others claim tieflings are blood creations of the Nine Hells, the result of bargains made by parents, ancestors, priests, nobles, or desperate families whose deals passed consequences down through the bloodline.

## Place in Arkhaven

Tieflings can appear in almost any state, race, or class, which makes them difficult for churches and rulers to categorise. Some live openly in cities and ports, while others hide their features or leave hostile settlements before suspicion becomes violence.

They are often looked down upon, feared, or treated as walking evidence of sin, corruption, or bad blood. Wealth, office, magical power, military service, or noble patronage can protect a tiefling from open abuse, but only if they are strong enough or useful enough to make others think twice.
