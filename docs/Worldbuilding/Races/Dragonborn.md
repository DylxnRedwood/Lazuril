## Dragonborn

Dragonborn are a rare and imposing people found in scattered communities across [Arkhaven](../Continents/Arkhaven.md), with some older lineages associated with the northern jungles, scar-adjacent ruins, and ancient draconic sites.

## Uncertain Origin

No single origin for the dragonborn is accepted across Arkhaven. Some elven scholars claim they were brought to the continent by a greater power, placed here for a purpose that predates modern states and perhaps even surviving histories.

Others argue that dragonborn emerged from old draconic lines after dragons walked among mortal peoples in humanoid form. This explanation is popular among churches, storytellers, and those who prefer ancestry over prophecy.

## Place in Arkhaven

Dragonborn appear among the reptilian and marsh-born peoples of the [Jhakur Clans](../Arkhaven%20States/Jhakur%20Clans.md), though they are not numerous enough to define the clans. Others live as soldiers, temple guards, mercenaries, smiths, ruin-watchers, or isolated family lines in human and mixed states.

They are often treated with awe, caution, or superstition. Their appearance makes them difficult to ignore, and their supposed connection to dragons gives them a reputation for pride, endurance, old power, and dangerous destiny.
