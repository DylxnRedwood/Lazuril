## Genasi

Genasi are rare people whose bloodlines carry the influence of elemental power. They may be born among many peoples of [Arkhaven](../Continents/Arkhaven.md), though their presence is most often linked to places where the world feels unstable, charged, or unusually close to raw elemental forces.

## Elemental Bloodlines

Most scholars divide genasi into air, earth, fire, and water lineages, though local traditions use many different names. Air genasi are associated with high mountains, stormlands, exposed cliffs, and impossible winds. Earth genasi are tied to deep stone, caverns, fault lines, mines, and ancient buried places. Fire genasi are linked to forge districts, volcanic heat, magical fire, desert rites, and old pacts with flame. Water genasi are most often found near rivers, lakes, coasts, floods, and scarred waterways.

In Arkhaven, many people connect genasi to the lingering powers of [The Godscar](../Arkhaven%20Lore/The%20Godscar.md), the deep mountain realms of the [Dwarves](./Dwarves.md), the flood-prone lands of [The Skeldic Concord](../Arkhaven%20States/The%20Skeldic%20Concord.md), and the river cultures of the north.

## Place in Arkhaven

Genasi are too rare to form a single state or unified culture. Most are raised among the people into which they are born, though their elemental nature often marks them as unusual, blessed, cursed, or dangerous.

Some become sailors, smiths, shamans, storm-watchers, ruin-delvers, healers, guards, mages, or wandering specialists. Their reputation depends heavily on the element they carry and the culture judging them. A water genasi may be honoured in a floodland village, while a fire genasi may be feared in a town that still remembers holy wars and burning shrines.
