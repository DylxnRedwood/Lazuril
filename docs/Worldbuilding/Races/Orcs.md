## Orcs

Orcs are a hardy people originally associated with the arid plains, scrublands, and flood-prone frontiers of northern [Arkhaven](../Continents/Arkhaven.md), especially the Thornbelt and Floodlands.

## Thornbelt and Floodlands

Older orcish communities lived as roaming clans, raiders, herders, hunters, and warrior bands across the harsh country now linked to the [Kharad Wildlands](../Arkhaven%20States/Kharad%20Wildlands.md) and [The Skeldic Concord](../Arkhaven%20States/The%20Skeldic%20Concord.md). Their cultures were tribal at heart, built around strength, oath, kinship, survival, and the ability to move with the land rather than dominate it permanently.

As human states expanded and frontier societies hardened, many orcs were pushed into service, exile, mercenary work, or mixed settlements. Others adapted into Skeldic oath-bands, Kharad trade gatherings, frontier militias, and city labour forces.

## Adaptation and Decline

Orc culture has survived, but time has not treated it gently. Old clan laws, oral histories, war-songs, and sacred migration routes have been fragmented by settlement pressure, mixed ancestry, military recruitment, and poverty.

Modern orcs can be found across Arkhaven as soldiers, caravan guards, smiths, labourers, hunters, mercenaries, farmers, and clan elders trying to preserve what remains. Many are still tribesmen at heart, even when living in cities that no longer understand the culture they came from.
