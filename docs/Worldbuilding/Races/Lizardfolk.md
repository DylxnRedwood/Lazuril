## Lizardfolk

Lizardfolk are reptilian people found mainly in the northern jungles, marshes, wetlands, river deltas, lake shores, and flooded forests of [Arkhaven](../Continents/Arkhaven.md).

## Northern Jungles

Lizardfolk are important among the [Jhakur Clans](../Arkhaven%20States/Jhakur%20Clans.md), where they form part of the scaled, shelled, venomous, and marsh-born peoples who dominate the northern jungle frontier. They also appear within the [Zul’Akari Clans](../Arkhaven%20States/Zul%E2%80%99Akari%20Clans.md), [Veylrath Clans](../Arkhaven%20States/Veylrath%20Clans.md), and [Myruun Clans](../Arkhaven%20States/Myruun%20Clans.md), though their role varies by region.

Their settlements are usually built around rivers, reed beds, mud islands, tree roots, submerged platforms, cave mouths, warm stones, and sacred pools. Many communities are difficult for outsiders to identify, especially during flood seasons.

## Territory and Memory

Lizardfolk culture is practical, territorial, and deeply tied to hunting rights, nesting grounds, river crossings, old ruins, and ancestral waters. They are not naturally cruel, but they often think in terms of survival, usefulness, threat, and obligation rather than human ideas of politeness or sentiment.

In modern Arkhaven, lizardfolk are often feared by frontier settlers because of old conflicts over northern land. To the lizardfolk, those same conflicts are remembered as defence against intrusion into lands that were already hunted, guarded, and inhabited long before human roads reached the jungle edge.
