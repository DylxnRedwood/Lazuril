## Elves

Elves are one of the oldest surviving peoples of [Arkhaven](../Continents/Arkhaven.md). Their civilisation predates most modern kingdoms, organised churches, and written human histories on the continent.

## The Silent Weald

They are most strongly associated with the south-western forests, especially the region now known as the Silent Weald after [The Green Silence](../Arkhaven%20Lore/The%20Green%20Silence.md). Most major elven settlements, archives, academies, noble houses, and sacred groves remain concentrated in these forests.

The main elven powers include [The Martarië Crown](../Arkhaven%20States/The%20Martari%C3%AB%20Crown.md), [Tirith I Daur](../Arkhaven%20States/Tirith%20I%20Daur.md), [Houses of Bârathanaear](../Arkhaven%20States/Houses%20of%20B%C3%A2rathanaear.md), [The Eldermarch Court](../Arkhaven%20States/The%20Eldermarch%20Court.md), and the [Court of Thalóriel](../Arkhaven%20States/Court%20of%20Thal%C3%B3riel.md). Elves also hold influence in mixed states such as the [Verdant Confederacy](../Arkhaven%20States/The%20Verdant%20Confederacy.md) and the [Windmere Assembly](../Arkhaven%20States/Windmere%20Assembly.md).

## Memory and Influence

Elven society places unusually high importance on continuity, memory, language, education, magical discipline, and the preservation of knowledge. This became even more important after the Green Silence, when large parts of older elven culture were swallowed by the forest and many records were lost.

Because of this history, elves are diligent record keepers. Documents are copied, witnessed, translated, sealed, and stored in separate archives so that history cannot vanish in the same way again. This cultural habit is one of the main reasons elves became closely involved in the creation and operation of [The Scriptor Compact](../Arkhaven%20Lore/The%20Scriptor%20Compact.md).

Elven magic is usually associated with illusion, divination, enchantment, warding, preservation, ritual practice, and memory. After the [War of the False Saints](../Arkhaven%20Lore/War%20of%20the%20False%20Saints.md), elven illusionists became controversial, but also valuable, because they were among the best suited to identifying false miracles and magical deception.

In modern Arkhaven, elves remain one of the continent’s major political, scholarly, and religious influences. Their population is smaller than humanity’s, but their long lives, archives, magical education, and Scriptor institutions allow them to hold influence far beyond their numbers.
