## Kobolds

Kobolds are small reptilian people found in mines, caves, jungle ruins, marsh settlements, old tunnels, and scar-adjacent places across [Arkhaven](../Continents/Arkhaven.md).

## Tunnels and Ruins

Kobolds are one of the scaled peoples of the [Jhakur Clans](../Arkhaven%20States/Jhakur%20Clans.md), where they live among rainforest, swamp, lake shore, and vine-choked ruin systems. Other kobold groups are found beneath human towns, near dwarven mines, around abandoned fortresses, and in tunnels too small or unstable for larger peoples to claim comfortably.

Many kobold communities organise around warrens, brood houses, trap networks, shrine chambers, mining claims, or old draconic stories. Some venerate dragons, dragonborn ancestors, buried monsters, or whatever ancient power their warren believes protects it.

## Cunning and Survival

Kobolds are often dismissed as weak by larger peoples, but that judgement gets many intruders killed. Kobolds survive through numbers, traps, narrow passages, warning systems, ambush routes, scavenged engineering, and obsessive knowledge of their home ground.

In modern Arkhaven, kobolds may be miners, tunnel scouts, trappers, smugglers, ruin-diggers, sewer workers, monster-handlers, or clan auxiliaries. They are rarely respected in open society, but every wise commander knows better than to chase kobolds into tunnels they prepared themselves.
