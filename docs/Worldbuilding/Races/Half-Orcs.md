## Half-Orcs

Half-orcs are people of mixed orcish and human ancestry, most commonly found in the frontier regions where [Humans](./Humans.md) and [Orcs](./Orcs.md) have lived, fought, traded, raided, married, and survived alongside one another for generations.

## Frontier Bloodlines

Half-orcs are especially common around the [Kharad Wildlands](../Arkhaven%20States/Kharad%20Wildlands.md), [The Skeldic Concord](../Arkhaven%20States/The%20Skeldic%20Concord.md), border towns, caravan roads, military settlements, and rough human frontier states. Their families may come from unions of love, alliance, captivity, adoption, survival, or simple proximity in hard lands where bloodline matters less than whether someone can be trusted.

They are not a single culture. Some are raised in orcish clan traditions, some in human towns, some in Skeldic oath-bands, and some between both worlds without being fully accepted by either.

## Reputation and Place

Half-orcs are often valued for strength, endurance, courage, and practicality, but they also face suspicion from people who reduce them to old stories of raiding and border violence.

In modern Arkhaven, many half-orcs become soldiers, labourers, guards, hunters, smiths, scouts, oath-speakers, mercenaries, or adventurers. The most respected among them are those who turn divided ancestry into social advantage, able to move between cultures that would otherwise refuse to speak.
