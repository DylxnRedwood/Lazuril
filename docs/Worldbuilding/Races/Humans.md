Humans are the most widespread and politically dominant people on [Arkhaven](../Continents/Arkhaven.md), but they are not considered native to the continent.

Human histories describe their arrival by sea aboard a small fleet of arks, expeditionary ships, and survival vessels. These ships carried sailors, settlers, priests, soldiers, craftspeople, scholars, officers, nobles, and families from an older homeland beyond the horizon. The name **Arkhaven** is human in origin: to the earliest settlers, the continent was a haven reached by ark.

The first human landing is most strongly associated with the [Republic of Havenor](../Arkhaven%20States/Republic%20of%20Havenor.md), the oldest human-founded state on the continent. From these early coastal settlements, humans expanded inland over generations, founding farms, towns, churches, roads, ports, and fortified states.

Today, humans dominate or strongly influence several major realms, including the Republic of Havenor, [The Vermillion Crown](../Arkhaven%20States/The%20Vermillion%20Crown.md), the [Verdant Confederacy](../Arkhaven%20States/The%20Verdant%20Confederacy.md), and the [Windmere Assembly](../Arkhaven%20States/Windmere%20Assembly.md). Human populations also exist in many mixed regions, frontier settlements, trade towns, religious centres, and conquered or contested territories.

Human power in Arkhaven is based on adaptation, population growth, agriculture, settlement expansion, trade, religious organisation, military flexibility, and political ambition. There is no single human ruler. Human states compete with one another as often as they compete with neighbouring peoples.

The human legacy is contested. Many humans see themselves as descendants of desperate survivors who crossed the sea and built a new home. Older peoples often remember the same process as colonisation, land seizure, displacement, treaty-breaking, forced conversion, and cultural absorption.

Human-led churches and states were major accelerants of the [War of the False Saints](../Arkhaven%20Lore/War%20of%20the%20False%20Saints.md), partly because their institutions were younger, their records less stable, and their rulers eager for divine legitimacy. This history contributed directly to the later authority of [The Scriptor Compact](../Arkhaven%20Lore/The%20Scriptor%20Compact.md).

In modern Arkhaven, humans control many of the continent’s largest cities, farmland regions, trade routes, ports, churches, and armies. Their central legacy remains one of survival becoming entitlement.
