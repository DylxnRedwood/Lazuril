## Tortles

Tortles are a long-lived, patient, and coastal people most strongly associated with the [Myruun Clans](../Arkhaven%20States/Myruun%20Clans.md) of northern [Arkhaven](../Continents/Arkhaven.md).

## Myruun Clan Houses

The Myruun Clans are dominated by tortles. Their coastal settlements, river shrines, shell-marked houses, fishing grounds, and elder councils form the core of Myruun society.

Tortles are well suited to the region’s jungle rivers, mangrove belts, sea cliffs, flooded lowlands, and scar-adjacent waters. Their long lives, defensive habits, coastal skill, river knowledge, and respect for old boundaries make them natural leaders among the Myruun.

## Patience and Memory

Tortle culture values patience, oathkeeping, shrine duty, navigation, fishing rights, controlled hospitality, and long memory. Tortle elders hold high status because they remember floods, treaties, monster migrations, lost routes, and disputes that younger peoples would forget.

Outside Myruun lands, tortles are often seen as calm travellers, river guides, shrine keepers, fishermen, and slow-speaking negotiators. In truth, their patience should not be mistaken for weakness. Tortle communities are difficult to move, difficult to frighten, and very difficult to make forget an insult.
