# Session 3: Redstone Revelations

*Played: 8 July 2026*  
*Location: Magpie Lane, Caer Oryn to Morfael*  
*Starting point: Magpie Lane*  

---

## Back to 17 Magpie Lane

With the rooftop attacker unconscious after the fall from the scaffolds and rooftops, the party moved quickly. They took him back into 17 Magpie Lane and down into the hidden basement beneath the derelict building.

They did this unseen.

Teclis kept one hand ready with invisibility held close, prepared to hide the captive or the party at a moment’s notice if anyone turned into the lane at the wrong time. The street outside still carried the noise of the Foundry Quarter, but beneath the floorboards of number 17 the air was close, damp, and stained by what had already happened there.

The captive was placed on a stool among the barrels. His hands and feet were secured while the party discussed how best to make him talk.

Teclis then created an illusion of a guillotine.

The image was severe enough to change the mood in the room. It was not merely a threat of violence. It was staged, deliberate, and theatrical. Cassian was visibly uncomfortable with it, the line between justice and cruelty already becoming difficult to ignore.

---

## Garron Duskholt

The captive was a human man named Garron Duskholt.

As the party forced him down toward the illusion of the guillotine, they noticed a brand burned into the back of his neck: the crying eye mark they had already come to associate with the hidden faction moving behind the events in Morfael and Caer Oryn.

When questioned, Garron admitted that the mark belonged to an organisation called the Obsidian Register.

At first, he tried to posture. He treated the work as a job, something done for coin and survival rather than conviction. He showed no real remorse for what had happened to Edrin Vaal, for the attack on the party, or for the wider harm caused by the Register’s operations.

That lack of remorse struck Cassian hard. Cassian broke Garron’s nose, leaving blood running down his face as the interrogation continued.

Nakoa’s claws at Garron’s throat and the fear of the guillotine illusion did the rest. Garron began to give answers.

---

## What Garron Revealed

Garron told the party that the drop-off for the lizard idol was at the Gilded Hart, in room 13.

He also confirmed that Edrin Vaal had been taken down the drain where the party later fought him, but Garron claimed not to know where Edrin had been taken after that. The drain was a route, not the final destination.

When asked about his superior, Garron first tried to give a false name: Johan Schmidt.

Alekthar was not convinced. He used detect thoughts, first catching enough from Garron’s surface thoughts to recognise the lie, then pushing deeper. Beneath the false answer, Alekthar found the name Garron was trying not to think about.

Odran Kesh.

Garron said that Kesh was holed up in the Counting House in the Foundry Quarter of Caer Oryn.

When questioned about House Vaelisar, Garron insisted that he did not know what had happened there. He claimed that the Obsidian Register would not risk interfering directly with noble houses. Even so, he also said that body bags had been carried out of the Vaelisar manor, suggesting there had been many deaths inside despite the official silence surrounding the sealing of the house.

---

## The End of the Interrogation

With the main line of questioning over, most of the party reached a silent agreement through telepathic communication. Cassian, unable to join that mental exchange through magic, stood apart from that private consensus.

Teclis then said his goodbyes.

He asked Garron to pick a number between one, two, and three.

Garron chose two.

Teclis summoned three magical daggers. The first shot past Garron over his shoulder. The third skimmed his ear. The second struck directly into his neck.

The force threw Garron backward into the barrels behind him. A moment later, the conjured blades vanished into thin air, leaving the basement quiet except for the aftermath.

---

## Inkglass Apothecary

After leaving Magpie Lane, the party travelled to the Academy Quarter during the remaining hours of the morning. Bells rang out across the district, calling students and professors to classes while the city’s scholarly life continued around them.

Their destination was Inkglass Apothecary, where they went to visit Quincy Adams.

Teclis was greeted warmly. He and Quincy had already built a working relationship, and Quincy received him with open arms rather than suspicion. Teclis asked that Nakoa be treated in the back room, and Quincy’s assistant, Elowen, took charge of his care.

Elowen admitted that she had never provided first aid to a tabaxi before, but assured Nakoa that the inner anatomy was surely similar enough, even if there was far more hair on the outside.

---

## The Red Dust

The party showed Quincy the red dust collected from Edrin Vaal’s body, including the material found around his teeth and under his nails.

Quincy identified it as veinstone.

He explained that the name came from the Crimson Chain, where the material was mined. There were different kinds of veinstone, marked by different colours, but red was the most common.

Quincy had heard rumours of veinstone being used in weapons and in gemstones designed to channel magic. Some had once been set into staves and wands to enhance spellcasting ability. That sort of use was no longer common. Mining had mostly ceased, and veinstone had become a rare resource, largely held by noble houses and the Crown.

Quincy agreed to examine the sample, but warned that he would need more material before he could study it fully.

He also explained that the waters around the Godscar have a reddish hue because of veinstone leaching into them. The water is considered fresh, but drinking it in large volumes is not advised.

Before the party left, Quincy provided two healing potions at a heavy discount to aid them in their future travels. They said their goodbyes, promising to return in a few days once Quincy had finished inspecting the dust.

---

## Return to Morfael

The party then travelled out of Caer Oryn and back down to Morfael.

On the way, they discussed the lizard figurine. Nakoa explained that relics like it had been found in the Veylrain Expanse before. He also noted something strange about them: if touched, they could simply vanish, turning to dust.

The comment left the object feeling less like ordinary contraband and more like something unstable, old, or deliberately protected against handling.

---

## The Chapel of the Last Lantern

Back in Morfael, the party reported to Sister Halva Dorn at the Chapel of the Last Lantern.

She told them that Watch-Corporal Dannel Strake had been looking for them. He wanted to know what he should report up the chain, particularly regarding the killing of Edrin Vaal and the party’s involvement in the events at the Flood Steps.

Teclis also made clear that he intended to arrange additional funding for the chapel, which was not in a fit state to properly support the work being asked of it.

Alekthar then asked whether Old Pell of the Harrowchain Ferry could place items from the chapel’s offering box among any shipments whose manifest bore the Amakiir sigil. The purpose was to create a recognisable marker, allowing the party to identify such shipments later if they came across them.

To help remember the objects, Teclis drew several of the offering-box items for future reference.

Before leaving the matter with Sister Dorn, the party warned that the townsfolk of Morfael should be more cautious, especially at night. They advised that whatever guards Watch-Corporal Strake could spare should patrol more actively and question or apprehend wandering robed figures.

---

## End Point

The session left the party with several clear threads: the Obsidian Register now had a name, Garron Duskholt had pointed them toward the Gilded Hart and room 13, Odran Kesh had been identified at the Counting House in the Foundry Quarter, and Quincy Adams had confirmed the red dust as veinstone.

At the same time, House Vaelisar had become more ominous. Garron claimed the Register would not interfere with noble houses, yet body bags had reportedly been carried from the manor. Whatever happened there was larger than a simple civic sealing.

The party returned to Morfael with new leads, more questions, and a growing sense that the red stone beneath the city was moving through hands far more organised than a single courier route.
