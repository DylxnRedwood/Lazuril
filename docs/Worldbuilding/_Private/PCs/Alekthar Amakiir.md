## Alekthar Amakiir

_Private working page for Alekthar Amakiir. This page is intended for campaign development, DM reference, future session notes, hidden continuity, and long-term tracking of House Amakiir, the Bound Radiance, the Night of Golden Tears, Obari's disappearance, and Alekthar's search for the truth behind the bindings._

Alekthar Amakiir is an Aasimar survivor of one of the old Arkborne Aasimar bloodlines of Arkhaven. He was born at **Cael Tharos**, the ancestral seat of House Amakiir in the Tharos Vale, a fertile valley on the south-western edge of the Crimson Chain within [The Vermillion Crown](The%20Vermillion%20Crown.md). By birth, he inherited the manners, obligations, and public dignity of an old noble house. By trauma, he inherited its ruin.

House Amakiir was never the greatest power in the Crown. Its importance came from land, mine charters, old blood, old memory, and a concealed inheritance known as the **Bound Radiance**. Only some members of the bloodline carried it. Those who did were trained in private and formally inducted through the rite of **Continuance**. Alekthar was not one of those chosen bearers. His younger sister, [Amari](Amari.md), was.

The campaign should treat Alekthar as a man shaped by exclusion from a secret that still destroyed his life. He was loved by his family, but he was denied the truth. He survived the massacre because he did not carry the very inheritance the attackers came to harvest. That fact should remain emotionally poisonous. It makes him heir, survivor, witness, and unintended loose end.

## Core Identity

Alekthar is not a simple revenge-seeker. Revenge lives in him, but it is not the full shape of him. He is an heir of a broken house, a son searching for a vanished father, a brother mourning a sister who became divine fire, and an Aasimar who does not know whether his own blood is holy, cursed, incomplete, or deliberately spared.

He believes the command he heard during the ritual was real.

**Find me. Find the truth. The bindings must be remade.**

He does not know who spoke those words. He does not know whether the speaker was celestial, divine, imprisoned, desperate, guilty, manipulative, or something older than the gods his family invoked. He has decided that the only way forward is to find the source and make judgement himself.

Alekthar's faith has become hard and narrow. He believes in truth. He believes in consequence. He believes that power without honesty destroys everything near it. He does not kneel easily, and any being asking for his devotion will have to answer for Cael Tharos first.

## House Amakiir

House Amakiir was one of the old Arkborne Aasimar lines, respected for age rather than overwhelming political dominance. The family held Crown charters to work the Tharos mines, which produced gold, silver, ceremonial metals, and small quantities of red-flecked stone taken from deep seams near Godscar-touched geology. These mines made House Amakiir useful to the Crown and watched by the Crown.

To the common people of the Tharos Vale, the Amakiir were generous but distant. They maintained roads, vineyards, river mills, miners' widows, shrines, and harvest protections, but their oldest ceremonies were private and their dead were not handled in the ordinary Crown fashion. The local people admired them, relied on them, and never entirely understood them.

House Amakiir's greatest secret was the Bound Radiance. Within the family it was spoken of through partial phrases: **The Light Within**, **The Golden Trust**, and **The Chain of Grace**. Alekthar heard those phrases as a child but was never allowed to understand them.

## The Bound Radiance

The Bound Radiance should remain a private campaign mystery. What Alekthar knows is incomplete and unreliable.

Known facts:

- Only certain members of House Amakiir carried it.
- Those bearers were prepared through private family instruction.
- The rite of Continuance formally revealed the burden to a bearer.
- The attackers at Cael Tharos knew enough to identify and harvest the bearers.
- The ritual used Aasimar blood, powdered red stone, and a runecarved bowl.
- The force opposing the Amakiir was crimson, constricting, and designed to smother or contain golden light.
- Amari's incomplete Continuance caused the gathered Radiance to draw back into her rather than remain under the ritualist's control.
- The ten bearers died hollowed by celestial fire.

The unanswered question is what the Bound Radiance was actually binding. It may have been a blessing, a prison key, a distributed lock, a celestial inheritance, a living seal, or a burden placed in mortal blood because no single vessel could safely contain it.

## The Night of Golden Tears

The Night of Golden Tears began as [Amari](Amari.md)'s Continuance celebration. Twenty-two family members were gathered at Cael Tharos, including nearly every living branch of the house. [Akaavi](Akaavi.md) organised the event and hired additional servants to make it grand. That decision opened the house to infiltration.

Alekthar became suspicious after meeting an unfamiliar servant in the kitchens. She knew details she should not have known, and the new servants' presence matched concerns he had overheard from [Obari](Obari.md) and [Arnemia](Arnemia.md). Before he could warn the hall, a red Tiefling paralysed him and rendered him unconscious.

When Alekthar woke, the feast had become a slaughter. The family were gagged and bound. Guards and servants had been killed. A blood circle had been drawn in the great hall using powdered red stone. At its centre stood [The Horned Tiefling](The%20Horned%20Tiefling.md), surrounded by cultists in stolen servant uniforms.

The ritual targeted the ten bearers of the Bound Radiance: [Aiemi](Aiemi.md), [Arnemia](Arnemia.md), [Akaavi](Akaavi.md), [Araenia](Araenia.md), [Amari](Amari.md), [Aldric](Aldric.md), [Aliana](Aliana.md), [Aleron](Aleron.md), [Aaldar](Aaldar.md), and [Aveline](Aveline.md). Their blood was drawn into the circle, but Amari's incomplete Continuance changed the ritual's outcome. The stolen light gathered in her and became uncontrollable.

Alekthar saw his sister become divine, terrified, radiant, and doomed. Then Amari became Light, and Light exploded.

## Immediate Survivors

Only seven family members survived the Night of Golden Tears.

Alekthar survived because he did not carry the Bound Radiance and because the ritual's blast threw him clear rather than burning him from within. [Obari](Obari.md) survived physically but never truly returned. [Ahkotan](Ahkotan.md), [Catrina](Catrina.md), and [Amalina](Amalina.md) survived from Arnemia's younger brother's branch. [Astaavi](Astaavi.md), the youngest child of Akaavi and [Miriam](Miriam.md), survived. [Avaris](Avaris.md), youngest daughter of [Araenia](Araenia.md) and [Hadvar](Hadvar.md), survived.

The dead included [Oberyn](Oberyn.md), [Aiemi](Aiemi.md), [Arnemia](Arnemia.md), [Akaavi](Akaavi.md), [Miriam](Miriam.md), [Araenia](Araenia.md), [Hadvar](Hadvar.md), [Amari](Amari.md), [Aldric](Aldric.md), [Aliana](Aliana.md), [Alysse](Alysse.md), [Aleron](Aleron.md), [Aldoran](Aldoran.md), [Aaldar](Aaldar.md), and [Aveline](Aveline.md).

## Obari's Silence and Disappearance

Alekthar's father, [Obari](Obari.md), was human and had married into House Amakiir later in life. He had once served in the Vermillion Crown's security apparatus, although he described the work as ledgers, permits, courier routes, and dull border reports. After the massacre, Alekthar understood that this was a careful lie of omission.

Obari survived the Night of Golden Tears but became unreachable. He moved when guided, ate when reminded, slept badly, and stared at doors no one else could see. He sometimes gripped Alekthar's wrist as if about to warn him, but no warning came.

Four years later, Obari disappeared from Cael Tharos before dawn. He left with a horse and an old leather case Alekthar did not recognise. Letters to Obari's former contacts received evasive answers or silence. One reply from Caer Oryn advised Alekthar to stop asking after matters connected to his father's former service.

This is one of Alekthar's strongest campaign hooks. Obari may have recovered enough to act. He may have followed a trail only he could recognise. He may be protecting Alekthar by vanishing, or leading danger away from the house. He may also have known more about the Bound Radiance than he ever admitted.

## Managed Decline

After the massacre, Alekthar inherited Cael Tharos, its debts, its obligations, and its dying authority. He was not ready, but no one would have been.

With help from [Ahkotan](Ahkotan.md) and [Catrina](Catrina.md), he spent years managing the decline of House Amakiir. Lesser holdings were sold. Outlying rights were surrendered. A hunting lodge passed to creditors. Warehouses in [Caer Oryn](Caer%20Oryn.md) went to a merchant consortium. Art disappeared from the west wing. The family bled influence contract by contract.

Yet Cael Tharos survived. The vineyards still produced. The orchards still bore fruit. The Tharos River still watered the terraces. The people of the vale still required judgement, repairs, wages, grain, blessings, and protection from predatory creditors. Alekthar learned stewardship in the cruelest possible way: not by preserving greatness, but by deciding what could be lost without losing the house entirely.

## Search for the Truth

At night, Alekthar searched the estate. He opened sealed rooms, broke locks, read ledgers, questioned old servants, studied scorch marks, examined portraits, and followed every reference to ceremonies he could find. He found marked family trees, cut-away letters, prayers to unnamed light, and a repeated phrase:

**That which is bound must remain.**

He did not find the truth.

Eventually, grief hardened into a vow. Alekthar blamed the hidden power as much as the cultists. He believed the voice in the ritual had used his family, saved him, condemned him, or all three. He swore that he would find it, find the truth, and remake the bindings not for obedience, but for judgement.

## Departure from Cael Tharos

A year after Obari vanished, Alekthar left the Tharos Vale. He passed stewardship of the estate to [Ahkotan](Ahkotan.md) and [Catrina](Catrina.md). They continued to raise [Amalina](Amalina.md), [Astaavi](Astaavi.md), and [Avaris](Avaris.md) together in the remnants of the house.

Alekthar left with a few trusted records, his mother's recovered pendant, a shard of gold-flecked red stone from the ruined hall, and the memory of the command he heard in the heart of the ritual.

He does not abandon House Amakiir by leaving. He leaves because the estate has become a cage of inheritance, debt, grief, and unanswered questions. He needs the world because the answer is no longer in the house.

## Session 1 Use

Alekthar can be drawn into the campaign through anything involving veinstone, red dust, noble archives, occult rites, hidden bloodline duties, cult activity, Crown sealed records, or rumours connected to Obari's old service. He should not need a random tavern hook. His personal mystery already points toward the campaign's larger bindings.

Alekthar should also connect naturally with other PCs. His search for the truth behind the Bound Radiance can intersect with Cassian's celestial and solar investigations, Teclis' institutional work around false miracles and religious truth, and Nakoa's dangerous contact with ancient relics and binding-adjacent forces.

A strong opener route is for Alekthar to be in or near Morfael because a clue has surfaced: red stone dust matching the ritual, an old Crown document referencing Obari, a dead courier from Cael Tharos, or a veinstone soldier whose existence suggests the old bindings are weakening.

## Relationship Hooks with Other PCs

Alekthar and Teclis can initially distrust each other. Teclis represents institutions, theological investigation, and sanctioned scrutiny. Alekthar has seen what noble secrecy and official containment can cost. At the same time, Teclis may be exactly the sort of mind capable of recognising that the Night of Golden Tears was not merely a cult atrocity.

Alekthar and Cassian can connect through the sky, celestial forces, and the question of whether divine light protects or corrupts. Cassian's study of the sun and stars can make Alekthar uncomfortable, because Alekthar has seen golden radiance become lethal.

Alekthar and Nakoa can connect through relic instinct and guilt. Nakoa touched what he should not have touched. Alekthar inherited the consequences of a secret he was not allowed to touch. Both are marked by ancient mechanisms they do not yet understand.

## DM Use

Alekthar works best when truth is costly. Do not give him easy answers. Give him documents with missing sections, witnesses who tell partial truths, enemies who know his family better than he does, and holy or celestial signs that are useful but morally ambiguous.

His strongest scenes should force him to decide whether truth matters more than safety, whether binding something is an act of mercy or domination, and whether his family were guardians, jailers, victims, or collaborators in something they no longer fully understood.
