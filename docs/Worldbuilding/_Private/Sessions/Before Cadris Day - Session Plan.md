# Before Cadris' Day - Session Plan

> [!dm] DM-Only Session File
> This session begins immediately after the party leaves the Counting House through the rear courtyard in the Foundry Quarter. Odran Kesh and his men are alive. The party avoided a fight and left with the lizard figurine in their possession.
>
> Cadris' Day is two days away. A veinstone shipment is expected to reach Caer Oryn by the River Oryn within the next twenty-four hours. Old Pell has agreed to mark suspicious cargo using an item taken from the lost property kept at the Chapel of the Last Lantern.

## Purpose of the Session

This is the last investigation-heavy session before Cadris' Day begins to change the scale of the campaign.

The party has several unfinished matters and should be allowed to choose their order. The most likely course is:

1. Leave the Counting House and realise Kesh is now watching them.
2. Deal with the lizard figurine and gain the first clear glimpse of the old rune-lock.
3. Visit The Final Journey and finally report to Arch-Lector Meren Voss.
4. Learn that the Vaelisar dead never entered Caer Oryn's lawful funerary system.
5. Receive word that Old Pell's marker has been placed on the suspected veinstone shipment.
6. Track the shipment through the river districts and docks.
7. Discover evidence that Edrin was one of several augmentation subjects and that something connected to the programme is intended for Cadris' Day.

Do not force all seven scenes if the party takes another route. The session works as long as the following facts can be reached by more than one path:

- Kesh is still active and now has reason to keep the party under observation.
- The lizard figurine is tied to an old pattern of places across the Vermillion Crown.
- The Vaelisar bodies were removed outside normal death registration and burial procedure.
- A large quantity of veinstone is being moved into Caer Oryn.
- The augmentation work did not end with Edrin Vaal.
- Cadris' Day is connected to whatever is being prepared.

---

# Current Position

The party has surfaced in the Ash Cart Yard behind the Counting House in the Foundry Quarter.

The Counting House remains open and outwardly normal. Odran Kesh has no reason to order an immediate attack. The party spoke with him rather than fighting their way through his office, and he gains more by learning what they do next than by starting a street battle in a working quarter.

The Obsidian Register is the name known to Garron Duskholt and other field agents. Kesh refers to the same organisation internally as the Black Register. Both names are correct, but they reflect different levels of access.

## Read Aloud: Ash Cart Yard

> Heat settles between the rear walls of the Foundry Quarter. The yard smells of soot, wet ash and hot iron. A pair of empty carts stand beneath a timber awning, their wheels grey with furnace dust.
>
> Behind you, the Counting House door closes.
>
> Nothing follows.
>
> Beyond the yard, Caer Oryn carries on as it did before you went below. Shift bells sound somewhere beyond the roofs. Workers cross Ironwake Lane with lunch tins and stamped brass tokens. Red and white Cadris' Day bunting hangs from a line between two buildings, already marked with soot.

Let the players decide where to go before introducing any new interruption.

---

# Odran Kesh After the Meeting

Kesh should remain useful because the party left him alive.

He does not send a kill squad after them. He sends one observer.

The observer is a plain-clothed Black Register runner who keeps well back and changes position whenever the party enters a busier street.

**DC 14 Wisdom (Perception):** notice the same man or woman twice in different places.

**DC 13 Wisdom (Insight):** the watcher is not preparing an ambush. They are noting where the party goes and who they meet.

**DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth) or a sensible counter-surveillance plan:** lose the watcher.

If the party captures the watcher, they know very little. Their orders are simply to follow, record stops and report before midnight.

If the party ignores them, Kesh later learns whether they visited The Final Journey, the river district, House Vaelisar, the Gilded Hart or another obvious site.

### Kesh's Position

Kesh is now trying to answer four questions:

- How much did Garron tell them?
- What do they know about the augmentation work?
- Why do they have the Veylrain figurine?
- Are they likely to interfere with the incoming shipment?

Kesh still believes the augmentation programme is controlled. He does not know the full truth of the old bindings or the purpose of the oldest relics.

---

# The Lizard Figurine

The figurine should become relevant before the party can put it away and forget about it.

It is small enough to fit comfortably in one hand. The stone is green-grey with darker mottling along the back and tail. Close examination shows that the markings are not painted. They pass through the material like veins through marble.

If handled through cloth, leather or a pouch, nothing happens.

If a character deliberately takes it into a bare hand, use the following.

## Read Aloud: First Bare-Handed Touch

> The stone is cold for no more than a heartbeat.
>
> Then it grips.
>
> Not with claws. The weight of it simply seems to catch against your palm. Fine lines brighten beneath the green stone, pale at first, then gold.
>
> The room, street or yard around you disappears.
>
> For an instant you are looking down upon country from impossible height. Eight points burn across the land. Thin lines run between them, not roads and not rivers, but something older laid across both. Together they form a single shape.
>
> At its centre is darkness.
>
> Then the figurine cracks soundlessly in your hand.

The figurine falls into a very fine green-grey powder. Most of the dust slips between the character's fingers, but some clings to the skin and disappears within a few breaths.

This is consistent with what Nakoa already knew of similar Veylrain relics: some vanish into dust after direct handling.

## The Eight Places

During the vision, the character hears or somehow understands eight place-names:

- **Asterwatch**
- **Orenthal**
- **Mereholt**
- **Cadrisford**
- **Alricourt**
- **Goldharrow**
- **Caer Rhodan**
- **Serefall**

Do not explain the names immediately.

These settlements sit upon the old leyline structure which forms part of the rune-lock sealing the buried god. The characters are not yet in a position to understand the whole mechanism.

## Gift of the Figurine

The first creature to trigger the figurine gains the following permanent minor gift.

### Lizard's Hold

The creature gains a climbing speed equal to its walking speed.

In addition, once per long rest, when the creature fails a Dexterity saving throw or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check, it can reroll the die and must use the new result.

The gift is not obviously arcane once absorbed. Detect Magic shows a faint alteration effect for the first hour, after which the change becomes part of the creature rather than a separate enchantment.

A remove curse spell does not remove it.

# The Final Journey

The party has delayed reporting to Arch-Lector Meren Voss for long enough that Teclis should expect a poor reception.

Do not play Voss as theatrical. He is tired, exacting and annoyed. He has already heard enough through Morfael and church channels to know that something serious has happened, but he does not have Teclis' full account.

The meeting should be private. Voss receives Teclis and the others in a plain chamber off the main funerary hall. There is a stone table, two lamps, a water basin and several closed registers.

## Read Aloud: The Final Journey

> The Final Journey is quieter than the streets outside. Heavy doors shut out most of the city's noise, leaving only the scrape of shoes on stone and the murmur of a prayer somewhere beyond the walls.
>
> An attendant takes Teclis' name, disappears through a side door and returns a few minutes later.
>
> “The Arch-Lector will see you now.”
>
> Meren Voss is waiting at a long stone table. Several ledgers have been set beside him. He looks at Teclis, then at the others.
>
> “You're late. Sit down.”

Voss then says only:

> “Start at Morfael. Tell me what happened.”

Let Teclis speak. Voss listens and interrupts only when the account becomes vague, skips an important event or turns opinion into fact.

### What Voss Wants Clarified

He asks about the following as they arise naturally:

- Edrin Vaal's condition when found.
- The brand S-V-AUG-03.
- The red dust later identified as veinstone.
- What Edrin said and whether he appeared to remember himself.
- Garron Duskholt.
- How Garron was questioned.
- Who killed Garron and why.
- Odran Kesh.
- The Counting House.
- The Obsidian Register.
- Room 13 at the Gilded Hart.
- House Vaelisar.
- The bodies seen leaving the manor.
- The black orb and the hidden Vaelisar chamber, if the party tells him.
- Any veinstone samples or relics currently in the party's possession.

### Garron Duskholt

Voss does not shout about Garron's death. He asks Teclis to describe it plainly.

If Teclis softens the account, Voss corrects him.

Useful lines:

> “Was he restrained?”

> “And after he was restrained, you killed him?”

> “I am asking what happened, not whether you believe he deserved it.”

If Teclis describes the guillotine illusion:

> “That was interrogation by threat of execution. Do not dress it up as anything else.”

If Teclis argues that Garron was dangerous:

> “Then bring dangerous men here, or to the Watch, or to someone with lawful custody. Killing a bound prisoner is not an investigation.”

Voss does not remove Teclis from his post. The evidence is too serious, and Teclis is already entangled in the matter.

Before the meeting ends, Voss makes the boundary clear:

> “Keep investigating. Report what you find. And do not execute another prisoner.”

That is all the rebuke needs to be.

---

# What Voss Has Found About House Vaelisar

Voss has checked the records available to The Final Journey since hearing that bodies were removed from House Vaelisar.

He has found no matching funerary trail.

For a household of that standing, this is highly irregular.

There are no corresponding entries for:

- formal transfer of the dead into church custody;
- mortuary intake;
- identification of the bodies;
- burial warrants;
- cremation warrants;
- noble family rites;
- requests for unnamed burial;
- removal of remains outside Caer Oryn.

This does not prove the Civic Directorate killed anyone. It proves that whoever removed the Vaelisar dead bypassed the systems that normally account for bodies in the city.

Voss can put it simply:

> “If those were Vaelisar bodies, they were taken somewhere they were not meant to be recorded.”

He will quietly continue checking church and mortuary records, but he will not openly accuse the Directorate without proof.

If asked whether this could be linked to undeath, he says:

> “Perhaps. It could also be murder, concealment, illegal examination or simple theft of the dead. We do not know yet.”

This is the kind of answer Voss should give throughout. He distinguishes what is known from what is suspected.

---

# Showing Voss the Figurine Vision

If the figurine has already been triggered, the party can describe the eight names and the pattern seen from above.

Voss recognises several of the names as old Vermillion settlements but has no religious tradition connecting all eight.

He has an attendant bring an old regional map used by the church for burial transfers, pilgrim roads and chapel jurisdictions.

The map is not ancient. The pattern upon it is.

If the party marks the eight settlements, the positions form a deliberate figure when connected in the same order seen in the vision.

**DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana):** the shape resembles a large binding or containment rune rather than a spell circle intended to summon something.

**DC 14 Intelligence (History):** several of these settlements are older than the modern Vermillion Crown, even where their present names are newer.

**DC 15 Intelligence (Religion):** the geometry is not associated with Kelemvor, Selûne, Shar or another familiar modern faith.

Voss has no answer for what is being bound.

If asked what he thinks:

> “I think eight towns do not accidentally sit on the points of a sigil.”

He then adds:

> “Find out who knew that before you did.”

Do not have Voss explain the old god, the Aasimar bindings or the full purpose of the lock.

---

# Word from Morfael

Use this scene when the party needs a push toward the river. It can happen while they are still at The Final Journey, immediately outside, or after they stop elsewhere in Caer Oryn.

A runner arrives with a short message from Sister Halva Dorn.

Old Pell has seen a shipment matching the suspicious Amakiir-marked traffic.

He has placed the agreed marker aboard it: a recognisable item taken from the lost property kept at the Chapel of the Last Lantern.

Choose a plain object the players can identify at a glance. A good choice is **a small brass Kelemvorite grave token with one corner bent**, left at the chapel months ago and never reclaimed.

Halva's message is brief:

> **Pell found one. Marker is aboard. Upriver cargo. Caer Oryn by tonight or morning if the docks hold it.**

The shipment is coming in along the River Oryn from the direction of Lake Oryn.

The party now has a reason to reach the river districts before the cargo disappears into Crown storage.

---

# Finding the Shipment

The river district is crowded because Cadris' Day traffic has begun to build. Barges wait for berths, dockhands move festival supplies, military stores receive priority and customs clerks are working late.

The party should be able to find the shipment through investigation rather than a single required check.

## Read Aloud: The River Quays

> The River Oryn is crowded from bank to bank. Barges sit three deep against the lower quays, their crews shouting across coils of rope while customs clerks work beneath hooded lamps.
>
> Cadris' Day banners have been strung along the warehouse fronts, but most of the men beneath them are too busy to look up. Crates of wine, flour, lamp oil, uniforms and ironwork wait beneath chalked numbers. A military wagon has been given half a lane to itself.
>
> Somewhere among it all is one marked piece of cargo.

### Ways to Locate It

Let the players use whatever makes sense. Suitable checks include:

| Method | Check | Result |
|---|---:|---|
| Follow unloading crews from upriver barges | DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) | Spot a guarded cargo being given unusual priority. |
| Read public cargo boards and berth lists | DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) | Narrow the search to two barges and one warehouse lane. |
| Question dockworkers without drawing attention | DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) | Learn that a Crown-marked consignment was moved off the normal queue. |
| Bribe a tally clerk | 5-10 gp | Gain the wagon number and receiving yard. |
| Use Alekthar's knowledge of the false Amakiir marks | DC 12 Intelligence (History) or Wisdom (Perception) | Identify a manifest using the same copied house authority seen before. |
| Follow Black Register observers | DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) or Wisdom (Survival) | Reach the cargo from the people guarding it rather than the paperwork. |

Do not stop the investigation because of a failed check. Failure costs time, money or discretion. It should not erase the lead.

### The Marked Cargo

The party eventually spots the chapel marker tied beneath a tarpaulin cord or wedged deliberately between two slats of a shipping crate.

The marked cargo is part of a larger consignment.

The visible documents describe it as something harmless and official, such as:

**SPECIAL ENGINEERING MATERIAL**  
**ARMATURE DIRECTORATE**  
**PRIORITY CIVIC CONSIGNMENT**

No public paperwork uses the word veinstone.

The destination is a secured Crown receiving yard serving the Foundry Quarter and Cadris' Day preparations.

---

# Examining the Veinstone Cargo

If the party gets close enough to open, inspect or magically examine one of the reinforced crates, it contains pieces of raw veinstone packed in treated cloth and fine wood wool.

The quantity is far greater than the samples they have seen so far.

The stone is red with fine golden flecks. Even through the wrappings it carries a slight warmth.

**DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana):** this is enough material for repeated magical or alchemical work. It is not a single experimental sample.

**DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation):** the packing list uses batch numbers rather than mineral weights. Several entries carry the prefix **AUG**.

**DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation):** find a folded handling sheet containing subject or cohort references.

Use numbers beyond Edrin's designation. For example:

- S-V-AUG-11
- S-V-AUG-14
- S-V-AUG-18
- S-V-AUG-21
- S-V-AUG-24

The point is not the exact sequence. The point is that **S-V-AUG-03 was not a unique case**.

Edrin was early.

There have been more subjects since.

# Optional Encounter: A Living Subject

Use this only if the session needs a fight or the party chooses a risky approach to the cargo.

One nearby transport wagon contains a restrained augmentation subject being moved under guard.

The subject is alive, frightened and unstable. They are not a mindless monster.

During the party's interference, the subject wakes, breaks one restraint and panics. Red-gold lines become visible beneath the skin around the neck, chest and forearms.

Black Register personnel move to contain the scene. Their priority is recovery if possible, death if not.

## Encounter Composition for a Level 4 Party

Use:

- **1 Augmented Subject**
- **2 Black Register field agents**
- **2 ordinary Crown guards**

The ordinary guards believe they are escorting a dangerous prisoner. They do not know the full nature of the experiments.

The fight is more interesting if the party has reason not to kill everyone.

### Augmented Subject

Use a **Berserker** or similarly simple martial stat block as the base, with the following changes:

- Reduce hit points to about 45 if needed for pacing.
- Damage is bludgeoning or weapon damage rather than supernatural claws unless the fiction calls for it.
- Once during the encounter, when reduced below half hit points, the subject releases a veinstone surge. Each creature within 10 feet makes a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw**, taking **2d6 force damage** on a failure or half as much on a success.
- After the surge, the subject is frightened and disoriented rather than empowered.

A character who succeeds on **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion)** after the surge can reach the subject if they are not being attacked at the time. Appropriate magic, restraint or healing can also end the encounter without killing them.

### What the Subject Knows

If saved and calmed, the subject can provide only fragments:

- They were taken or recruited through a Crown-linked programme.
- There were other subjects.
- Veinstone was implanted, infused or used during treatment.
- Some subjects died.
- They heard staff talking about a demonstration on Cadris' Day.

Do not let the subject explain the entire project.

---

# The Cadris' Day Link

Whether through the cargo paperwork, the optional subject or a stolen dispatch case, the party can find a final document connecting the augmentation work to Cadris' Day.

Keep it bureaucratic rather than melodramatic.

A suitable document reads:

> **CADRIS' DAY - SPECIAL PRESENTATION**  
> **FINAL COHORT TRANSFER**  
> **Receiving authority: Armature Directorate**  
> **Security support: Surveyor Directorate**  
> **Transfer to be completed before second bell on the eve of Cadris' Day.**

A separate initial or seal indicates approval from the Lord Protector's office.

This does not prove that Maeron Cadris understands every part of the experimentation. It proves that his government intends to present its result publicly.

That distinction is useful later.

---

# Ending the Session

The strongest ending is the moment the party understands that the Cadris' Day celebration and the augmentation work are the same story.

Do not add another attack after the reveal unless the players create one.

Give them the document, let them read it, and allow a few minutes of discussion.

Then remind them of the time.

Cadris' Day is two days away.

The veinstone has arrived.

The final cohort is being moved.

Odran Kesh is still alive and knows they are involved.

The party now has one day to decide what they are going to do before the city fills for the celebration.

---

# If the Party Goes Somewhere Else

The session should not depend on visiting scenes in the planned order.

## If They Return to Odran Kesh

Kesh will see them again if they come openly. He is less patient this time but still willing to talk.

He will not admit to the full augmentation programme unless they show proof. If they mention the shipment, he knows they have moved faster than expected.

He may offer a warning rather than a threat:

> “You have two days before half the city is in the streets. Decide whether you want answers or a riot.”

Kesh knows that Cadris' Day is dangerous because the programme is being pushed into public view before all of its problems are solved.

He does not know about the rune-lock.

## If They Go to House Vaelisar

The manor remains under seal. Security is tighter than before. The important new information is that no lawful funerary record exists for the bodies removed from the site.

A character who watches the property for an hour can notice Directorate records staff removing boxed papers rather than searching the wreckage.

## If They Return to Morfael

Old Pell can tell them exactly where and when he marked the cargo and what vessel carried it. This makes locating the shipment automatic but costs enough time that the cargo may already be within Caer Oryn's receiving yards when they return.

## If They Ignore the Shipment

The cargo reaches the Foundry Quarter and disappears into a secured Crown yard before morning.

The party can still learn of the Cadris' Day presentation through Voss, Kesh, captured correspondence or later investigation, but interfering with the material becomes much harder.

---

# Background Clock

Use the following as a guide rather than a rigid timetable.

## Tonight

- The veinstone shipment enters Caer Oryn or waits outside the busiest docks.
- Kesh has the party watched.
- Final Cadris' Day preparations continue throughout the Foundry and river districts.

## Tomorrow

- The shipment is moved into a secure Crown yard.
- Black Register staff begin closing obvious loose ends.
- The final augmentation cohort is prepared for transfer.
- Street barriers, parade routes and public viewing areas begin to close parts of the city.

## Eve of Cadris' Day

- The final cohort is moved under heavy guard.
- Directorate security increases sharply.
- Access to the Foundry Quarter becomes difficult without papers, disguise or an official sponsor.

## Cadris' Day

- Maeron Cadris intends to present the results of the augmentation programme as a new achievement of the Vermillion Crown.
- The public does not know the cost of the work.
- The party may know enough to intervene, expose it, investigate further or allow the presentation to proceed while watching for what goes wrong.

# DM Reference: Threads in Play

### Odran Kesh
Alive. Not yet an open enemy. Watching the party.

### Obsidian Register / Black Register
The same network. Obsidian Register is the field-facing name. Black Register is the internal compartment name used by Kesh.

### Lizard Figurine
Veylrain relic. Direct bare-handed handling can consume the relic, grant a minor physical gift and reveal the eight-point lock pattern.

### Rune-Lock Settlements
Asterwatch, Orenthal, Mereholt, Cadrisford, Alricourt, Goldharrow, Caer Rhodan and Serefall.

### Arch-Lector Meren Voss
Still Teclis' superior at The Final Journey. Angry about Teclis' conduct, but more concerned with getting an accurate account and investigating the missing Vaelisar dead.

### House Vaelisar
The bodies removed from the manor do not appear in ordinary funerary or mortuary records.

### Old Pell
Has carried out the party's plan and marked a suspicious shipment using an item from the Last Lantern's lost property.

### Veinstone Shipment
Inbound by the River Oryn from Lake Oryn. Due within twenty-four hours, subject to dock traffic.

### Cadris' Day
Two days away. The experimental augmentation programme is intended to be shown publicly as part of the celebration.
