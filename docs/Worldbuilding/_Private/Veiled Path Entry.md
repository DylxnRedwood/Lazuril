# Veiled Path Entry

## Nakoa’s Induction

### The Contact

After the party leaves House Vaelisar, a human woman named **Una Redmarch** bumps into Nakoa in a crowded Caer Oryn street.

She carries folded mourning cloth and appears to be an ordinary travelling seamstress. Within the Veiled Path, she is known as **Moth**.

As she steadies herself against Nakoa, she slips a folded note and a drilled copper coin beneath his sleeve.

> “Forgive me. I was watching the wrong feet.”

If Nakoa notices or catches her, she says:

> “The obvious hand is for children.”

She leaves the note with him and disappears into the crowd.

### The Note

> Rook taught you to watch the hand.
>
> He neglected to teach you who watches the rook.
>
> You opened Thirteen. You found the false name. You walked an old road without knowing whose footprints lay beneath yours.
>
> At the second bell after dusk, carry the enclosed coin to the dry fountain behind the Old Weighhouse in the River Districts.
>
> Place it between the teeth of the stone fox.
>
> Do not wait beside it.
>
> Follow the person who returns it.
>
> Come alone. Speak no names. Tell no watchman, priest, Crown agent or scriptor.
>
> You are not invited twice.
>
> The Path has noticed you.

### The Route

At the dry fountain, Nakoa places the coin inside the mouth of a stone fox.

An elderly beggar later removes it and walks away without acknowledging him. Nakoa must follow at a distance through several streets, a covered market and an apparently abandoned dye shop called **The Unspun Thread**.

The route ends in its cellar.

### The Test

Una waits behind a plain table. Before her are:

- A purse containing fifty gold pieces.
- A sealed letter bearing a Crown official’s crest.
- A false black-and-gold feline figurine.

She says:

> “Take what belongs to you.”

The expected answer is to take nothing.

If Nakoa reaches for something, Una asks:

> “Does opening a door make the room yours?”

When he refuses the objects, she removes the figurine.

> “Good. He did teach you something.”

### The Offer

Una explains that room 13 was connected to old routes once used by the Veiled Path. Someone is moving relics, veinstone and forged papers through those routes without permission or payment.

The Path has noticed Nakoa because he found the room, recognised the Veylrain materials and carries skills taught by Arden Rook.

If asked whether Arden remains a member, Una answers:

> “A person may leave the road. That does not mean the road forgets their footsteps.”

### The First Veil-Tithe

Una asks Nakoa to surrender one genuine secret.

> “Coin proves only that you possess coin. Give the Path one truth that no stranger could purchase.”

She does not demand the truth about his relic, though she watches his reaction carefully.

### The Rules

Una recites the rules:

> “Do not steal from the marked.”
>
> “Do not expose a safehouse.”
>
> “Do not speak our signs to law, Crown, temple, scriptor or torturer.”
>
> “Pay the veil-tithe when the road carries you.”
>
> “A job may be refused. A debt may not be forgotten.”
>
> “Never ask who waits at the end of the Path.”

She then asks:

> “Do you enter?”

If Nakoa accepts, he chooses a Path-name.

> “Do not choose something you love. Do not choose something you fear losing. Choose a name you could hear spoken at your own funeral without turning your head.”

### The Mark

Una returns the copper coin, now threaded with dark-grey silk.

It identifies Nakoa as a member and may provide access to safehouses, couriers, fences, forged papers and criminal information.

Every service carries a price, tithe or future debt.

### First Assignment

Una gives Nakoa his first task:

> “Derren Kerr is a name written over another name. Find the first name.”
>
> “Do not confront him.”
>
> “Do not warn whoever employs him.”
>
> “Bring us the name, the hand that altered it, or the reason someone wished it buried.”
