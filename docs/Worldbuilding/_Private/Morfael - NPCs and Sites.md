# Morfael: NPCs and Sites

> [!dm] DM-Only Settlement Reference
> This file supports [[Campaign Opener - The Man in the River]] and the early chapters of *The Bindings Must Be Remade*. It is written for private campaign planning and contains hidden faction links, secrets, and possible future leads.

## Settlement Summary

[[Morfael]] is a riverside town attached to the outer edge of [[Caer Oryn]], capital of [[The Vermillion Crown]]. It is not wealthy enough to be clean and not distant enough to be free. The town exists because the capital needs river labour, ferry crossings, customs records, cheap storage, fish markets, pauper burials, repair yards, and people willing to do work the upper city prefers not to see.

Morfael should feel wet, lived-in, poor in places, useful to everyone, and trusted by no one. It is close to power, but rarely protected by it.

The opening chapter [[Campaign Opener - The Man in the River]] begins here.

## Tone and Naming Style

Morfael is within [[The Vermillion Crown]], a human-led nation with militarised institutions, river authority, old Crown offices, and strict control of movement near [[Caer Oryn]]. Names in Morfael should feel grounded, human, slightly worn, and regionally distinct without being too obviously symbolic.

Avoid names that state the NPC’s role too bluntly. A ferryman should not be called Ferryman Reed. A gravekeeper should not be called Graves. Most people here are ordinary humans with ordinary names who happen to live beside dangerous secrets.

## Important Sites

### The Flood Steps

The broad stone steps where the river meets the town. Fisherfolk, laundresses, children, dockhands, ferrymen, and paupers use these steps every day. During flood season, the lower steps vanish under brown-green water.

This is where [[Edrin Vaal]] washes ashore in [[Campaign Opener - The Man in the River]]. Red grit can be found in the cracks between stones before the body appears, fine as brick powder and faintly metallic.

**Associated NPCs:** [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Tovin Rusk|Tovin Rusk]], [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Lysa Marrowick|Lysa Marrowick]], [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Mara Venn|Mara Venn]], [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Old Pell|Old Pell]], [[Cassian Clarke]], [[Nakoa]].

### The Black Eel Market

A cramped fish and salvage market near the Flood Steps. Its stalls sell river fish, smoked eel, cheap knives, netting, hooks, repaired boots, recovered river goods, and things no one asks too many questions about.

The market is not a thieves’ guild den, but [[The Veiled Path]] uses it when it needs ordinary trade to hide unusual cargo. Red veinstone dust and Veylrain fragments have recently moved through the market under false cargo names.

This is where [[Nakoa]] has spent most of his time before the opener, watching for relic traffic and following Rennic Sallow’s lead. It is also where [[Cassian Clarke]] first crossed paths with him while tracing the last known route of a missing courier from Eira Lorne’s list.

**Associated NPCs:** [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Mara Venn|Mara Venn]], [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Rennic Sallow|Rennic Sallow]], [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Branna Cale|Branna Cale]], [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Tovin Rusk|Tovin Rusk]], [[Nakoa]], [[Cassian Clarke]].

### Chapel of the Last Lantern

A small, practical chapel connected to [[The Final Journey]]. It serves drowned sailors, paupers, dock accidents, unidentified dead, and families too poor to pay for formal rites in [[Caer Oryn]].

Its bell is old and cracked. Locals say it sounds different when rung for someone who has not fully died.

**Associated NPCs:** [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Sister Halva Dorn|Sister Halva Dorn]], [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Caldus Wye|Caldus Wye]], [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Nira Clent|Nira Clent]], [[Cassian Clarke]], [[Teclis Nimule Sílorion]], [[Alekthar Amakiir]], [[Nakoa]].

### The Lumenwell Shrine

A small Selûnite alcove built into an old flood wall. A shallow basin holds river water. Sailors, ferrymen, widows, messengers, and travellers leave candles here before night crossings or long roads.

The shrine does not need a dramatic sky omen to matter. It matters because it is where ordinary people ask for safe passage, write names of the missing, leave letters for those who may never return, and sit with uncertainty.

[[Cassian Clarke]] is drawn here through missionary work, comfort for local families, and his own habit of writing home from places where distance and faith meet. He has helped [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Eira Lorne|Eira Lorne]] keep a list of missing riverfolk whose families do not trust the watch.

**Associated NPCs:** [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Eira Lorne|Eira Lorne]], [[Cassian Clarke]], [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Sister Halva Dorn|Sister Halva Dorn]].

### The Red Lockhouse

A Crown-controlled customs and lock office near the canal mouth. Publicly, it handles tolls, cargo seals, river permits, and lock schedules. Privately, its lower chambers connect to covered river infrastructure used by the [[Crown Scar Directorate]].

The Red Lockhouse should become a follow-up investigation site after the opening conflict.

**Associated NPCs:** [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Joric Marn|Joric Marn]], [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Lieutenant Carro Vance|Lieutenant Carro Vance]], [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Surgeon-Mage Pellisar Oth|Surgeon-Mage Pellisar Oth]].

### The Kestrel Drain

A wide storm outflow barred with rusted iron. At low water, a small creature or desperate person could crawl inside parts of it. Red grit gathers near the grate after rain and stains the water before sinking between stones.

This is the likely route by which [[Edrin Vaal]] enters the river, whether escaped, dumped, or released.

**Associated NPCs:** [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Old Pell|Old Pell]], [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Iven Cald|Iven Cald]], [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Veyra Sorn|Veyra Sorn]], [[Nakoa]].

### Harrowchain Ferry

A chain ferry linking Morfael to the opposite bank and several river paths used by labourers, smugglers, and those who prefer not to pass through Crown gates.

One missing courier from Eira Lorne’s list was last seen between Harrowchain Ferry and the Black Eel Market. This is the lead that first places Cassian and Nakoa on the same path.

**Associated NPCs:** [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Old Pell|Old Pell]], [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Bram Odrick|Bram Odrick]], [[Cassian Clarke]], [[Nakoa]].

### The Hook and Bell

A low tavern near the quay. It serves fish stew, sour beer, dockside gossip, and cheap beds. It is not safe, but it is useful.

**Associated NPCs:** [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Orwyn Breck|Orwyn Breck]], [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Sella Varrow|Sella Varrow]], [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Rennic Sallow|Rennic Sallow]].

### Vellum House

A narrow records office where cargo manifests, false invoices, letters, permits, and dockside contracts are written for people who cannot read or cannot safely use official channels.

A copied fragment of a mineral manifest bearing an Amakiir-linked cargo mark can be found here or traced back here. This gives [[Alekthar Amakiir]] a grounded reason to come to Morfael.

**Associated NPCs:** [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Rennic Sallow|Rennic Sallow]], [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Nira Clent|Nira Clent]], [[Alekthar Amakiir]], [[Nakoa]].

### Cairnwell Yard

A repair yard for carts, barges, cracked wheels, pulley frames, chain posts, and dock hoists.

**Associated NPCs:** [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Garran Tallow|Garran Tallow]], [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Aedan Marric|Aedan Marric]].

### Low Lantern Row

A narrow row of lodging houses, cheap rooms, widows’ rooms, and rented storage. People disappear here without always leaving town.

**Associated NPCs:** [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Lysa Marrowick|Lysa Marrowick]], [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Maelis Wren|Maelis Wren]], [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Veyra Sorn|Veyra Sorn]].

### Marrowgate Road

The road climbing from Morfael toward the controlled outer approaches of [[Caer Oryn]]. City watch, Crown agents, tax carts, and official processions use this road more than locals do.

**Associated NPCs:** [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Watch-Corporal Dannel Strake|Watch-Corporal Dannel Strake]], [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Cerys Varrow|Cerys Varrow]], [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites#Lieutenant Carro Vance|Lieutenant Carro Vance]].

## Opening Conflict NPCs

### Edrin Vaal

Human man, early 30s. Failed veinstone-dust subject.

Edrin is the man who washes ashore at the Flood Steps. He was once a soldier, prisoner, or condemned military subject used in an early [[Crown Scar Directorate]] experiment involving powdered red [[Veinstone]]. He is not evil. He is frightened, damaged, and dangerous because something has been forced into his body and soul.

**Opening role:** Central conflict.

**Secret:** Edrin was designated Subject V-03. He can lead the party toward the Red Lockhouse if stabilised.

### Mara Venn

Human woman, late 40s. Fishmonger at the Black Eel Market.

Mara is loud, practical, and protective of local children. She knows who belongs in Morfael and who walks like they are pretending to belong.

**Opening role:** Local adult witness and civilian anchor.

**Useful information:** Mara saw a covered black barge pass before dawn, moving against the usual traffic. Its crew wore plain cloaks but military boots.

### Tovin Rusk

Human boy, 11. Errand-runner and net-mender.

Tovin is quick, curious, and bad at staying away from danger. He is the first to see Edrin in the water.

**Opening role:** Child witness in immediate danger.

**Useful information:** Tovin saw gold light under Edrin’s skin before anyone touched him and red dust along the lower flood stones before the adults arrived.

### Old Pell

Half-elf man, elderly. Ferryman of Harrowchain Ferry.

Old Pell knows the river better than the town watch knows its streets. He speaks slowly and notices everything.

**Opening role:** River expert and possible escape contact.

**Useful information:** Edrin did not drift from far upriver. The current suggests he came from a nearby drain, lock tunnel, or concealed outflow.

### Sister Halva Dorn

Dwarf woman, middle-aged. Lay-priest of the Chapel of the Last Lantern.

Halva tends the poor dead, drowned sailors, unnamed bodies, and families who cannot afford the upper city. She is stern, compassionate, and difficult to intimidate.

**Opening role:** The person most likely to have gathered the party through the Last Lantern Inquiry.

**Useful information:** Edrin is not undead. His soul is present, but badly anchored and misaligned.

### Lysa Marrowick

Human woman, 30s. Laundress and widow.

Lysa works the Flood Steps because she has no better place to wash linen. She is practical, tired, and protective of her younger neighbours.

**Opening role:** Civilian trapped close to Edrin when he wakes.

**Useful information:** The cloth around Edrin is military medical binding, not common linen. It is stained with red mineral grit rather than ordinary blood alone.

### Rennic Sallow

Halfling man, 50s. Broker-scribe at Vellum House.

Rennic writes cargo manifests, letters, false invoices, and dockside contracts for people who cannot or should not use official channels. He is not a full member of [[The Veiled Path]], but he has passed messages for them and knows [[Arden Rook]] by reputation.

**Opening role:** Nakoa’s local contact, Alekthar’s route into the false manifest, and the bridge between relic traffic and the missing-person trail.

**Useful information:** Red veinstone dust was moved through Morfael in sealed spice jars two nights earlier, and a forged or misused Amakiir-linked mineral mark appears on a related cargo reference. Rennic has a tiny dust smear wrapped in waxed paper, which reacts faintly when Nakoa comes close.

### Watch-Corporal Dannel Strake

Human man, 40s. Lower-town watch corporal.

Strake is tired, cautious, and not as corrupt as people assume. He wants order first and truth second, but he knows Crown involvement usually means ordinary people vanish from the story.

**Opening role:** First arriving authority.

**Useful information:** If the Crown claims Edrin, no one in Morfael will ever hear the truth again.

### Veyra Sorn

Tiefling woman, 20s. Silent Chamber spotter posing as a dockside clerk.

Veyra appears quiet, ink-stained, and unimportant. She is anything but. She watches Morfael for evidence of Crown failures resurfacing.

**Opening role:** Hidden observer.

**Useful information:** If caught or followed, she links the opening incident to the [[Silent Chamber]] without revealing the full structure.

### Lieutenant Carro Vance

Human man, 30s. Crown recovery officer.

Vance is clean, polite, controlled, and threatening by implication. He claims Edrin is part of a military health matter.

**Opening role:** Official Crown response after the conflict.

**Useful information:** Vance’s presence proves the incident matters to someone higher than the town watch.

### Surgeon-Mage Pellisar Oth

Gnome man, 60s. Alchemical physician attached to the recovery team.

Pellisar cares more about what Edrin’s body proves than whether civilians were hurt. He watches Nakoa too closely if he sees the resonance.

**Opening role:** Scientific/alchemical face of the cover-up.

**Useful information:** He recognises the red veinstone dust process immediately and is alarmed by the reaction.

## Civic and Authority NPCs

### Cerys Varrow

Human woman, 50s. Crown-appointed reeve of Morfael.

Cerys manages taxes, dock complaints, street repairs, and official notices. She is capable and unpopular because her authority comes from [[Caer Oryn]].

**Public role:** Settlement administrator.

**Secret:** She knows the Red Lockhouse receives sealed orders she is not permitted to read.

### Iven Cald

Human man, 30s. Deputy watchman and Strake’s second.

Iven is younger than Strake and more eager to impress Crown officers. He is not cruel, but he mistakes obedience for competence.

**Public role:** Junior authority figure.

**Secret:** He has accepted small payments to ignore night traffic near the Kestrel Drain.

### Joric Marn

Human man, 40s. Customs factor at the Red Lockhouse.

Joric keeps records, stamps permits, and pretends not to understand the coded entries in certain ledgers.

**Public role:** Customs official.

**Secret:** He knows the phrase “stone meal” means something dangerous, but he does not know the full truth.

### Elian Marr

Human man, 60s. Retired lock engineer.

Elian knows the old waterworks beneath Morfael better than current officials do. He has bad knees, sharp eyes, and no love for modern Crown secrecy.

**Public role:** Elderly technical expert.

**Secret:** He knows an older service route into the lower Red Lockhouse chambers.

## Religious and Healing NPCs

### Eira Lorne

Human woman, 30s. Caretaker of the Lumenwell Shrine.

Eira is not a formal priestess. She keeps the basin clean, lights candles, records names of the missing, and helps travellers leave letters for people they may not see again.

**Public role:** Selûnite shrine keeper.

**Secret:** She has been keeping a private list of missing riverfolk whose families do not trust the watch. Cassian has helped her comfort several of those families and organise the list into names, routes, dates, and last-known crossings. One of those missing people also appears in Rennic Sallow’s hidden courier notes, creating the first overlap between Cassian and Nakoa.

### Caldus Wye

Human man, 50s. Body-washer and quiet helper at the Chapel of the Last Lantern.

Caldus prepares bodies for burial and knows the difference between drowning, beating, sickness, and something stranger.

**Public role:** Mortuary assistant.

**Secret:** He once helped hide a body from Crown officials because the family begged him to.

### Maelis Wren

Human woman, 40s. Apothecary of Low Lantern Row.

Maelis sells poultices, sleep draughts, fever tinctures, dockhand liniment, and things she claims are only for rats.

**Public role:** Healer and apothecary.

**Secret:** A Crown buyer recently purchased clotting agents, bitter sedatives, surgical spirits, and jars of red mineral residue in bulk.

### Nira Clent

Human woman, 50s. Teacher, letter-reader, and chapel record keeper.

Nira teaches children their letters in the morning and writes death notices in the afternoon. She is patient but not soft.

**Public role:** Teacher and scribe.

**Secret:** She copied a partial manifest bearing an Amakiir-linked mineral mark before Joric Marn destroyed the original page.

## Trade, Labour, and Ordinary Town NPCs

### Garran Tallow

Human man, 40s. Smith and chain repairer at Cairnwell Yard.

Garran repairs ferry chains, lock tools, cart axles, hooks, knives, and dock brackets.

**Public role:** Smith and practical repairman.

**Secret:** He repaired a broken restraint buckle of unusually fine Crown steel last week. It had red dust packed into the hinge.

### Aedan Marric

Human man, 30s. Shipwright and barge carpenter.

Aedan works on low river barges, ferry planks, warehouse doors, and anything else that needs timber.

**Public role:** Carpenter and shipwright.

**Secret:** He saw fresh scrape marks on the underside of the Red Lockhouse water gate.

### Orwyn Breck

Human man, 50s. Owner of the Hook and Bell.

Orwyn knows which drink loosen tongues and which questions close them. He dislikes trouble but profits from it.

**Public role:** Innkeeper.

**Secret:** Veyra Sorn rents a small upstairs room under a false name.

### Sella Varrow

Human woman, 20s. Server at the Hook and Bell.

Sella is sharper than her employer and better at hearing conversations from across the room.

**Public role:** Tavern worker and rumour source.

**Secret:** She knows Rennic Sallow keeps a second ledger hidden under the floor at Vellum House.

### Branna Cale

Human woman, 60s. Baker and pie-seller.

Branna feeds dockhands, children, guards, and anyone who can pay in coin or useful gossip.

**Public role:** Food seller and local gossip hub.

**Secret:** She saw Tovin Rusk speaking with a cloaked stranger the day before Edrin washed ashore.

### Bram Odrick

Human man, 40s. Barge captain.

Bram moves goods along the river and claims he never remembers illegal cargo once it leaves his deck.

**Public role:** River trader.

**Secret:** He refused a job involving sealed jars of red powder and now worries someone else accepted it.

### Lerris Doon

Human man, 30s. Stable-hand and cart driver.

Lerris moves crates between Morfael and the outer roads to [[Caer Oryn]]. He is cheerful when sober and useful when frightened.

**Public role:** Driver and messenger.

**Secret:** He knows which warehouse carts avoid official inspection.

### Hesta Brinn

Human woman, 70s. Net-mender and grandmother figure.

Hesta sits near the market, repairs nets, and sees more than people expect.

**Public role:** Elderly local and informal witness.

**Secret:** She remembers another man with red dust in his clothes and dark veins under his skin being carried into the Red Lockhouse months ago.

## Criminal, Informant, and Hidden Faction NPCs

### Arden Rook

High-level note: [[Arden Rook]] is not normally present in Morfael at the opening. His influence reaches the town through Rennic Sallow and [[The Veiled Path]].

**Role in Morfael:** Off-screen contact, rumour source, possible later arrival.

**Use:** Arden gives Nakoa a reason to follow the relic-smuggling trail without making the opening feel like a direct quest from him.

### Rennic Sallow

Rennic appears above as an opening NPC, but he is also the most important criminal-adjacent local contact.

**Role in Morfael:** Broker between ordinary dock trade and hidden networks.

**Use:** Rennic can help the party hide, forge a cargo pass, identify a false ledger, or contact [[The Veiled Path]]. He also provides Nakoa with the first red-dust sample that reacts faintly to Nakoa’s relic-touched magic. His hidden courier notes overlap with Eira Lorne’s missing-person list, which is why Cassian and Nakoa already crossed paths before the chapel meeting.

### Veyra Sorn

Veyra appears above as an opening NPC, but she also acts as the first visible thread of the [[Silent Chamber]].

**Role in Morfael:** Observer, cleaner, witness-controller.

**Use:** If the players notice her, they gain an early route into Crown intelligence. If they do not, she becomes the reason the Crown learns their names.

### Mavren Holt

Human man, 40s. Pawnbroker and fence.

Mavren buys tools, rings, relic scraps, family heirlooms, and things that were definitely not stolen if no one asks.

**Public role:** Pawnbroker.

**Secret:** He briefly handled a gold-marked Veylrain fragment and sold it to a buyer using Crown coin.

### Corven Saye

Human man, age uncertain. Silent Chamber handler.

Corven should not appear openly at the start. He uses intermediaries and gives orders through coded trade requests.

**Public role:** Unknown.

**Secret:** He is Veyra Sorn’s handler and reports failed experiment sightings upward.

## PC Presence and Relationship Web

### The Last Lantern Inquiry

The easiest way to start the campaign is with **the Last Lantern Inquiry**. Sister Halva Dorn has quietly gathered the four player characters at the Chapel of the Last Lantern the night before [[Edrin Vaal]] washes ashore.

On the table are four pieces of evidence:

- A strip of blood-stiffened medical binding.
- A sealed pinch of red grit that leaves rust-coloured stains on cloth and stone.
- A copied fragment of a cargo manifest bearing an Amakiir-linked mineral mark.
- Eira Lorne’s list of missing riverfolk, marked with names, routes, dates, and last-known crossings.

This meeting gives the party a reason to stand near the Flood Steps the following morning without forcing them to already be close friends.

### [[Nakoa]]

Nakoa is present because [[Arden Rook]] or [[The Veiled Path]] has sent him toward Rennic Sallow and the Black Eel Market. His interest is relic traffic, red veinstone dust, and Veylrain fragments.

Rennic shows Nakoa a tiny smear of red dust wrapped in waxed paper. It is not the same as Nakoa’s black-and-gold relic, but it answers in the same hidden language. This gives Nakoa an active reason to inspect Morfael before Edrin appears.

Nakoa has been in Morfael for several days before the opening and already knows Cassian Clarke. Their first meeting was practical and tense: Cassian was following a missing courier’s human trail, while Nakoa was following the same person’s hidden cargo route. They each discovered the other had part of the truth.

**Knows beforehand:** Rennic Sallow by contact and Cassian Clarke through the missing courier lead. He may recognise Teclis and Alekthar from the chapel meeting, but does not truly know them yet.

### [[Alekthar Amakiir]]

Alekthar is present because an Amakiir-linked mineral mark has appeared on a suspicious Morfael manifest. Since the Night of Golden Tears, he has been responsible for the remnants of a diminished house: old contracts, failing holdings, inaccessible mines, and a family name that can still be misused by people who know where to look.

This is not just a rumour that sounds like his tragedy. It is evidence that someone may be exploiting his house’s old trade authority to move experimental material.

**Knows beforehand:** Teclis by formal inquiry or reputation. Cassian lightly from the chapel meeting. Nakoa as the relic-connected outsider Cassian has already encountered while tracing a missing courier.

### [[Teclis Nimule Sílorion]]

Teclis is present because [[Arch-Lector Meren Voss]] sends him from [[The Final Journey]] to investigate rumours of improper soul-binding, false resurrection, or heretical miracle-work in Morfael. Voss does not respect Teclis’ posting and likely treats this errand as a way to keep him occupied away from more important church matters.

**Knows beforehand:** Cassian as a grounded witness among the families, shrine-keepers, and riverfolk affected by recent disappearances. Alekthar by record, reputation, or a prior formal conversation regarding sensitive religious history. Nakoa only after Rennic and Cassian draw him into the Last Lantern Inquiry.

### [[Cassian Clarke]]

Cassian is present because his Selûnite missionary work has brought him to Morfael’s poor river shrines, ferrymen, missing-person families, and travellers. He has been helping Eira Lorne and Sister Halva with letters, blessings, comfort for widows, and rites for those who live their lives at thresholds: the road, the river, the night crossing, and the long journey home.

Cassian is not here because the sky did something dramatic. He is here because people are suffering, because Morfael is exactly the kind of place that needs a missionary who listens, and because a man washing ashore half-dead is a moral and spiritual crisis before it is a cosmic clue.

His active contribution before the fight is the missing-person list. Cassian can put names, routes, families, and grief behind what might otherwise look like a strange magical incident.

Cassian already knows Nakoa from tracing one of those missing names. He may not fully trust him, but he knows Nakoa is not simply preying on Morfael’s suffering. Nakoa has information about hidden routes and cargo movement that Cassian cannot get from grieving families alone.

**Knows beforehand:** Teclis from the inquiry, Nakoa from the missing courier lead, and Alekthar from the chapel meeting and shared concern over the evidence.
