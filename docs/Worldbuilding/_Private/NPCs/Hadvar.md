## Hadvar

_Private NPC file for Alekthar's campaign continuity._

Hadvar was the human husband of [Araenia](Araenia.md) and father to [Aaldar](Aaldar.md), [Aveline](Aveline.md), and [Avaris](Avaris.md). He died during the Night of Golden Tears.

Hadvar did not carry the Bound Radiance, but he was married into one of the branches most affected by it. Two of his children carried the inheritance and were targeted by the ritual. His youngest daughter, Avaris, survived.

## Relationship to Alekthar

Hadvar's death reinforces one of Alekthar's central wounds: the secret did not only kill those who carried it. It killed spouses, parents, servants, and relatives who were simply close enough to be destroyed.

## DM Use

Hadvar can be used through household records, letters to Araenia, or documents concerning Avaris' inheritance. He may have been one of the family spouses who knew very little but noticed enough to be frightened.

Linked PC: [Alekthar Amakiir](Alekthar%20Amakiir.md).
