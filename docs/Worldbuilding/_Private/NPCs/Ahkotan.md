## Ahkotan

_Private NPC file for Alekthar's campaign continuity._

Ahkotan Amakiir is Arnemia's younger brother, husband to [Catrina](Catrina.md), father to [Amalina](Amalina.md) and [Aldoran](Aldoran.md), and one of the seven survivors of the Night of Golden Tears. Unlike several other members of his generation, Ahkotan did not carry the Bound Radiance.

During the ritual's collapse, Ahkotan was hurled clear of the great hall through one of the large windows and onto the outside terrace. He saw the explosion of golden light touch the sky.

## Aftermath

Ahkotan became one of the practical pillars of House Amakiir after the massacre. He helped Alekthar manage the decline of the house, preserve what remained of the estate, negotiate obligations, handle accounts, and keep Cael Tharos functioning after the mines failed.

When Alekthar eventually left the Tharos Vale, stewardship passed to Ahkotan and Catrina. They continued to care for the surviving younger generation: Amalina, [Astaavi](Astaavi.md), and [Avaris](Avaris.md).

## Relationship to Alekthar

Ahkotan is one of Alekthar's few living anchors. He is not merely an uncle. He is the man who helped teach Alekthar how to preserve a dying noble house without pretending it was still whole.

## DM Use

Ahkotan can send letters, warnings, estate updates, old records, requests for help, or news that something has changed in the Tharos Vale. If Cael Tharos becomes threatened again, Ahkotan is the person most likely to contact Alekthar directly.
