## Obari

_Private NPC file for Alekthar's campaign continuity._

Obari is Alekthar Amakiir's human father, the widower of [Arnemia](Arnemia.md), and one of the seven surviving family members of the Night of Golden Tears. He later disappeared from Cael Tharos four years after the massacre.

Obari married into House Amakiir later in life. He was not noble by Amakiir blood and carried no celestial inheritance, but he was educated, disciplined, observant, and careful with his words. Before marriage, he served in the Vermillion Crown's security apparatus. He described that service as dull work involving ledgers, travel permits, border reports, courier routes, and administrative details. This was almost certainly a deliberate understatement.

Obari taught Alekthar that faith was not the same as certainty, and that a person could live honourably even when the world denied clean answers. He also recognised that Alekthar's mind would not rest until satisfied with an answer.

## The Night of Golden Tears

Obari suspected danger before Amari's Continuance. He was unsettled by Akaavi's decision to hire unfamiliar servants and by the illness spreading through the servant quarters. His fears were correct, but he did not know what he was truly protecting. During the ritual, [The Horned Tiefling](The%20Horned%20Tiefling.md) addressed him as **Inquisitor**, exposing that Obari's former service had been more serious than he admitted.

Obari survived physically, but after the explosion he became unreachable. He stared at spaces no one else could see, moved when guided, ate when reminded, and sometimes gripped Alekthar's wrist as if trying to warn him.

## Disappearance

Four years after the massacre, Obari left Cael Tharos before dawn. A horse was missing, and a gatekeeper later admitted that Obari passed through the north road wrapped in a travelling cloak and carrying an old leather case Alekthar did not recognise.

Alekthar wrote to Obari's former contacts. Most did not respond. One reply from Caer Oryn advised him to stop asking after matters connected to his father's former service.

## DM Use

Obari is a major campaign thread. He may be alive and following the cult's trail. He may be compromised by what he saw during the ritual. He may have recovered a hidden memory. He may have been part of an order that knew about the Bound Radiance but not its full purpose. His old case may contain maps, identities, sealed orders, evidence, or a tool for finding the Horned Tiefling.

If Obari appears, he should not be reduced to a helpless victim. He was broken by the ritual, but the fact that he left by choice suggests that something in him returned.
