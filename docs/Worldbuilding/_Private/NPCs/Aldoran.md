## Aldoran

_Private NPC file for Alekthar's campaign continuity._

Aldoran Amakiir was the son of [Ahkotan](Ahkotan.md) and [Catrina](Catrina.md), and brother of [Amalina](Amalina.md). He died during the Night of Golden Tears.

Aldoran is not recorded as a bearer of the Bound Radiance. Like several others, he died because he was present at Cael Tharos when the secret violence around the Continuance unfolded.

## Relationship to Alekthar

Aldoran's death matters because it keeps Ahkotan and Catrina from being merely practical survivors. They are grieving parents as well as stewards. Their help to Alekthar came from people who were carrying their own loss.

## DM Use

Aldoran can be used to deepen scenes involving Ahkotan, Catrina, and Amalina. Any memorial, keepsake, or room preserved at Cael Tharos can remind Alekthar that the surviving stewards paid heavily and still chose duty.
