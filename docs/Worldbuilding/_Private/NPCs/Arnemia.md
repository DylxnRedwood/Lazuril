## Arnemia

_Private NPC file for Alekthar's campaign continuity._

Lady Arnemia Amakiir was Alekthar's mother, [Obari](Obari.md)'s wife, [Amari](Amari.md)'s mother, and one of the senior living bearers of the Bound Radiance before the Night of Golden Tears. She died during the failed ritual at Cael Tharos.

Arnemia was composed, devout without being narrow, and fiercely protective of House Amakiir's privacy. She loved both her children, but she accepted the family's old division between those who carried the secret and those who did not. Amari was prepared for the Continuance. Alekthar was deliberately kept innocent of the burden.

Her most important emotional line to Alekthar is that she apologised for excluding him while still refusing to break the rule: this was the way it had always been, and the way it would remain.

## Bound Radiance

Arnemia carried the Bound Radiance and understood at least part of the family's ancient duty. Her pendant, recovered from her ashes, is now one of Alekthar's key personal relics. It may contain a hidden mark, memory, protective working, bloodline recognition, or a fragment of instructional symbolism.

## Relationship to Alekthar

Alekthar loved Arnemia, but he also blames her. His anger is not simple hatred. He believes that if she had told Obari the truth, Obari might have stopped the infiltration or understood the danger in time. Arnemia's secrecy is therefore one of the emotional roots of Alekthar's vow to find the truth.

## DM Use

Arnemia should be used as a source of painful ambiguity. She may have been right to keep Alekthar ignorant. She may have been following rules whose purpose had been forgotten. She may have known more than she could safely explain. Any letter, locket, pendant inscription, or private prayer from Arnemia should feel like love and failure in the same breath.
