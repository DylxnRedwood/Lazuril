## Riven

_Private NPC working page for Riven. This page supports [Nakoa](Nakoa.md), Veylrath monster tracking, the Vurokai borderlands, and the twin hunter dynamic with [Suri](Suri.md)._

Riven is one of [Nakoa](Nakoa.md)'s younger twin siblings. Together with [Suri](Suri.md), he has become known locally as a capable monster tracker, moving through rainforest trails, river margins, and savannah borderlands where the Veylrain Expanse thins toward Vurokai.

Riven and Suri are respected because they do work that lets other people sleep. They track predators, drive off threats, study signs of monster movement, and investigate the strange disturbances that sometimes rise after heavy rain exposes old ground.

## Role

Riven is a tracker, hunter, scout, and protector. Where Suri is likely to pause and read the whole trail, Riven may be quicker to act once a pattern becomes clear. He is not reckless, but he trusts his instincts and physical competence.

He understands spoor, scent, sound, ambush points, wind direction, disturbed mud, broken branches, drag marks, and the behaviour of frightened prey. He likely prefers decisive movement over long debate.

## Relationship to Nakoa

Riven may find Nakoa entertaining, infuriating, and worrying in equal measure. He likely enjoys Nakoa's stories more than Kaelo does, but he also knows how easily curiosity can become a trail of blood.

If Riven learns about the relic incident, he may approach it as a hunter first. Did the relic leave anything behind? Did something follow Nakoa? Does the magic have a scent, sound, rhythm, or behavioural pattern? Does Nakoa move like himself, or has something else changed beneath the obvious signs?

Riven is a useful sibling because he can be blunt without being cold. He may say the thing others avoid: that Nakoa touched something stupid and now the whole family may have to track the consequences.

## Personality

Riven is direct, physically confident, and more likely to act than theorise. He is not unintelligent. His intelligence is practical, immediate, and tested in movement rather than conversation.

He is close to Suri, and the two should feel like an experienced pair who can communicate with small gestures, glances, and half-finished phrases. Riven may be the louder of the two, but he respects Suri's judgement deeply.

## Use in Play

Riven can provide tracking support, physical backup, monster knowledge, and family pressure. He is especially useful if danger approaches Tethvale, Vurokai, or the routes between settlements.

He can also be a strong witness to Nakoa's changed presence. Hunters notice what scholars miss. If animals react strangely to Nakoa, if predators avoid his trail, or if old ruin-things follow his scent, Riven may be among the first to notice.

## Secrets and Future Development

Riven may have found signs near the relic ruin that he has not yet connected to Nakoa.

Riven may be tempted to hunt whatever changed his brother, even if the target cannot be hunted in any ordinary way.

Riven may become reckless if he believes Nakoa is being used as bait by something ancient.

Riven may eventually recognise a monster sign that mirrors the golden branching in Nakoa's markings.
