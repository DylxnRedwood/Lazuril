## Aaldar

_Private NPC file for Alekthar's campaign continuity._

Aaldar Amakiir was an Aasimar son of [Araenia](Araenia.md) and [Hadvar](Hadvar.md), brother to [Aveline](Aveline.md) and [Avaris](Avaris.md). He carried the Bound Radiance and died during the Night of Golden Tears.

Aaldar was one of the young bearers whose existence helped continue the distributed inheritance of House Amakiir. His death further broke the chain of instruction and succession.

## Bound Radiance

Aaldar was one of the ten bearers targeted by [The Horned Tiefling](The%20Horned%20Tiefling.md). His blood and light were drawn into the ritual before Amari's uncontrolled Radiance overwhelmed the working.

## Relationship to Alekthar

Aaldar belongs to the branch that left only [Avaris](Avaris.md) alive. His death gives Avaris a grief that parallels Astaavi's: survival as the youngest remnant of a nearly erased branch.

## DM Use

Aaldar can be used through bearer records, seating diagrams, or family instruction lists. His training may contain clues about what the male bearers were expected to guard, endure, or recognise during Continuance.
