# Edrin Vaal

> [!dm] DM-Only NPC  
> Edrin Vaal is the missing courier, the man in the river, and the first proof that Veylrain relic-smuggling, House Amakiir cargo fraud, and Crown veinstone experimentation are connected.

## Overview

Edrin Vaal was a human courier based in [[Morfael]], moving relics smuggled from the [[Veylrain Expanse]] into [[Caer Oryn]]. His final job used falsified [[House Amakiir]] cargo manifests to disguise relic fragments and veinstone dust as legitimate mineral freight.

At the end of the job, secret Crown agents seized him in Caer Oryn. He was taken by the [[Crown Scar Directorate]], experimented on as **Subject-V-Augment-03**, then disposed of into the [[River Oryn]] when the trial failed.

His body washes up at the Flood Steps beneath the Black Eel Market. When [[Nakoa]] comes close, the veinstone dust inside Edrin responds to Nakoa’s own altered relic-state. Edrin resurrects violently, overwhelmed by traumatic visions of the smuggling route, the Crown laboratory, the river, and the red stone forced into his body.

## Description Before Capture

Edrin was a lean human man of about 34, roughly 5 ft 10, wiry from years of dock work, ferry crossings, and courier runs. He had weathered olive-brown skin, short dark brown hair, hazel-green eyes, a narrow face, and an old split scar across his lower lip.

He dressed like a practical river courier: waxed boots, dark wool layers, a patched river cloak, fingerless gloves, and a reinforced satchel with hidden stitching. He looked forgettable by design, the sort of man who could pass through customs, markets, ferry points, and warehouse yards without being remembered.

He was careful, nervous, observant, and good at appearing harmless. He was not heroic, but he was not cruel. He told himself he was only moving cargo, even when the cargo began to stain his hands red.

## Description After the Experiment

When found in the river, Edrin looks dead but unfinished. His skin is cold, grey, and waterlogged. Red dust clings beneath his nails, around his teeth, and inside old puncture wounds. Dark vein-like cracks run under his skin, especially across his throat, ribs, wrists, and chest.

His wrists, ankles, shoulders, and ribs carry deep restraint marks. His torso bears surgical scars and red-stained injection sites. Near his left collarbone is a partial experimental mark: **V-AUG-03**.

After resurrection, his eyes open bloodshot and wide, with faint red-gold pinpricks in the pupils. Gold lines flicker beneath his skin like broken writing. He coughs red dust, claws at invisible restraints, and mistakes dockworkers, party members, and lantern-bearers for Crown handlers.

He is frightening, but he is not the villain. He is a traumatised man trapped inside a failed experiment.

## Residence in Morfael

Edrin kept a rented room above a net-mender’s loft on a narrow lane behind the [[Black Eel Market]]. Locals know it as **Vaal’s Room**, though the building itself is usually called **The Gullhook Loft**.

The room is cramped, damp, and deliberately plain. It contains a rope bed, a small writing board, wax scraps, spare courier tags, old ferry tallies, and a hidden floorboard beneath a loose crate.

The room can be found through [[Rennic Sallow]], [[Eira Lorne]], dock gossip, or papers recovered from Edrin’s body.

## The Amakiir Seal

Hidden beneath the floorboard in Edrin’s room is a small brass cargo seal bearing a House Amakiir trade mark. It is not a noble signet and does not prove direct family involvement. It is a cargo seal used to stamp mineral manifests, freight wrappings, and warehouse receipts.

The seal has been used recently. Red dust is trapped in its grooves.

This is the clue that reveals Edrin’s smuggling route was disguised under House Amakiir cargo authority. To [[Alekthar Amakiir]], the seal should feel deeply wrong. Someone has been using his family’s diminished legitimacy as cover for veinstone movement.

Possible nearby clues include false mineral manifests, scraps marked “stone meal”, Veylrain wrapping cloth, ferry marks, and a half-burned route note pointing upriver toward Caer Oryn.

## What Edrin Knows

Edrin does not understand the full conspiracy. He knows enough to be dangerous.

He knows relics came from the Veylrain Expanse. He knows the Black Eel Market and Morfael ferry routes were used. He knows House Amakiir cargo papers hid the shipments. He knows Crown agents took him after the final delivery. He remembers the designation **Subject-V-Augment-03**. He remembers red stone being ground into his body.

He does not know why Nakoa woke him. He does not know the full purpose of the Crown Scar Directorate. He does not know who authorised the work.

## Use in Session

Edrin should begin as horror, then become evidence, then become tragedy.

His panic can produce broken phrases:

“No more chains.”

“I was sealed before your gods had names.”

“I hear the bindings thinning.”

“I wore the world as a shackle.”

“I am the wound beneath the stone.”

If stabilised, Edrin gives fragments, not exposition. If killed, his body remains evidence. If seized by the Crown, the party learns the Crown wanted him badly enough to recover him in public.

Edrin is guilty enough to complicate sympathy and innocent enough to make the Crown monstrous.
