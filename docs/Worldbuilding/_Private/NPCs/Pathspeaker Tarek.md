## Pathspeaker Tarek

_Private NPC working page for Pathspeaker Tarek. This page supports [Nakoa](Nakoa.md), Tethvale, river navigation, clan trade routes, and family obligations._

Pathspeaker Tarek is [Nakoa](Nakoa.md)'s father, a respected trader, navigator, and negotiator in Tethvale. His title reflects more than simple travel knowledge. Tarek is responsible for maintaining safe movement through dangerous rainforest waterways, shifting river bends, flood-season canopy routes, family obligations, settlement permissions, and those old places that sensible people avoid unless there is no other choice.

Tarek knows the Veylrain Expanse as a living system. He knows which river bends shift after heavy rain, which canopy paths can take a loaded party during flood season, which families honour passage, which crossings require gifts, and which routes should be left unspoken around outsiders.

## Role in Tethvale

Tethvale depends on people like Tarek. By human city standards it may be small, but by Veylrath standards it is a major river-trade and navigation settlement. Trade is not merely commerce there. It is medicine moving before fever spreads, warnings travelling before predators arrive, families meeting before disputes harden, and routes being maintained before the jungle takes them back.

Tarek is respected because he is reliable. He does not treat navigation as personal brilliance. He treats it as duty. A wrong route can kill people. A careless promise can endanger a settlement. A hidden hazard ignored for convenience can become someone else's funeral.

## Family

Tarek is father to [Kaelo](Kaelo.md), [Nakoa](Nakoa.md), [Suri](Suri.md), [Riven](Riven.md), and [Enzo](Enzo.md). He is partnered with [Amara](Amara.md), whose background as a relic appraiser and historian gives the family a strong respect for old objects, old records, and old warnings.

Tarek likely expected at least some of his children to remain tied to routes, protection, trade, or settlement duty. Kaelo has done so most clearly. Suri and Riven serve the region through tracking and monster work. Enzo preserves family reputation in a louder, less reliable way. Nakoa is the difficult one: gifted, loved, useful, and almost impossible to keep pointed at safe work for long.

## Relationship to Nakoa

Tarek loves Nakoa, but love does not make him blind. He has likely spent years watching Nakoa's curiosity deepen from harmless mischief into something more dangerous. He understands the value of cleverness, but he also knows the rainforest has no respect for clever people who refuse to listen.

The relic incident would concern him deeply if he knew the full truth. Tarek's instinct may be to protect his son, but his position and worldview would also push him toward proper clan process. If Nakoa has broken ruin law, mishandled a relic, or brought an unknown magical alteration back into the family line, Tarek cannot simply pretend it is private.

This tension is useful. Tarek can be both loving and severe. He can want Nakoa safe and still insist that the matter must be brought before elders, appraisers, shamans, or ruin-wardens.

## Personality

Tarek is steady, practical, and difficult to rush. He measures people by whether they honour passage, keep promises, and understand the difference between boldness and carelessness.

He is not without humour, but his humour is dry and often aimed at preventing panic. He has the temperament of someone who has guided frightened travellers through bad water before and knows that shouting rarely improves a route.

When angry, he becomes quieter rather than louder.

## Use in Play

Tarek can provide family pressure, route knowledge, settlement context, trade contacts, local authority, and a grounded Veylrath perspective on law. He can also be used to remind Nakoa that consequences travel home faster than he expects.

He should not be portrayed as a simple obstacle. His caution is earned. If Tarek says a route is unsafe or a ruin should not be approached, the reason should be real.

## Secrets and Future Development

Tarek may already suspect that Nakoa has concealed something serious.

Tarek may know old route names connected to the ruin where Nakoa found the relic.

Tarek may have made past agreements with ruin-wardens that limit what he can hide for his family.

Tarek may eventually have to choose between acting as father and acting as pathspeaker.
