## Miriam

_Private NPC file for Alekthar's campaign continuity._

Miriam was the human wife of [Akaavi](Akaavi.md) and mother to [Aldric](Aldric.md), [Aliana](Aliana.md), [Alysse](Alysse.md), [Aleron](Aleron.md), and [Astaavi](Astaavi.md). She died during the Night of Golden Tears.

Miriam did not carry the Bound Radiance, but she was part of the inner household by marriage and would have understood the emotional weight of Amari's Continuance even if she did not know its full truth. Her children included several bearers, which placed her at the edge of the family's hidden burden.

## Relationship to Alekthar

Miriam's death is part of the wider injustice of the massacre. She was not a target because of a secret power inside her, but because she belonged to a family that kept dangerous secrets. Her youngest son, [Astaavi](Astaavi.md), survived and now carries the grief of being the only surviving child of his branch.

## DM Use

Miriam can be used to humanise Akaavi's branch of the family. Letters from her to relatives, household notes, or nursery records may show that she feared the pressure placed on her children. She may also have noticed something about Akaavi's hired staff before the attack.

Linked PC: [Alekthar Amakiir](Alekthar%20Amakiir.md).
