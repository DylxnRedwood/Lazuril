## Talu

_Private NPC working page for Talu. This page supports [Nakoa](Nakoa.md), Veylrath quiet carrying, clan law, and legal movement at the edge of smuggling._

Talu is a Tabaxi courier from the Veylrath Clans and one of [Nakoa](Nakoa.md)'s closest childhood friends. Among outsiders he might easily be called a smuggler, and he does very little to discourage the romance of that reputation. Within Veylrath law, however, his work is better understood as **quiet carrying**.

Quiet carrying is the clan-recognised movement of goods, letters, medicines, warnings, and sometimes people without announcing every burden to foreign traders, rival families, patrols, or hostile powers along the route. It is legal only while it serves clan law. Cargo may be hidden from outsiders, but not from the Canopy Concord, relevant elders, or ruin-wardens where relics, poisons, dangerous texts, cursed objects, or unstable goods are involved.

Talu understands that line.

He simply enjoys walking as close to it as possible.

## Role

Talu is a runner, courier, route finder, favour broker, and discreet carrier. He knows canopy paths, river timings, seasonal crossing points, informal tolls, family obligations, and which questions should be asked aloud only after the wrong people have left.

He is not a criminal in Veylrath terms, at least not by default. He operates in the space between public trade and private necessity. He may carry medicine without alerting raiders, letters without alerting rival families, warnings without causing panic, or people without giving foreign authorities an easy trail.

The important distinction is that Talu's secrecy is meant to protect clan interests, not undermine them. If he hides cargo from the Canopy Concord or lies to ruin-wardens about relics, he has crossed from quiet carrying into crime.

That line should matter in play.

## Relationship to Nakoa

Talu and Nakoa grew up around the same routes, rumours, bridges, river-stairs, and adults who could tell from a single glance when children were preparing to do something stupid.

Talu knows Nakoa's curiosity is not an act. He has likely enabled it more than once, whether by carrying a message, looking the other way, finding a route, or loudly claiming he knew nothing whatsoever about where Nakoa had gone.

He is also one of the people best placed to notice that Nakoa has changed since the relic incident. Talu may see the golden flecks in Nakoa's markings, notice strange objects moving near him, or hear rumours before they reach the family.

## Personality

Talu is quick-tongued, practical, and fond of making lawful work sound more dangerous than it is. He likes being underestimated by people who think law and secrecy cannot occupy the same path.

He is loyal, but not obedient. He enjoys edge-cases, loopholes, coded phrases, and the little theatre of pretending he is more scandalous than he is. Underneath that performance, he understands consequences. A quiet carrier who truly ignores clan law does not stay free for long.

Talu should feel like someone who knows exactly which rules can bend and which ones break bones.

## Use in Play

Talu can provide routes, rumours, movement, discreet deliveries, clan gossip, warnings, contacts, and access to information that would not pass through formal channels. He can also bring trouble to Nakoa's door by carrying something that seems minor but is not.

He is a good source for messages from Tethvale, hints of Veylrath concern, hidden movement of relic fragments, or early signs that foreign powers are using clan routes badly.

Talu can also pressure Nakoa by asking favours. A quiet carrier's work depends on trust, and Nakoa's new connection to strange magic may make him useful in ways Talu is tempted to exploit.

## Secrets and Future Development

Talu may have carried a warning connected to the ruin Nakoa entered and failed to understand its importance.

Talu may be aware of Veiled Path route signs without knowing their full origin.

Talu may be under obligation to an elder, route-warden, or family whose interests conflict with Nakoa's curiosity.

Talu may eventually be forced to choose between protecting Nakoa and reporting dangerous relic-related movement under clan law.
