## Akaavi

_Private NPC file for Alekthar's campaign continuity._

Akaavi Amakiir was Arnemia's brother, Miriam's husband, and father to [Aldric](Aldric.md), [Aliana](Aliana.md), [Alysse](Alysse.md), [Aleron](Aleron.md), and [Astaavi](Astaavi.md). He carried the Bound Radiance and died during the Night of Golden Tears.

Akaavi was proud, extravagant, and eager to be seen as important within House Amakiir. He organised Amari's Continuance celebration and boasted that it would be the grandest celebration ever hosted in the house. He personally paid for additional servants to make the event suitably impressive.

That decision became the opening through which the cult entered Cael Tharos.

## The Night of Golden Tears

[The Horned Tiefling](The%20Horned%20Tiefling.md) humiliated Akaavi in front of the family, calling him pompous, drunken, and responsible for the end of the legendary Amakiir line. Akaavi's guilt should be treated as real, but not complete. He was foolish and vain, but he did not knowingly betray the family.

Akaavi's blood was drawn into the ritual as one of the bearers of the Bound Radiance. He died hollowed by celestial fire.

## Relationship to Alekthar

Alekthar's memory of Akaavi is likely poisoned by what happened. Akaavi was family, and he was not evil, but his pride helped create the conditions for the massacre. This makes him a difficult figure for Alekthar to mourn cleanly.

## DM Use

Akaavi can be used through debts, receipts, servant contracts, guest lists, or correspondence from the hired labour network. The list of new servants may be one of the most important surviving clues to the cult's infiltration.
