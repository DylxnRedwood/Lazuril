## Suri

_Private NPC working page for Suri. This page supports [Nakoa](Nakoa.md), Veylrath monster tracking, the Vurokai borderlands, and the twin hunter dynamic with [Riven](Riven.md)._

Suri is one of [Nakoa](Nakoa.md)'s younger twin siblings. Together with [Riven](Riven.md), she has become known locally as a capable monster tracker, working through rainforest trails, river margins, and savannah borderlands where the Veylrain Expanse thins toward Vurokai.

Suri and Riven are respected because their work is practical and dangerous. They protect settlements from predators, displaced beasts, and older things that sometimes wander out of ruined ground after heavy rain.

## Role

Suri is part scout, part hunter, part path-reader, and part local protector. Her work depends on patience, observation, discipline, and the ability to recognise when a trail belongs to an ordinary animal, a hungry predator, a wounded monster, or something stranger.

She is likely the more measured of the twins, or at least the one who prefers to understand a threat before committing to the kill. She reads broken foliage, mud pressure, disturbed insects, claw marks, scat, silence, and the sudden absence of normal birdsong.

## Relationship to Nakoa

Suri may admire Nakoa's courage while having very little patience for his lack of caution. She knows the difference between entering danger because people need protection and entering danger because curiosity has started purring.

If she learns about the black-and-gold feline relic, her first instinct may be threat assessment. What changed? Can it be tracked? Did anything follow him? Does his scent, shadow, or movement feel wrong? Has the jungle reacted to him differently?

Suri can challenge Nakoa in a way scholars cannot. She may not know what the relic was, but she knows when something has been marked.

## Personality

Suri is focused, dry, and more direct than Nakoa. She is not humourless, but her humour often lands after the danger has passed. She respects skill, preparation, and people who understand that fear is information rather than weakness.

She is close to Riven, but they should not be identical. Suri is the one more likely to pause, listen, and ask what the trail is trying to hide.

## Use in Play

Suri can provide monster lore, tracking support, local warnings, physical protection, and a grounded hunter's view of Nakoa's magical alteration. She can also bring news from the Vurokai side of the Expanse, especially if beasts or ruined places are behaving strangely.

She is useful when the campaign needs someone who can read the land physically rather than academically or magically.

## Secrets and Future Development

Suri may have tracked something near the ruin after Nakoa's collapse.

Suri may suspect that the relic did not vanish cleanly, but left a trail through Nakoa.

Suri may be more afraid for Nakoa than she is willing to show.

Suri may eventually recognise that something hunting the party is following Nakoa specifically.
