## Enzo

_Private NPC working page for Enzo. This page supports [Nakoa](Nakoa.md), family continuity, Tethvale storytelling, and rumour movement through performance._

Enzo is [Nakoa](Nakoa.md)'s youngest sibling, a self-styled bard, storyteller, exaggerator, and enthusiastic keeper of family legend. He is never happier than when inflating the exploits of his siblings to anyone willing to listen, especially when the siblings in question are absent and therefore unable to correct him.

Enzo may not yet be the great bard he imagines himself to be, but he is socially useful in a way the family should not underestimate. Stories travel faster than cargo, and Enzo knows how to make people repeat things.

## Role in Tethvale

In Tethvale, Enzo is likely known as a lively presence around river platforms, trade gatherings, common meals, and returning boats. He listens to travellers, repeats rumours, reshapes family incidents into better stories, and treats every sibling achievement as material waiting for improvement.

His storytelling is comic on the surface, but it can become important. Enzo may hear rumours before formal authorities do. He may know which version of an event people believe. He may accidentally spread something useful or dangerous.

## Relationship to Nakoa

Enzo looks up to Nakoa in the way younger siblings often admire the person most likely to do something unwise and interesting. To Enzo, Nakoa is not merely a brother. He is a walking story: sneaking into forbidden places, learning from a mysterious scholar, travelling farther from home, and now perhaps carrying a secret that makes the old tales feel awake.

This admiration can be dangerous. If Enzo learns too much about the relic incident, he may struggle to keep it quiet. Not because he means harm, but because a secret shaped like that is exactly the kind of thing he wants to turn into myth.

Nakoa may need to protect Enzo from the story he creates around him.

## Personality

Enzo is bright, dramatic, affectionate, and difficult to discourage. He enjoys applause, gasps, interruptions, and the moment when a listener asks what happened next.

He is not necessarily foolish. He understands people through reaction, mood, and rhythm. He may be less useful in a ruin than his siblings, but in a crowded settlement he can read attention better than most.

His weakness is embellishment. He does not always understand when exaggerating a story changes the stakes for people living inside it.

## Use in Play

Enzo can provide rumours, emotional levity, family warmth, and unexpected social information. He can also create problems by repeating a distorted version of events.

He is useful when the campaign needs information moving informally through Tethvale or the Veylrath settlements. A rumour about golden markings, a vanished relic, a strange scholar, or Nakoa's new magic could easily pass through Enzo's performance before anyone realises the danger.

## Secrets and Future Development

Enzo may already suspect Nakoa has become part of a story larger than ordinary family trouble.

Enzo may invent a tale about the black-and-gold feline relic that accidentally preserves a real clue from older oral tradition.

Enzo may be approached by someone seeking information about Nakoa because he talks too freely.

Enzo may eventually need to learn the difference between honouring a story and endangering the people inside it.
