## Amari

_Private NPC file for Alekthar's campaign continuity._

Amari Amakiir was Alekthar's younger sister, daughter of [Arnemia](Arnemia.md) and [Obari](Obari.md), and a bearer of the Bound Radiance. She was the intended subject of the Continuance on the Night of Golden Tears. She died, vanished, or transformed when the failed ritual collapsed.

Amari was kind, bright, and uncomfortable with the attention placed upon her. She had not asked for private lessons, old retainers' reverence, long hours with [Aiemi](Aiemi.md), or the way Arnemia looked at her with both pride and grief. Alekthar loved her deeply and did not resent her personally. He resented that she was burdened while he was excluded.

## The Incomplete Continuance

Amari's Continuance had not been completed when [The Horned Tiefling](The%20Horned%20Tiefling.md) attempted to harvest the Bound Radiance from the family. This was the ritual's fatal instability.

When the blood of the ten bearers reached the central nexus, the gathered Radiance did not remain under the cult's control. Amari drew it back into herself. She grew brighter as the others dimmed. She did not control it. She became the centre of a gold and red storm, and the stolen light burned through the family.

Alekthar's final sight of her was as something divine, terrified, and unbearably bright. Then she became Light, and Light exploded.

## Status

Amari is publicly dead. Privately, her fate should remain open. She may have been consumed, transfigured, scattered, imprisoned within the bindings, remembered by the Radiance, or used as a living fracture in an old seal. Alekthar does not know whether to mourn her as dead or fear that some part of her remains.

## Relationship to Alekthar

Amari is the emotional core of Alekthar's story. He saw her as sister, victim, destroyer, and miracle in the same moment. His question is not only who killed House Amakiir. It is what happened to Amari, and whether the power that spoke through the ritual used her.

## DM Use

Amari can appear as visions, echoes, golden light, distorted memory, a voice within binding mechanisms, or evidence that the Bound Radiance did not vanish cleanly. Do not answer her fate too early. The uncertainty is one of Alekthar's strongest levers.
