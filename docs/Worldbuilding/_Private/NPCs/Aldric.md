## Aldric

_Private NPC file for Alekthar's campaign continuity._

Aldric Amakiir was an Aasimar son of [Akaavi](Akaavi.md) and [Miriam](Miriam.md). He carried the Bound Radiance and died during the Night of Golden Tears.

As one of Akaavi's children, Aldric belonged to the family branch most directly tied to the pride and failure that opened the Continuance celebration to infiltration. He was not responsible for that failure, but he died inside its consequences.

## Bound Radiance

Aldric was one of the ten bearers targeted by [The Horned Tiefling](The%20Horned%20Tiefling.md). His blood was drawn into the ritual circle and his inner light was extracted before the ritual collapsed.

## Relationship to Alekthar

Aldric's death contributes to the near-erasure of Akaavi and Miriam's children. Of that branch, only [Astaavi](Astaavi.md) survived. Alekthar may remember Aldric as one of the relatives placed on the other side of the great hall's invisible divide: one of the chosen, one of the burdened, one of the dead.

## DM Use

Aldric can be used in family trees marked with gold ink, training records, or fragments of Continuance preparation. If the bearers had different strengths or aspects of the Radiance, Aldric can represent one of those lost facets.

Linked PC: [Alekthar Amakiir](Alekthar%20Amakiir.md).
