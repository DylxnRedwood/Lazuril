## Aleron

_Private NPC file for Alekthar's campaign continuity._

Aleron Amakiir was an Aasimar son of [Akaavi](Akaavi.md) and [Miriam](Miriam.md). He carried the Bound Radiance and died during the Night of Golden Tears.

Aleron was among the younger generation whose private preparation helped convince Alekthar that the family was divided by something more important than age or status. He was one of the chosen, and that made him one of the targets.

## Bound Radiance

Aleron was one of the ten bearers whose blood was drawn into the ritual circle. His light was part of the power that gathered around Amari before the ritual failed and exploded.

## Relationship to Alekthar

Aleron can stand for the lost future of House Amakiir. With so many young bearers dead, the family did not merely lose people. It lost continuity, instruction, memory, and whatever distributed role the bearers were meant to serve.

## DM Use

Aleron's name may appear in training rosters, bloodline diagrams, or ritual seating plans. If Alekthar reconstructs the order in which bearers were positioned in the hall, Aleron's placement may matter.
