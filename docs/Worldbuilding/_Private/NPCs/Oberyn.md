## Oberyn

_Private NPC file for Alekthar's campaign continuity._

Oberyn was Alekthar Amakiir's human grandfather and the husband of [Aiemi](Aiemi.md). He married into the old Arkborne Aasimar line of House Amakiir and spent much of his later life acting as the house's representative among noble families, Crown officials, contract holders, and charter authorities.

In youth, Oberyn was remembered as a brave and capable fighter. In age, he still carried himself with discipline and strength. [Obari](Obari.md) described him as one of the finest men he knew, which should matter because Obari was not given to careless praise.

Oberyn died during the Night of Golden Tears. He was not a bearer of the Bound Radiance, but he was killed by the violence unleashed when the ritual collapsed and the great hall of Cael Tharos shattered.

## Relationship to Alekthar

Alekthar did not know Oberyn as closely as he might have wished. Oberyn's duties often kept him away from the estate, renewing contracts and preserving the external legitimacy of House Amakiir. To Alekthar, he represented the outward-facing dignity of the family.

His death adds another layer to Alekthar's bitterness. Even those who did not carry the secret still died for being near it.

## DM Use

Oberyn can be used through old correspondence, contract ledgers, military contacts, retired comrades, or Crown documents. He may have known which officials watched the Tharos mines most closely, even if he did not know the full truth of the Bound Radiance.
