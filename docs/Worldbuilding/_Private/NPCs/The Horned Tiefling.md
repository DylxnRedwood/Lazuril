## The Horned Tiefling

_Private NPC file for Alekthar's campaign continuity._

The Horned Tiefling is the unknown red Tiefling who led the ritual attack on House Amakiir during the Night of Golden Tears. His true name, allegiance, patron, and current fate are unknown.

He appeared in Cael Tharos under the cover of servants hired for Amari's Continuance celebration. He demonstrated paralysis or compulsion magic, communicated directly into Alekthar's mind, recognised that not all Aasimar of the Amakiir line carried the Bound Radiance, and addressed [Obari](Obari.md) as **Inquisitor**. This means he possessed intelligence on both House Amakiir and Obari's concealed past.

## Personality and Presentation

The Horned Tiefling was theatrical, cruel, and deeply prepared. He taunted the family, humiliated [Akaavi](Akaavi.md), and treated the massacre as both revenge and performance. Once the ritual began, his mockery stopped and he became purposeful. This distinction matters. He is not only a sadist. He is a ritualist with doctrine, grievance, and a clear objective.

His ritual chant reframed the Bound Radiance as an old wrong. His language suggests that he, or the force behind him, believed House Amakiir had bound something that should not have been bound.

## The Ritual

The Horned Tiefling used blood, powdered red stone, a runecarved bowl, cultists' blood, and a crimson containment field to harvest or extinguish the Bound Radiance. He expected the bearers' power to be drawn into the ritual nexus. He did not expect [Amari](Amari.md)'s incomplete Continuance to reverse the flow.

When Amari drew the Radiance into herself, the Tiefling was stunned and then frightened. This is important evidence that Amari's reaction was not part of his plan.

## Status

No confirmed body was found. The Tiefling and his cultists may have been consumed, buried, transported, shielded, or deliberately extracted by the same force they served. Alekthar's uncertainty about this should remain active.

## DM Use

The Horned Tiefling is Alekthar's most obvious antagonist thread, but he should not be treated as the final answer. He is a doorway into a deeper conflict over the bindings, the Bound Radiance, the Godscar, and the old forces beneath Lazuril.

Potential roles:

- Surviving ritual leader seeking a second attempt.
- Dead cultist whose records reveal the patron behind the attack.
- Warlock, priest, or chosen agent of the imprisoned old god.
- Former inquisitorial target who recognised Obari from past investigations.
- Member of a faction that believes the Amakiir were jailers rather than guardians.
