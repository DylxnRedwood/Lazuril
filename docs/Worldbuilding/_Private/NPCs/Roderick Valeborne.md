# Roderick Valeborne

## Position

**Senior Sequestration Prefect of the Second Station** within the **Office of Civic Hazard and Sealed Property**, a specialist office of the **Surveyor Directorate**.

Valeborne has authority to seal damaged, abandoned or magically hazardous properties, appoint guards, restrict access, coordinate surveyors and corpse-removal teams, and issue investigation writs. He is senior enough to act against noble estates but still answers to the Surveyor-General and Hillkeep.

## Office

Valeborne works from **Red Seal House**, beside the Court of Petitioners in the High Quarter. The building handles condemned properties, disputed estates and civic exclusion orders.

His principal clerk is **Maelin Orrow**, who screens correspondence and controls appointments.

## Contacting Him

The party can reach him by:

- Filing a petition at the Court of Petitioners.
- Sending a letter addressed to Senior Prefect Roderick Valeborne, Red Seal House, Petitioners’ Steps, High Quarter, Caer Oryn.
- Asking Arch-Lector Meren Voss to support a request concerning unrecorded deaths or improper removal of bodies.
- Using Alekthar’s connection to House Amakiir to request a formal audience.
- Waiting near House Vaelisar shortly after sunrise, when Valeborne sometimes inspects the site.

A report involving false Directorate guards, unauthorised use of his seal or an active magical hazard would reach him quickly.

## Appearance

Valeborne is a tall human man in his early fifties, slightly stooped from years spent over plans and survey tables. He has an angular face, deep-set grey eyes and close-cut dark hair heavily silvered at the temples.

He wears a dark burgundy Directorate coat with black cuffs and a silver badge depicting a key crossed over a closed door. His hands are usually marked with charcoal, sealing wax or surveyor’s chalk. At hazardous sites he wears a heavy charcoal overcoat and fitted leather gloves.

## Persona

Valeborne is restrained, exacting and relentlessly procedural. He rarely raises his voice and asks short, precise questions. He dislikes speculation, theatrical behaviour and incomplete accounts, but respects clear timelines, physical evidence and consistent testimony.

He believes procedure exists because frightened witnesses lie, powerful families conceal things and dangerous buildings do not care who owns them.

Typical questions include:

> “When did you enter?”

> “Which door was open?”

> “Who touched the object?”

> “Who told you those men were mine?”

## House Vaelisar

Valeborne genuinely issued the original order sealing House Vaelisar after reports of structural collapse, multiple deaths and an unexplained magical discharge.

The guards currently stationed there are not the wardens he assigned. He received apparently valid paperwork transferring the perimeter to a specialist security detachment under Armature Directorate authority and reluctantly withdrew his own personnel.

If the party tells him that the guards are impostors, the rear entrance was unsecured or a fractured black orb is suspended inside, he initially threatens them with arrest for entering a sealed property. He then realises they may be the only witnesses not controlled by whoever has taken over the manor.

His response:

> “You entered a property under my seal, interfered with an active hazard and returned without informing the gate watch.”

After a pause:

> “Now tell me why the gate watch was not mine.”

Valeborne can become a difficult but valuable ally. He has the authority to open records and issue writs, but is increasingly suspicious that someone within the Directorate has misused his office and seal.
