## Rayna Clarke

_Private NPC working page for Rayna Clarke, created as part of Cassian Clarke's private PC development._

Rayna Clarke is the wife of [Cassian Clarke](Cassian%20Clarke.md) and mother of [Leo Clarke](Leo%20Clarke.md) and [Lyra Clarke](Lyra%20Clarke.md). At the beginning of the campaign she is 38 years old. She is a star charter whose work connects astronomy, navigation, religious observation, mathematical patterning, and the slow interpretation of celestial change.

Rayna met Cassian during his years of study in [Gullspire](../../docs/Worldbuilding/Arkhaven%20States/Havenori%20Republic/Gullspire.md), in the south east of the [Havenori Republic](../../docs/Worldbuilding/Arkhaven%20States/Havenori%20Republic.md). Their relationship grew from shared nights of observation, argument, note-taking, and the belief that the heavens could be both holy and measurable.

## Role in Cassian's Backstory

Rayna is not merely Cassian's wife in the background. She is one of the key people who helped shape his understanding of the stars, the moon, and the strange accretion disk around the sun.

Where Cassian often frames questions through the union of faith and science, Rayna is more directly concerned with patterns. She charts, compares, measures, and preserves records over time. She may not always know what a celestial irregularity means, but she is exactly the sort of person who would notice that the sky has stopped obeying old records.

Their shared work around the stars and the sun should be treated as emotionally important. Cassian's love for Rayna is bound up with the night sky, and his locket reflects the stars from the night they first met.

## Personality

Rayna should feel intelligent, patient, observant, and quietly intense. She is not passive, and she should not be reduced to a vulnerable spouse waiting for Cassian to return. Her work matters. Her records matter. Her judgement should matter.

She likely understands Cassian's need to travel better than anyone else, because she knows the questions troubling him are real. That does not mean his absence is easy. Rayna agreed that he should go, but she and the children still carry the cost of that decision.

## Current Position

After the Sharite conflict around Gullspire and the spread of similar tensions elsewhere, Rayna took Leo and Lyra to [Seabarrow](Seabarrow.md), where they now stay with [Percy Clarke](Percy%20Clarke.md) and [Meri Clarke](Meri%20Clarke.md) in the [Windmere Assembly](Windmere%20Assembly.md).

This move was made for safety, but it should also keep Rayna connected to maritime star-lore, coastal Selunite worship, and the practical consequences of a changing sky.

## DM-Only: Rayna's Possible Discovery

Rayna should be considered one of the first people close to Cassian to notice that the accretion disk's irregularities are not random.

She may have records showing that dark arcs, warped sections, altered solar brightness, or inconsistent moonlight correspond to particular dates, locations, or alignments. She does not need to understand the old god connection. In fact, it is better if she does not. Her importance lies in having evidence that the disk is behaving like a mechanism under stress rather than a natural phenomenon.

Possible discoveries for later use include:

Rayna's charts show that certain damaged sections of the disk correspond to regions along the [Godscar](The%20Godscar.md).

Rayna found that moonlight behaves differently after certain solar anomalies, suggesting the accretion disk affects not only sunlight but reflected lunar light.

Rayna realised that the pattern reflected in Cassian's locket resembles an older alignment preserved in a forgotten chart.

Rayna hid or copied records before the Sharites could destroy them, and those records may become vital later.

## Connection to the Sharite Conflict

Rayna's profession made her a target. The Sharites around Gullspire did not only oppose Selunite worship; they opposed the act of charting and studying the heavens. A star charter who could prove the accretion disk was changing would be dangerous to them.

Orthodox Sharites may see Rayna as a desecrator of sacred darkness. A corrupted splinter may see her as someone whose work must either be stolen, silenced, or repurposed.

## DM Use

Rayna can serve as a source of evidence, emotional pressure, intellectual partnership, and future danger. She should not be used only as bait. Her strongest use is as someone who can push Cassian's arc forward because she noticed something before he did.

If Cassian begins to doubt whether his pilgrimage matters, Rayna's records can prove that the celestial disturbances are real. If Cassian becomes too certain, Rayna's caution can complicate him. If the party needs a reason to care about the sun and accretion disk, Rayna's work can provide the bridge.
