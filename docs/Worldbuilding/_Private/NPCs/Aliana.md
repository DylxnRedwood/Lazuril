## Aliana

_Private NPC file for Alekthar's campaign continuity._

Aliana Amakiir was an Aasimar daughter of [Akaavi](Akaavi.md) and [Miriam](Miriam.md). She carried the Bound Radiance and died during the Night of Golden Tears.

Aliana was one of the family members seated among those Alekthar knew were connected to the hidden inheritance, even though he did not yet know its name. Her presence helps underline how widely the Bound Radiance was distributed through House Amakiir before the massacre.

## Bound Radiance

Aliana was one of the ten bearers targeted in the ritual. Her blood and light were drawn into the working before Amari's incomplete Continuance caused the Radiance to recoil into her.

## Relationship to Alekthar

Aliana's death is another reminder that Alekthar was excluded from a burden that killed those included. His grief toward the bearer cousins should be complicated by the childhood distance created by private instruction and family reverence.

## DM Use

Aliana can appear through marked family genealogies, old lesson schedules, or references in Aiemi's private notes. If each bearer was watched for different signs, Aliana's record may preserve terminology Alekthar can later decode.
