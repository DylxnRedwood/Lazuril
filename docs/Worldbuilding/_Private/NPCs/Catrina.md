## Catrina

_Private NPC file for Alekthar's campaign continuity._

Catrina is the human wife of [Ahkotan](Ahkotan.md), mother to [Amalina](Amalina.md) and [Aldoran](Aldoran.md), and one of the seven survivors of the Night of Golden Tears.

She survived the massacre and became one of the quiet powers holding the remnants of House Amakiir together. In the aftermath, Catrina helped Alekthar learn accounts, land disputes, inheritance matters, harvest forecasting, contract language, and the hard practical work of keeping Cael Tharos alive.

## Aftermath

Catrina now shares stewardship of the estate with Ahkotan. She helps raise Amalina, [Astaavi](Astaavi.md), and [Avaris](Avaris.md), making her one of the central maternal figures in the diminished house.

## Relationship to Alekthar

Catrina is useful because she represents care without mysticism. She cannot answer the oldest secrets of the Bound Radiance, but she understands survival, duty, and the humiliations of decline. Alekthar may trust her judgement more than he trusts the surviving fragments of sacred family tradition.

## DM Use

Catrina can be used as a correspondent from Cael Tharos, a guardian of estate records, or a voice of practical warning. If Alekthar is chasing a dangerous truth without regard for the living family left behind, Catrina is the person who should call him on it.

Linked PC: [Alekthar Amakiir](Alekthar%20Amakiir.md).
