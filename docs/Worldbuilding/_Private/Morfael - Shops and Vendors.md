# Morfael: Shops and Vendors

> [!dm] DM-Only Settlement Support
> This file expands practical shops, vendors, street sellers, and service counters in [[Morfael]]. It supports [[Morfael - NPCs and Sites]], [[Campaign Opener - The Man in the River]], and [[Session 2 - Ashes at the Last Lantern]].
>
> Morfael vendors should feel useful, worn, and local. Most are not fantasy shopkeepers with magical menus. They are people trying to keep food, heat, rope, records, or burial cloth moving under the shadow of [[Caer Oryn]].

## Running Morfael Commerce

Morfael sits close enough to Caer Oryn to be regulated, but far enough below it to be treated as a service town. Its shops are practical: food, rope, fish, tools, letters, ferries, repairs, cheap medicine, drink, storage, records, and discreet handoffs.

Prices are fair when people trust you and inflated when they think you are trouble.

> [!dm] Tone
> Morfael trade should feel wet, smoked, ink-stained, and patched. A vendor may sell food with one hand and pass a rumour with the other. A shop can be honest and still know where smuggling happens. Ordinary people should not all be criminals, but most have learned not to ask unnecessary questions.

## Black Eel Market Vendors

### Branna Cale's Pie Cart

Branna Cale sells food from a wide handcart with a patched canvas roof and two iron braziers that never seem fully clean. She feeds dockhands, children, guards, ferrymen, and anyone willing to pay in coin or gossip.

> [!readaloud] Read Aloud: Branna's Cart
> The pie cart announces itself by smell before sight: hot grease, pepper, eel, onions, and damp charcoal. A broad woman in a flour-dusted shawl turns pasties with a knife so worn the handle has gone smooth. Children orbit the cart with the discipline of gulls, hoping for broken crusts.
>
> Branna Cale looks you over once and says, “Buying, asking, or pretending one is not the other?”

**Sells:** eel pasties, mutton pies, hot broth, old bread fried in fat, small beer.

**Prices:** cheap for locals, normal for strangers, free scraps for frightened children.

**What Branna Knows:**

- Tovin Rusk was speaking to a cloaked stranger before Edrin washed ashore.
- Watchmen buy food here when they want to hear rumours without admitting it.
- Market folk saw clean boots near the Flood Steps before dawn.

**Secret:** Branna knows more about children’s movements than any watchman in Morfael.

### Mara Venn's Fish Boards

Mara Venn works two stained boards near the lower market line. She sells fresh fish, smoked eel, fish heads for stew, and scraps for cats, dogs, and poorer families.

> [!readaloud] Read Aloud: Mara's Boards
> Mara Venn’s stall is all knife rhythm, river stink, and wet wood. Silver fish lie open on the boards, their scales catching the grey light. Eels smoke over a low brazier, twisting slowly in the heat. Mara does not stop cutting when she speaks.
>
> “If you’re here for clean answers, you came to the wrong boards.”

**Sells:** river fish, smoked eel, salted scraps, fish oil, gull bait, cheap knife sharpening.

**What Mara Knows:**

- Who is local and who is pretending.
- Which boats came in before dawn.
- Which market stalls were moved after the Flood Steps incident.

**Secret:** Mara saw a covered black barge moving against usual traffic before dawn, its crew hiding military boots beneath plain cloaks.

### Hesta's Net-Stool

Hesta Brinn sits on a low stool with nets across her knees, repairing tears and listening to everything. She looks harmless because she is old; she survives because she is not harmless.

> [!readaloud] Read Aloud: Hesta's Net-Stool
> An elderly woman sits at the market edge with a fishing net spread across her lap. Her fingers move with slow certainty, tying knots without looking at them. Around her stool are cork floats, broken hooks, and a chipped mug of tea gone cold.
>
> She looks up only after you have been standing there long enough to become interesting.

**Sells / Provides:** net repairs, fishing line, old hooks, small local introductions.

**What Hesta Knows:**

- Children use routes adults forget.
- Men with clean coats have been seen asking quiet questions.
- Another red-veined man may have been moved toward the Red Lockhouse months earlier.

**Secret:** She can identify a Crown river cart by the sound of its wheel hubs.

## Quay and Tavern Vendors

### The Hook and Bell

The Hook and Bell is a low tavern near the quay. It is not safe, but it is useful. People come here to drink, sleep, make small deals, wait for bad weather to pass, or become forgettable.

> [!readaloud] Read Aloud: The Hook and Bell
> The Hook and Bell squats near the quay with yellow light leaking through warped shutters. Inside, the ceiling is low, the air is warm, and everything smells of sour beer, fish stew, wet wool, pipe smoke, and old arguments.
>
> A bell hangs above the bar, cracked down one side. Beside it is an iron hook big enough for cargo or a man.

**Proprietor:** Orwyn Breck.

**Staff:** Sella Varrow, server and rumour source.

**Sells:** sour beer, fish stew, cheap bread, dock rum, rented floor space, cramped upstairs rooms.

**Access:** public, but strangers are noticed.

**What Happens Here:**

- Dockhands talk after shifts.
- Smugglers use ordinary noise as cover.
- Watchmen drink when off duty.
- Veyra Sorn may rent an upstairs room under a false name.

**Secrets:**

- Orwyn knows who rents rooms but does not always know their real names.
- Sella knows Rennic has a second ledger hidden at Vellum House.
- A private back booth can be bought for an hour if the party pays and does not bleed on the floor.

## Low Lantern Row Vendors

### Maelis Wren's Apothecary

Maelis Wren keeps a narrow apothecary in Low Lantern Row. It is half healer’s room, half poison cupboard, though she insists the dangerous things are only for rats.

> [!readaloud] Read Aloud: Maelis Wren's Apothecary
> The apothecary is narrow, warm, and bitter-smelling. Bundles of dried herbs hang from the ceiling beams. Brown bottles line the shelves in uneven rows. A kettle ticks over a tiny stove beside a basin stained with old tinctures.
>
> Maelis Wren wipes her hands on a dark apron and says, “If it’s bleeding, sit. If it’s cursed, leave. If it’s both, pay first.”

**Sells:** poultices, fever tinctures, dockhand liniment, sleep draughts, pain tonics, clotting powder, rat poison.

**Useful Items:** healer’s kit, antitoxin substitute, sedative draught, burn salve, smelling salts.

**What Maelis Knows:**

- A Crown buyer recently purchased surgical spirits, clotting agents, bitter sedatives, and jars suitable for residue storage.
- Red mineral grit behaves badly when mixed with alcohol or heat.
- She can identify whether puncture wounds came from needle, thorn, tooth, or tool.

**Secret:** She kept one invoice stub from the Crown buyer because she disliked his hands.

### Mavren Holt's Pawn Counter

Mavren Holt buys what people cannot afford to keep and sells what they should not ask too much about.

> [!readaloud] Read Aloud: Mavren's Counter
> Mavren Holt’s pawn counter is protected by iron mesh, three locks, and a dog that looks too tired to bark but not too tired to bite. Shelves behind him hold cracked tools, wedding rings, old medals, boat charms, cheap blades, and objects wrapped in cloth with no labels.
>
> Mavren smiles like a man who has never once been surprised by poverty.

**Sells:** pawned tools, knives, charms, old jewellery, patched boots, trinkets, questionable relic scraps.

**Buys:** almost anything small enough to hide and valuable enough to deny.

**What Mavren Knows:**

- He briefly handled a gold-marked Veylrain fragment.
- The buyer paid in Crown-stamped coin and did not haggle.
- He can identify some hidden marks used by fences and relic runners.

**Secret:** Mavren has one tiny shaving from the Veylrain fragment embedded in a cloth under his counter.

## Records, Letters, and Permits

### Vellum House

Vellum House writes things for people who cannot write, should not write, or cannot safely use official channels. It is not openly criminal. It is useful enough that the town tolerates it.

> [!readaloud] Read Aloud: Vellum House
> Vellum House is barely wider than its front door. Inside, the walls are packed with pigeonholes, tied ledgers, old cargo slips, and paper bundles weighted with stones. The room smells of dust, ink, wax, and damp wool.
>
> Every surface has writing on it, and none of it looks meant for strangers.

**Workers:** Rennic Sallow, Nira Clent sometimes, hired copyists.

**Sells / Provides:** letters, death notices, cargo manifests, ferry permits, false invoices, copied contracts, witness statements.

**Legitimate Use:** helping illiterate locals and small traders.

**Hidden Use:** laundering cargo language, disguising mineral shipments, producing passable paperwork.

**Secrets:**

- A partial Amakiir-linked mineral mark was copied here.
- A destroyed ledger page may have survived as a mirror-copy or blotter impression.
- Rennic’s second ledger is hidden under a floorboard or beneath the lower paper press.

### The Reedmark Post

The Reedmark Post handles practical courier work through Morfael: letters, ferry messages, death notices, family correspondence, route tags, returned parcels, and northbound handoffs.

> [!readaloud] Read Aloud: The Reedmark Post
> The Reedmark Post sits between a cooper’s yard and a shuttered rope store, its sign painted with a reed stalk bound in red twine. Inside, waxed satchels hang from pegs and reed tubes are bundled by route. A wall of wooden cubbies bears practical labels: Morfael Local, Caer Oryn Outer, Harrow Road, Returned, Northbank, Veylrain Edge.
>
> Behind the counter, a woman with ink-stained fingers looks up and says, “Destination, sender, paid speed, paid risk. In that order.”

**Proprietor:** Maela Quist.

**Services:** local letters, Caer Oryn outer delivery, northbound post, Veylrain edge handoffs, discreet courier routing.

**Veylrain Requirements:** settlement, clan, trail, kin-mark, known handoff contact, or enough family detail to route through edge-runners.

**Secrets:**

- Fast routes are watched.
- Discreet routes are unreliable but safer.
- A Crown-adjacent office has recently placed holds on some northbound satchels.

## Repair Yards and Working Shops

### Cairnwell Yard

Cairnwell Yard repairs what keeps Morfael moving: carts, barges, chain posts, hoists, ferry fittings, cracked wheels, lock tools, and warehouse doors.

> [!readaloud] Read Aloud: Cairnwell Yard
> Cairnwell Yard rings with practical noise: hammer on bracket, saw through wet timber, chain dragged over stone. Half-repaired carts stand beside stacks of planks and bent iron. A barge rudder leans against a wall like the wing of some dead river beast.
>
> Nobody here looks idle. Even the arguments seem scheduled.

**Workers:** Garran Tallow, Aedan Marric, apprentices.

**Sells / Provides:** cart repairs, chain work, hooks, brackets, wheel fixes, timber patching, tool sharpening.

**Useful for PCs:** mundane repairs, improvised gear, identifying restraint hardware, finding riverwork clues.

**Secrets:**

- Garran repaired a broken Crown-steel restraint buckle with red dust in the hinge.
- Aedan saw scrape marks under the Red Lockhouse water gate.
- A damaged ferry chain was recently replaced faster than the official budget should allow.

## Authority Counters

### The Red Lockhouse Public Counter

The Red Lockhouse public counter is where ordinary people meet Crown river authority: tolls, cargo stamps, lock times, ferry disputes, chain gate delays, and denials issued with clean ink.

> [!readaloud] Read Aloud: Red Lockhouse Public Counter
> The Red Lockhouse is too clean for Morfael.
>
> Its lower stones are wet with river spray, but the brass handles shine. Notices are pinned in straight lines beside the public counter. A clerk behind iron-framed glass stamps papers without looking at the people attached to them.
>
> Somewhere below the floor, water moves through unseen machinery with a slow, heavy pulse.

**Public Services:** toll stamps, cargo seals, lock schedules, river permits, chain gate notices.

**Key NPC:** Joric Marn.

**Access:** public counter only without permit. Lower chambers are restricted.

**Secrets:**

- “Stone meal” appears in coded entries.
- Sealed orders arrive from Caer Oryn that even local officials cannot open.
- The lower chambers connect to covered river infrastructure used by the Crown Scar Directorate.
