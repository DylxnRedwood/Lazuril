# The Counting House

> [!warning] Private DM Notes
> This page contains the location of Captain Odran Kesh, the structure of a Black Register field office, evidence concerning the Anvil Work, hidden routes beneath the Foundry Quarter, encounter plans, and information that should not be shown to players.

## Summary

**Public name:** The Foundry Counting House  
**Common name:** The Counting House  
**Street:** Ironwake Lane, Foundry Quarter, [[Caer Oryn]]  
**Public purpose:** Foundry payroll, shift reconciliation, accident compensation, contract accounts, material invoices, and worker registration  
**Secret purpose:** Black Register field command post, covert payment office, operational archive, interview site, and document destruction point  
**Current commanding officer:** Captain Odran Kesh  
**Related agent:** [[Garron Duskholt - Threadknife]]  
**Related operations:** The Anvil Work, Magpie Lane, the Gilded Hart room 13, veinstone movement, and the recovery activity at [[House Vaelisar]]

The Counting House is where the Foundry Quarter turns labour into numbers. Hundreds of workers pass through its public hall each week to collect wages, dispute missing shifts, register injury payments, or receive stamped work tokens. It is dull, necessary, crowded, and protected by the simple fact that interfering with it delays wages for people who cannot afford delay.

That public function is genuine.

The upper floor and basement conceal a Black Register command post operated by Captain Odran Kesh. Kesh uses the building because covert money disappears most easily inside legitimate money. Payments to field agents are entered as overtime, dead men remain on payroll as operational identities, medical supplies are buried inside accident compensation accounts, and restricted freight is passed through the books as furnace loss or spoiled ore.

The Counting House should not feel like a secret fortress from the outside. It should feel like a place where a clerk can ruin a worker's month by moving one line in a ledger.

---

## Canon and Naming

The name Garron gave the party under interrogation was **the Obsidian Register**. That is a field-facing name used by branded agents, expendable couriers, and people who are never meant to understand the full organisation.

Internally, Kesh calls the compartment **the Black Register**. It is a deniable operational arm protected by the Surveyor Directorate. The two names refer to the same network at different levels of access.

Garron knew enough to name Captain Odran Kesh as his practical superior. He did not know the full chain above Kesh, the identities of all political patrons, or the final purpose of the oldest relics.

Odran Kesh is the canonical spelling used throughout the existing campaign notes.

## Purpose at the Table

Use the Counting House to provide:

- A grounded infiltration scene inside an active civic workplace.
- A direct confrontation with Garron's superior officer.
- Evidence that the conspiracy operates through ordinary state systems.
- Moral pressure caused by innocent clerks and workers being present.
- A route toward the deeper Anvil Work without revealing the whole campaign.
- Information connecting Magpie Lane, room 13 at the Gilded Hart, veinstone shipments, and official protection.
- A chance for Kesh to become a recurring antagonist, reluctant informant, prisoner, or corpse depending on the party's choices.

The central tension is not whether the party can defeat the guards. It is whether they can reach Kesh, secure useful records, and leave without allowing the state to describe them as armed criminals who attacked a payroll office full of civilians.

# Location and Surroundings

## Ironwake Lane

The Counting House stands on Ironwake Lane, a controlled street between Ironmeasure Hall and a row of furnace supply offices. The road is broad enough for payroll carts and document wagons, but narrower than the main foundry roads. Soot gathers in the mortar. Steam pipes run overhead between buildings. Shift bells carry clearly through the lane.

Nearby sites include:

- **Ironmeasure Hall:** Material inspection office. Red dust residue or false cargo weights may be noticed here.
- **Gate Six:** The nearest controlled entrance to the Foundry Quarter.
- **The West Furnace Steps:** A smoking and meal-break area used by workers.
- **The Ash Cart Yard:** A rear service yard shared by several civic buildings.
- **An old culvert line:** Runs beneath Ironwake Lane and joins older service routes under the quarter.

The street is busiest before the first shift, at midday, and during the final hour before payroll shutters close.

## Public Reputation

Workers do not like the Counting House, but they trust that it will still be there tomorrow. It is known for strict records, slow disputes, and accurate payments. Rumour says senior clerks can recover wages lost three years earlier, provided the worker has the correct token and enough patience.

No one publicly associates the building with spies or secret police.

People who work nearby have noticed:

- Unmarked document carts arriving after midnight.
- Senior officers using the side entrance rather than the public hall.
- Clerks burning records during weeks when no audit was scheduled.
- Injured men entering through the rear and never appearing at the public counter.
- A shuttered upper window that remains lit during late-night operations.

Most assume corruption, private banking, or Crown procurement. In Caer Oryn, those explanations are dangerous enough without inventing worse ones.

# Exterior

The Counting House is a square, three-storey building of dark red brick, pale stone trim, and iron-framed windows. Its roof is steep and slate-covered, with two narrow chimneys and a small signal lantern beneath the eastern eave.

A brass plaque beside the main entrance reads:

> **FOUNDRY COUNTING HOUSE**  
> **WAGES, COMPENSATION, MATERIAL ACCOUNTS, AND REGISTERED CONTRACTS**  
> **DISPUTES HEARD BY TOKEN AND APPOINTMENT**

The main doors are heavy oak reinforced with iron strips. A smaller side door opens into Clerk's Cut, a narrow alley between the Counting House and a bonded tool store. The rear service gate faces the Ash Cart Yard.

### Exterior Details

- Six broad public windows face Ironwake Lane. Their lower panes are protected by iron grilles.
- Workers queue beneath a covered stone arcade during payroll hours.
- Two uniformed civic guards stand at the front when the counters are open.
- A narrow brass speaking tube beside the side door connects to the upper office.
- The rear gate is wide enough for a small document cart.
- A gutter chain beside the eastern wall can be climbed, but it is visible from Gate Six during daylight.
- The upper east window belongs to Kesh's office. Its shutters can be locked from inside with a crossbar.

## Read Aloud: Exterior

> The Counting House looks less like a bank than a workshop built for paper. Dark brick walls rise behind an iron-railed queue, their windows divided into narrow squares of smoke-stained glass. Workers wait beneath a stone arcade with brass tokens in hand while clerks call numbers from behind the doors. Above them, a small lantern hangs beneath the eastern roofline, unlit despite the soot-dark afternoon.
>
> Every person entering is counted. Every person leaving is watched long enough to be remembered.

# Daily Routine

## Normal Hours

**Before first bell:** Payroll carts arrive. Senior clerks enter through the side door.  
**Morning:** Wage collection, shift amendments, contract entries, and material invoices.  
**Midday:** Public hall remains open with reduced staff. Kesh usually reviews field reports upstairs.  
**Afternoon:** Compensation disputes and contractor appointments.  
**After final shift bell:** Public hall closes. Ordinary clerks leave. Black Register work begins.  
**After midnight:** Burn furnace, covert payments, interviews, and document transfers.

## Current Situation After Garron's Death

Kesh does not receive a magical warning when one of his agents dies. Garron was expected to report in person or through a marked drop.

Use the following timing:

### Within Twenty-Four Hours

Kesh believes Garron is delayed or lying low. The Counting House operates normally. Kesh is present and has not begun destroying the Magpie Lane records.

### Between Twenty-Four and Forty-Eight Hours

Kesh knows Garron has missed two signals. He begins reviewing exposure risks, quietly doubles the rear guard, and orders Damaris Thornbrook to separate the most damaging ledgers for destruction.

### After Forty-Eight Hours

Kesh assumes Garron is captured or dead. He remains at the Counting House because abandoning the office without orders would expose the Directorate and make him the obvious scapegoat. He prepares an evacuation case, burns minor records, and places a warning on room 13 at the Gilded Hart.

### If the Party Publicly Names Kesh

The Counting House enters controlled lockdown. The public counters stay open to preserve appearances, but Kesh prepares to capture the party, identify their sources, and leave through the culvert if the situation turns against him.

# Approaching the Counting House

## Public Entry

Anyone may enter the public hall during operating hours, but visitors must take a brass queue token and state their business to the vestibule clerk.

Credible reasons include:

- Missing wages.
- A disputed work token.
- Compensation following a foundry injury or death.
- Delivery of a contract or invoice.
- Ecclesiastical inquiry into a dead worker.
- Audit or material inspection business.
- A request to meet a senior clerk concerning a registered contractor.

The public route provides the easiest entry but the hardest movement into restricted areas.

## Ecclesiastical or Official Writ

A death inquiry writ from the Final Journey can compel the Counting House to produce a worker's public wage and compensation records. It does not grant access to upper offices, closed archives, or Crown-restricted accounts.

Kesh will respect a valid writ in public. He will not allow it to reach anything useful without delay, redaction, or substitution.

## Side Door

The Clerk's Cut entrance is used by senior staff, inspectors, and approved contractors.

- **Deception DC 14:** Pose as a scheduled contractor or courier with plausible paperwork.
- **Thieves' Tools DC 15:** Open the ordinary lock.
- **Investigation DC 14:** Notice a second catch connected to a bell wire.
- **Thieves' Tools DC 16:** Disable the bell wire without sounding it.

## Rear Gate

The rear gate opens during document-cart deliveries and ash removal.

A party disguised as cart workers can enter with a credible load, but the cart token is checked against a slate in the guard room.

- **Deception DC 13:** Pass casual inspection with clothing, tools, and a plausible cart.
- **Forgery Kit DC 15:** Create a usable delivery token.
- **Perception DC 14:** Notice the rear guard checking wheel chalk marks as well as paperwork.

## Roof

The roof can be reached from the bonded tool store or by climbing the eastern gutter chain.

- **Athletics DC 13:** Climb the chain or adjacent brickwork.
- **Stealth DC 15:** Reach the roof without being seen from Gate Six.
- **Thieves' Tools DC 14:** Open the roof hatch.

The roof hatch leads to the upper ledger gallery. It is trapped with a hanging brass strip that strikes a chime if lifted carelessly.

## Culvert Route

The basement escape route can be approached from the old service culvert beneath Ironwake Lane. Garron's information about drains and sealed works routes can point the party in the correct direction, but the tunnel is not obvious.

- **Investigation or Survival DC 15:** Follow the correct culvert branch using scrape marks, boot mud, and recent lamp soot.
- **Perception DC 14:** Notice the disguised iron door in the culvert wall.
- **Thieves' Tools DC 17:** Open the Crown lock without Kesh's key.

This route enters the basement near the holding room.

# Security

## Visible Security

During public hours:

- Two civic guards at the front.
- One vestibule clerk checking tokens.
- Two guards in the ground-floor guard room.
- Six to ten ordinary clerks and cashiers.
- Between fifteen and forty workers or contractors in the public hall.

After public hours:

- Two front guards.
- Two rear guards.
- Sergeant Ulric Ashford.
- Two Black Register field agents.
- Damaris Thornbrook and one cipher clerk.
- Captain Odran Kesh.

## Hidden Security Measures

### Bell Wires

Restricted doors have fine brass wires connected to small bells in the guard room. The bells are quiet enough that the public hall will not notice them.

### Shutter Bars

A lever in the guard room drops iron bars across the public exit and rear passage. This is intended to contain thieves until the Watch arrives.

### Speaking Tubes

Kesh's office, the guard room, the cashier cage, and the basement burn room are connected by brass speaking tubes.

### The Red Drawer

A red-painted drawer in the operations room contains alchemical fire salts. Pulling the interior cord ignites documents placed in the destruction tray and fills the room with bitter grey smoke.

### Register Plate

The secure archive door contains a thin veinstone-flecked brass plate. It is not a powerful ward, but it becomes warm when the door is opened without the correct key and causes the signal lantern under the eastern eave to glow dull red.

- **Arcana DC 14:** Recognise the plate as a simple detection device rather than a defensive ward.
- **Thieves' Tools or Arcana DC 16:** Bypass it.

## Alarm Stages

### Stage One: Scrutiny

A clerk or guard believes something is wrong. Doors remain open. A runner quietly informs the guard room.

### Stage Two: Controlled Lockdown

Public exits are barred. Clerks tell workers there is a cash discrepancy. Kesh sends agents to observe rather than attack.

### Stage Three: Evidence Purge

Damaris moves priority ledgers to the burn room. Kesh secures his evacuation case. Sergeant Ashford attempts to isolate armed intruders from civilians.

### Stage Four: Escape or Capture

Kesh withdraws toward the basement culvert if he believes the office is lost. He prefers to capture at least one party member, but he will not die merely to protect paper.

# Ground Floor

## C1. Token Vestibule

The vestibule is divided by iron rails into three narrow lanes. A clerk sits behind a barred desk and issues brass tokens stamped with numbers and letters. A wooden board lists the day's available services.

### What Can Be Found

- A drawer containing public queue tokens.
- A slate naming senior staff currently present.
- A small bell pedal beneath the clerk's desk.
- A visitor book that records contractors, clergy, inspectors, and unusual appointments.

### Checks

- **Investigation DC 12:** The visitor book has several entries written in the same hand under different names.
- **Insight DC 13:** The vestibule clerk has been trained to delay difficult visitors while informing someone upstairs.
- **Sleight of Hand DC 14:** Take or exchange a restricted visitor token unnoticed.

## C2. Public Wage Hall

The main hall contains four long counters, benches, notice boards, and chalked queue lines. Clerks work behind brass grilles while workers argue over shifts, deductions, injuries, and missing payments.

The room is noisy, crowded, and full of civilians during public hours.

## Read Aloud: Public Hall

> The hall smells of wet wool, soot, sealing wax, and old paper. Workers crowd beneath numbered boards while clerks call tokens without looking up. Coin passes through narrow brass trays. Complaints are repeated, shortened, entered, and stamped until anger becomes a line of ink.
>
> Above the counters hangs the Crown's painted scale, one pan holding a hammer and the other a ledger.

### What Can Be Found

- Public payroll books.
- Lists of registered foundry workers.
- Injury compensation notices.
- Shift tokens and contractor stamps.
- A notice warning that false claims are punishable by imprisonment.

### Clue

A careful search of recent payment summaries shows wages issued to workers whose names appear on death compensation lists. These are operational identities being kept active.

- **Investigation DC 15:** Identify three duplicated names.
- **History or Investigation DC 17:** Recognise the duplicated entries as a deliberate ghost-payroll system rather than ordinary clerical error.

## C3. Cashier Cage and Decoy Vault

The cashier cage is protected by brass grillework and a locked iron door. A visible vault stands behind it. The vault contains legitimate payroll coin, sealed compensation packets, and contractor payments.

This is not the Black Register vault.

### Contents

- Approximately **1,800 gp equivalent** in mixed payroll coin on a normal payday.
- Sealed wage packets bearing worker names and shift numbers.
- Compensation payments intended for injured workers and bereaved families.
- A smaller lockbox containing **75 gp** in unassigned accounting surplus.

Stealing the payroll hurts ordinary workers and creates immediate city-wide attention. The money is heavily marked through stamped packets, recorded weights, and numbered bags.

### Checks

- **Investigation DC 13:** The public vault is too visible and too routinely audited to conceal covert payments.
- **Thieves' Tools DC 17:** Open the vault.
- **Insight DC 12:** The cashiers are frightened of robbery, but know nothing about Kesh's operation.

## C4. Compensation Room

This private ground-floor room is used for injury claims, workplace deaths, and disputes that should not be shouted across the public hall. It contains a long table, six chairs, a wash basin, and shelves of forms.

Black Register agents occasionally use it to meet low-level couriers under the cover of compensation interviews.

### What Can Be Found

- Standard injury forms.
- Death-payment certificates.
- A locked drawer containing blank forms already bearing a civic seal.
- A bloodstain beneath the table, scrubbed but not fully removed.
- A small loop fixed beneath one chair, suitable for securing an ankle chain.

### Checks

- **Medicine DC 12:** The blood came from a facial injury rather than a fatal wound.
- **Investigation DC 14:** The chair loop and floor scratches show that someone was restrained here.
- **Forgery Kit DC 14:** The pre-sealed blank forms can be used to create a convincing foundry death or injury record.

## C5. General Clerks' Office

Rows of desks fill this warm, cramped room. Ink bottles, sand shakers, wax sticks, ledger weights, and personal cups are arranged with quiet precision.

Most clerks here are ordinary civic employees.

### What Can Be Found

- Shift ledgers.
- Lists of foundry closures.
- Contractor addresses.
- Material loss reports.
- A speaking-tube mouth hidden behind a hinged timetable.

### Clue

One material-loss ledger records repeated small quantities of red mineral waste attributed to furnace contamination. The amounts match veinstone movements disguised as industrial loss.

- **Investigation DC 14:** Notice the repeated pattern.
- **Arcana or Nature DC 15:** Realise the listed disposal precautions are excessive for ordinary ore contamination.

## C6. Guard Room

The guard room contains weapon racks, a duty slate, a narrow table, six stools, and the shutter-bar lever. A wall board shows patrol positions and expected cart arrivals.

### Contents

- Four spears, two light crossbows, and ordinary restraint chains.
- A ring of public-area keys.
- The front and rear shutter levers.
- A coded slate marking Black Register staff as inspection officers.
- A brass speaking tube connected to Kesh's office.

### Checks

- **Investigation DC 13:** Several supposed inspectors have no corresponding public appointments.
- **Thieves' Tools DC 13:** Jam a shutter lever before lockdown.
- **Sleight of Hand DC 14:** Take the public key ring.

## C7. Rear Cart Court

The rear court is enclosed by brick walls and a wide timber gate. Document carts, ash bins, and sealed paper crates are stored beneath a lean-to roof.

A stair rises to the first-floor side landing. A locked door leads to the basement archive stairs.

### What Can Be Found

- Wheel chalk matching the carts used at Magpie Lane.
- Empty document crates lined with thin copper foil.
- Grey courier tags without public serial numbers.
- A false-bottom ash bin large enough to conceal a person or several document cases.

### Checks

- **Investigation DC 13:** The copper-lined crates were not built for paper.
- **Perception DC 14:** Notice recent red-black grit in one cart seam.
- **Stealth DC 13:** Move from the court to the upper landing while the rear guard is distracted.

# First Floor

## C8. Upper Ledger Gallery

A long gallery overlooks the public wage hall through interior windows. Senior clerks can watch the counters below without being seen clearly from the floor.

The gallery contains shelf after shelf of active ledgers, contractor files, and shift summaries.

### What Can Be Found

- A hidden viewing slit into the public hall.
- A roof hatch with a brass chime trap.
- A locked cabinet containing staff background files.
- A list of workers flagged for unusual endurance, rapid healing, criminal history, debt, or lack of family ties.

The flagged list is an early selection document for the Anvil Work.

- **Investigation DC 14:** Understand that the listed traits are not relevant to payroll or ordinary employment.
- **Medicine DC 15:** Recognise that the injury histories were selected to identify people who survive trauma unusually well.

## C9. Captain Odran Kesh's Office

Kesh's office is severe, clean, and almost bare. It contains a broad desk, two visitor chairs, a map cabinet, a locked sideboard, and a narrow eastern window. There are no family portraits, trophies, or decorative weapons.

A polished sabre rack stands beside the desk. The rack is empty when Kesh is present.

## Read Aloud: Kesh's Office

> The office is arranged like a position prepared for defence. The desk faces the door. The chairs offer no cover. Every cabinet can be reached without turning one's back on a visitor. A map of the Foundry Quarter hangs behind the desk, its public roads carefully inked and its older service routes left blank.
>
> The man waiting inside wears a plain charcoal coat and clean grey gloves. He does not rise in surprise. He looks at each of you in turn, as though matching faces to reports he has already read.

### What Can Be Found

- Kesh's key ring, including the true vault and culvert keys.
- A hand crossbow beneath the desk.
- A locked drawer containing Garron's last three field reports.
- A receipt for room 13 at [[The Gilded Hart Hotel]].
- A folded map showing Magpie Lane, Ironwake Lane, and three sealed culvert entrances.
- A sealed order bearing the office mark of Director Sann Morcant, but no explicit operational wording.
- A list of nine agents identified only by codenames.

### Garron's File

Garron's file uses the codename **Threadknife** and records:

- Assignment to observe and stop inquiry at Magpie Lane.
- Authority to use lethal force if the relic route was exposed.
- A missed reporting mark after the rooftop attack.
- A withheld payment of **18 gp** pending confirmation that the party had been neutralised.
- A note in Kesh's hand: **Useful, fast, undisciplined when cornered. Do not promote.**

### Checks

- **Investigation DC 13:** The map cabinet is shallower than the wall behind it.
- **Investigation DC 15:** Find the concealed evacuation case behind the map cabinet.
- **Thieves' Tools DC 16:** Open Kesh's desk drawer.
- **Insight DC 15:** Kesh has deliberately removed anything that could be used to threaten a family or private attachment.

### Evacuation Case

The concealed case contains:

- **110 gp** in unmarked mixed coin.
- Two sets of plain clothes.
- A compact disguise kit.
- Three blank travel papers.
- A coded address in the River Districts.
- A black glass token marked with the crying eye and thin crown.
- One dose of antitoxin.
- A packet of grey powder that creates thick smoke when crushed into flame.

## C10. Black Register Operations Room

This room is the true heart of the Counting House. A central table holds route maps, agent assignments, payment codes, and sealed message tubes. The walls are lined with shallow cabinets rather than bookshelves.

The room has no public function.

### What Can Be Found

- The current agent roster for the Foundry Quarter cell.
- Grey-tag freight movements.
- A partial schedule for subject transfers.
- Medical supply requisitions hidden as accident-treatment orders.
- A board marking room 13 at the Gilded Hart as a rotating dead drop.
- A file labelled **AUG-03**, referring to Edrin Vaal.
- A notation that the lizard idol is an **anchor-piece of Veylrain origin**.
- A brief recovery reference to House Vaelisar.

### The AUG-03 File

The file confirms:

- Edrin was moved through the Morfael route.
- He survived an exposure event that should have killed him.
- His instability made him unsuitable for controlled deployment.
- He was marked for recovery, observation, and possible termination.
- Garron was not told Edrin's final destination.

The file does not explain the full procedure.

### House Vaelisar Reference

The Vaelisar entry reads:

> **Separate authority. No field interference. Recovery teams operating under direct civic seal. Twelve removals confirmed. One containment object transferred. Register personnel are not to approach the property without written instruction.**

This tells the party that Kesh's operation did not cause the Vaelisar incident, but someone above or beside him used related systems to contain the aftermath.

### Checks

- **Investigation DC 12:** Identify room 13 as a dead drop rather than a permanent base.
- **Investigation DC 14:** Connect the medical requisitions to missing-person movements.
- **Arcana DC 15:** Understand that the anchor-piece is intended to stabilise or direct veinstone reactions.
- **Investigation DC 16:** Decode enough transfer marks to identify the next route beneath Furnace Nine.
- **Investigation DC 18:** Identify Director Sann Morcant's office as the common administrative shield without finding a direct signed order.

## C11. Cipher Room

Damaris Thornbrook works here with one trusted assistant. The room contains cipher wheels, code ledgers, sealing tools, and a narrow stove used to soften wax.

### What Can Be Found

- A current code sheet for agent codenames.
- Wax impressions of civic and contractor seals.
- A list of dead workers whose identities remain active.
- Three letters waiting to be encoded.
- A half-decoded message concerning missing subjects beneath Furnace Nine.

### Checks

- **Investigation DC 14:** Use the code sheet to identify Threadknife as Garron.
- **Forgery Kit DC 15:** Create a convincing Black Register message or grey tag.
- **Investigation DC 17:** Decode a reference to a medical handler called **the Chirurgeon of Cinders**, whose real identity is not recorded here.

## C12. Private Interview Room

This windowless room contains a bolted table, four chairs, and a drain in the floor. The walls are lined with thick felt beneath timber panels to deaden sound.

Kesh uses it for agent discipline, courier questioning, and meetings that require privacy.

### What Can Be Found

- Restraint brackets beneath the table.
- A cabinet containing manacles, clean cloth, smelling salts, and basic medical supplies.
- Scratches on the inside of the door.
- A loose panel concealing two signed confessions from compromised agents.

Kesh does not treat torture as entertainment. He permits controlled violence when he believes it will produce useful information. This distinction matters to him and means nothing to his victims.

## C13. Senior Staff Rest Room

A small room with four cots, a kettle, lockers, and a washstand. It is used during long audits and overnight operations.

### What Can Be Found

- Sergeant Ashford's spare uniform.
- Damaris's personal notebook, written in a private shorthand.
- Two Black Register field coats.
- Ordinary food, blankets, and personal items that make clear the people working here live normal lives outside the building.

Damaris's notebook contains doubts about the number of dead subjects and a fear that Kesh is being prepared as a future scapegoat.

# Basement

## C14. Public Archive Stacks

The basement archive contains old wage records, compensation files, contractor accounts, and shift books. The shelves are densely packed and labelled by year and foundry.

Most records are legitimate.

### What Can Be Found

- Historical worker records.
- Lists of industrial deaths.
- Old maps attached to property accounts.
- A sequence of files removed from the shelves and replaced with empty covers.

### Checks

- **Investigation DC 13:** Notice that files involving workers later selected for Anvil screening have been removed.
- **History DC 15:** Identify an old culvert map showing service passages beneath the modern Foundry Quarter.

## C15. Burn Room

A brick furnace dominates this hot, soot-black room. Officially it destroys expired payroll drafts and damaged records. In practice it is used to erase operational evidence.

A long iron table stands beside the furnace with wire baskets for documents.

### What Can Be Found

- Partially burned grey tags.
- Fragments of a transfer list.
- A copper-lined wrapping strip stained with red-black mineral dust.
- Ash containing melted sealing wax from several directorate offices.
- The red ignition cord from the operations room.

### Checks

- **Investigation DC 12:** Recover a fragment bearing Garron's codename.
- **Investigation DC 15:** Reconstruct enough of a transfer list to identify Furnace Nine.
- **Arcana or Nature DC 15:** Confirm that the red-black dust is veinstone residue.
- **Constitution save DC 12:** Avoid coughing loudly if the furnace has just been used.

## C16. True Operational Vault

The true vault is concealed behind a rolling archive shelf. It is smaller than the public vault and protected by a Crown lock, the Register plate, and a second internal deadbolt.

### Contents

- **240 gp** in covert operational funds.
- Six black glass identity tokens.
- Three sealed packets of red veinstone dust, each no larger than a thumb joint.
- A lead-lined evidence box containing a chipped fragment from an unknown green-black relic.
- Blank grey courier tags.
- Field poisons, antitoxin, and two potions of healing.
- A ledger of agent payments.
- A sealed message tube addressed to Captain Odran Kesh.

### Sealed Message

The message contains a direct instruction from an unnamed superior:

> **Hold the Ironwake office. Recover the Morfael leak. Do not permit House Vaelisar to become part of your inquiry. If the elf Scriptor becomes involved, identify his authority before acting. The aasimar is to be observed, not taken, unless he accesses restricted material.**

This confirms the party has already drawn organised attention.

### Checks

- **Investigation DC 14:** Find the concealed shelf release.
- **Thieves' Tools DC 18:** Open the Crown lock.
- **Arcana or Thieves' Tools DC 16:** Bypass the Register plate.

## C17. Holding Room

This low brick chamber contains two barred cells, a bench, a bucket, and a wall ring. It is not a formal prison and does not appear on the building plan.

One cell is currently empty.

The other may contain a prisoner if the party arrives before Kesh has completed the next transfer.

### Optional Prisoner: Hessa Marr

Hessa Marr is a human furnace labourer in her early thirties. She was selected after surviving a boiler rupture with injuries that should have killed her. She refused a medical assessment and was detained after attempting to flee the quarter.

Hessa knows:

- She was questioned about pain tolerance and previous injuries.
- A clerk asked whether she had living family.
- She heard the words **Furnace Nine** and **preparation ward**.
- She saw one captive with red dust around his gums.
- Kesh did not personally hurt her, but authorised her detention.

Hessa is frightened, furious, and willing to testify if she believes the party can keep her alive.

## C18. Culvert Passage

A reinforced iron door opens into a narrow brick tunnel descending beneath Ironwake Lane. The passage joins an old service culvert and branches toward the river, Magpie Lane approaches, and deeper foundry infrastructure.

Kesh's route is marked by tiny cuts at knee height on the right-hand wall.

### Checks

- **Survival DC 13:** Follow Kesh toward his prepared exit.
- **Perception DC 14:** Notice a side niche containing a hidden cloak and lantern.
- **Investigation DC 15:** Identify scrape marks from restrained prisoners being moved through the tunnel.

The route does not lead directly into the main Anvil facility. It leads to a sealed works junction beneath Furnace Nine, which is the next rung of the investigation.

## C19. Signal Lantern and Observation Eave

The small lantern beneath the eastern eave can display clear, amber, or dull red light through rotating glass panels.

- Clear means normal operation.
- Amber means an expected courier has arrived.
- Red means a restricted door or vault has been opened without authorisation.

A narrow observation slit provides a view of Ironwake Lane, Gate Six, and the approach from Magpie Lane.

### What Can Be Found

- A spyglass.
- A shift timetable.
- A list of recurring patrol gaps.
- Chalk marks recording the party's likely routes if they have already been observed in the Foundry Quarter.

# Captain Odran Kesh

## Summary

**Name:** Captain Odran Kesh  
**Ancestry:** Human  
**Age:** 44  
**Role:** Black Register field commander for the Foundry Quarter  
**Public cover:** Senior security auditor attached to the Foundry Counting House  
**Superior:** Operationally protected through Director Sann Morcant's office  
**Subordinate:** Garron Duskholt, known as Threadknife  
**Current objective:** Contain the Magpie Lane breach, protect the Anvil Work, determine what the party knows, and avoid becoming the official cause of a scandal

## Appearance

Odran Kesh is a broad-shouldered human man of forty-four with the compact build of a career soldier who has remained disciplined after leaving regular service. He is neither enormous nor physically theatrical. His strength shows in posture, balance, and the ease with which he remains still.

His hair is dark brown, cut close at the sides, with iron-grey spreading from both temples. He has grey-green eyes, a straight nose broken once and set properly, and a thin scar running from the edge of his left ear toward the jaw. The little finger of his left hand is missing at the first joint, an old blast injury from military service.

Kesh wears a plain charcoal coat over fitted leather armour, clean grey gloves, dark trousers, and polished but practical boots. He carries a narrow military sabre on his left hip and a compact hand crossbow beneath his coat. Nothing he wears displays rank unless he chooses to pin on his civic auditor's badge.

His most noticeable quality is attention. Kesh looks at doors, hands, distances, and relationships before he looks at decoration.

## Demeanour

Kesh is calm, precise, and almost painfully controlled. He does not shout unless noise itself serves a purpose. He asks short questions and allows silence to pressure the answer.

He is not impressed by threats made for effect. He becomes more cautious around people who speak plainly and already know something true.

Kesh treats the party according to what he sees:

- **Teclis:** A politically dangerous witness whose office might turn a hidden death into a jurisdictional crisis.
- **Cassian:** A soldier or sworn warrior whose conscience may be used against his companions.
- **Alekthar:** A noble-blooded figure connected to routes and houses that the Directorate is already watching.
- **Nakoa:** The least predictable member of the group, especially if the Veylrain relic reacts to him or his ancestry.

## Voice and Mannerisms

- Speaks quietly and uses full names once he learns them.
- Removes one glove when he intends to offer a genuine agreement.
- Touches the missing joint of his left finger when considering a risk.
- Never sits with his back to a door.
- Corrects inaccurate details even when doing so gives away knowledge.
- Looks toward the quietest party member when someone else makes a threat.

## Core Belief

Kesh believes Caer Oryn will eventually face an enemy that ordinary soldiers cannot survive. This belief began during a border deployment in which his unit was ordered to hold a position against a magical threat they had not been trained or equipped to face. Most of the unit died while senior officers later described the loss as acceptable.

He concluded that courage is not enough. He also concluded that officials who demand sacrifice will always hide from the process that creates their defenders.

The Anvil Work disgusts him in practice, but he believes abandoning it would guarantee worse losses later. He tells himself that tighter control, better selection, and fewer public mistakes will make the programme defensible.

This is not innocence. Kesh has ordered kidnappings, concealed deaths, authorised coercive preparation, and protected the people conducting the trials.

## What Kesh Wants

1. Learn exactly what Garron revealed.
2. Recover or destroy evidence taken from Magpie Lane.
3. Prevent the party from reaching Furnace Nine before the current transfer is complete.
4. Keep House Vaelisar outside his operational responsibility.
5. Preserve enough evidence to protect himself if Morcant's office attempts to blame him.
6. Avoid unnecessary civilian deaths inside the Counting House.

## What Kesh Knows

Kesh knows:

- The Obsidian Register name is a field cutout for the Black Register.
- Garron was assigned to stop the party at Magpie Lane.
- Edrin Vaal was an unstable exposure survivor, not merely a courier.
- The lizard idol is a Veylrain anchor-piece used to shape veinstone reactions.
- Room 13 at the Gilded Hart is a rotating dead drop.
- Selected workers, prisoners, soldiers, and isolated civilians are being prepared beneath Furnace Nine.
- The Anvil Work has produced a small number of enhanced survivors and many deaths.
- Director Sann Morcant's office shields access, money, and movement without issuing explicit written orders.
- House Vaelisar was handled by a separate recovery authority under direct civic seal.
- Twelve bodies were removed from the manor and one containment object was transferred.
- Someone above his level is interested in Alekthar's bloodline and Teclis's authority.

## What Kesh Does Not Know

Kesh does not know:

- The true nature of the old god beneath the Godscar.
- The full purpose of the accretion disk or celestial bindings.
- The complete history of the Veylrain relics.
- The true nature of the Vaelisar Dawnmantle.
- Whether Sann Morcant is the architect of the Anvil Work or another protected intermediary.
- Who authorised the oldest experiments.
- Why certain aasimar lines and ancient relics interact with the stone.

## Kesh's Reaction to Garron's Death

If told Garron is dead, Kesh does not visibly grieve.

He asks:

> "Did he die before or after he gave you my name?"

If the party admits executing Garron after interrogation:

> "Then do not speak to me of law. Speak of leverage, evidence, and what you intend to do next. Those are the honest subjects available to us."

Privately, Kesh is angry. Garron was difficult, unreliable, and increasingly reckless, but he was still Kesh's responsibility. Kesh also understands that the party is willing to kill prisoners, which changes his approach from intimidation to containment.

## Opening Lines

> "You came through a controlled quarter, entered a payroll office, and climbed toward the only room that mattered. I will assume this is not about missing wages."

> "Garron missed his report. You arrived carrying his questions. That narrows the possibilities."

> "You may call it the Obsidian Register. That was the name given to people who were never expected to reach this room."

> "Before anyone draws steel, look through the glass. There are thirty workers downstairs who do not know my name. Decide whether they deserve to become part of your method."

## If Asked Who Commands Him

> "Orders are protected through Morcant's office. That is not the same as saying Morcant writes them. Men at his level survive by ensuring verbs never touch their names."

## If Asked About the Anvil Work

> "It is an attempt to produce soldiers who remain standing when ordinary training, armour, and prayer fail. The method is brutal because the material is brutal. That is not a defence. It is the reason the work remains hidden."

## If Asked About Edrin

> "Edrin Vaal survived the wrong part of the process. His body adapted and his mind did not. He was moved for observation. Then he escaped, was released, or was used to test the route. I was not given a clean answer."

## If Asked About House Vaelisar

> "Not mine. Not Garron's. Not the Register's. A direct civic recovery team sealed that house and removed twelve bodies. One object left under containment. I was ordered not to ask what it was."

## If Asked Where the Captives Are

Kesh initially says:

> "No one you can reach without crossing half the quarter under alarm."

Under pressure, bargain, or successful investigation:

> "Beneath Furnace Nine. The old inspection wards. The public entrance is sealed. The service junction opens after the third shift bell."

## Negotiating with Kesh

Kesh will bargain if the party demonstrates that they possess evidence capable of surviving their deaths.

He may offer:

- Safe passage from the Foundry Quarter.
- The release of Hessa Marr.
- The route to Furnace Nine after the current transfer has moved.
- Confirmation of Edrin's file.
- A meeting through room 13 with a higher intermediary.
- A list of low-level agents in exchange for protecting the deeper programme.

He wants:

- Garron's documents and any grey tags recovered at Magpie Lane.
- Silence concerning the Counting House.
- Time to relocate exposed staff.
- The Veylrain anchor-piece if the party possesses it.
- A promise that Teclis will not immediately deliver the evidence to a public civic office.

Kesh honours narrow agreements exactly as worded. He does not honour the spirit of an agreement that threatens the programme.

## Combat Behaviour

Kesh does not begin combat merely because the party reaches his office. He talks while assessing positions and waiting for Sergeant Ashford to close routes below.

If combat begins:

1. Kesh orders civilians cleared or contained away from the fighting.
2. He uses the desk and doorway to restrict movement.
3. He targets the most mobile or magically disruptive character first.
4. He uses smoke to break line of sight if surrounded.
5. He withdraws toward the basement rather than fighting to the death.
6. He attempts to take a conscious prisoner if this can be done without sacrificing escape.

Kesh surrenders if reduced below one-quarter of his hit points, cut off from the culvert, and shown that his records have already been secured. He does not surrender to promises alone.

## Suggested Fifth Edition Profile

Use a strengthened **Veteran/Spy hybrid** suitable for a capable low-to-mid-level party.

**Armour Class:** 16  
**Hit Points:** 78  
**Speed:** 30 ft.  
**STR 14, DEX 16, CON 14, INT 16, WIS 15, CHA 14**

**Saving Throws:** Dex +6, Wis +5  
**Skills:** Deception +5, Insight +5, Investigation +6, Perception +5, Stealth +6  
**Senses:** Passive Perception 15  
**Languages:** Common and one campaign-appropriate military or trade language

### Traits

**Calculated Defence.** As a reaction, Kesh adds 3 to his AC against one attack that would hit him, provided he can see the attacker.

**Field Command, 3 per day.** As a bonus action, Kesh chooses one allied creature within 30 feet that can hear him. The creature may use its reaction to move up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks or make one weapon attack.

**Registerer's Eye.** Kesh has advantage on Insight checks made to identify deliberate lies concerning names, orders, routes, or operational responsibility.

**Smoke Capsule, 1 per day.** Kesh breaks an alchemical capsule at a point within 10 feet. A 10-foot-radius area becomes heavily obscured until the end of Kesh's next turn unless dispersed by strong wind.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Kesh makes two sabre attacks or two hand-crossbow attacks.

**Sabre.** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 damage, or 1d8 + 3.

**Hand Crossbow.** Ranged Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. Hit: 6 damage, or 1d6 + 3.

**Pommel Strike.** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 bludgeoning damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or be unable to take reactions until the start of its next turn.

Adjust his hit points and number of guards rather than increasing damage if the party is lower level.

# Running the Confrontation

## Kesh Has Prepared for Questions, Not the Party

Kesh knows investigators may eventually reach the Counting House. He does not expect the specific combination of Teclis, Cassian, Alekthar, and Nakoa unless reports have already identified them.

He should be dangerous because he controls the environment, not because he possesses impossible knowledge.

## Preferred Scene Structure

### 1. Entry

The party chooses public access, disguise, stealth, rooftop entry, or culvert approach.

### 2. Discovery

They find enough evidence to understand that the public office conceals something larger.

### 3. Pressure

A clerk, guard, or alarm begins the lockdown. Civilians become a practical and moral complication.

### 4. Meeting Kesh

Kesh speaks before fighting. He tests what they know and offers a narrow bargain.

### 5. Choice

The party may arrest, kill, expose, bargain with, follow, or allow Kesh to escape.

### 6. Consequence

The city responds according to what witnesses, records, and bodies remain.

# Outcomes

## Kesh Is Captured

Kesh becomes a dangerous prisoner. He will cooperate only if convinced that Morcant's office intends to erase him. He can provide the Furnace Nine route, but withholds names above Morcant until given meaningful protection.

The Directorate will attempt to recover or kill him before he reaches an open court.

## Kesh Is Killed

The Black Register loses an experienced field commander but not the operation. Damaris may flee with records. Morcant's office describes Kesh as a corrupt auditor running an unauthorised criminal cell.

The party gains evidence but loses the person best able to explain its structure.

## Kesh Escapes

Kesh becomes a recurring antagonist who now understands the party's methods. He does not pursue revenge for its own sake. He isolates contacts, changes routes, pressures witnesses, and tries to force the party into public mistakes.

He may later approach them when he discovers that his own superiors intend to sacrifice him.

## The Counting House Burns

If the burn room or red drawer spreads fire, the public archive becomes endangered. Workers lose wage histories, death claims, and compensation records. Even a justified attack creates civilian hardship.

Damaris will risk herself to save legitimate records before operational files, revealing where her loyalties have shifted.

## The Party Leaves Quietly With Evidence

This is the most damaging outcome for the Register. The office may continue functioning for several hours before anyone understands what was taken.

Kesh responds by locking down Furnace Nine, warning room 13, and identifying which party member is most likely to preserve copies.

# Immediate Leads From the Counting House

The party can leave with several clear directions:

1. **Furnace Nine:** A sealed works junction and preparation ward beneath the Foundry Quarter.
2. **Room 13 at the Gilded Hart:** A rotating dead drop connected to higher intermediaries.
3. **Director Sann Morcant:** The office providing administrative protection.
4. **House Vaelisar:** A separate but related civic recovery operation involving twelve bodies and one containment object.
5. **The Veylrain Anchor-Piece:** A relic used to stabilise or direct veinstone exposure.
6. **The Ghost Payroll:** A record of agents, missing subjects, dead workers, and false identities hidden inside normal civic accounts.

The Counting House should reveal a functioning system, not the final villain. The party has reached a command post inside the machine. The machine continues above, below, and around it.
