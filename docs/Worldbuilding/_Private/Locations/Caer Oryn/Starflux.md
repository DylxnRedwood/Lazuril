# Starflux

_Private DM-facing location and witness notes for the High Quarter of Caer Oryn._

## Overview

Starflux is a refined but affordable coffee shop on the corner of Hartglass Row, close to the Gilded Hart and within walking distance of House Vaelisar. Directorate clerks, wealthy students, household servants and junior academics use it as neutral ground away from their employers.

The shop occupies the ground floor of a narrow pale-stone building. Its dark-blue interior is fitted with brass lamps, smoked-glass windows and a slow-turning orrery suspended above the central tables. The atmosphere is quiet, warm and discreet, smelling of roasted coffee, beeswax and spiced pastries.

Small tables are spaced far enough apart that conversations do not carry easily. Three curtained booths at the rear can be used for private meetings.

## Maelin Orra

Maelin Orra is Starflux's human proprietor and principal barista. She is in her early fifties, with tightly braided grey hair, spectacles and a measured, observant manner.

She previously managed the kitchens and accounts of a minor High Quarter household and understands how vulnerable servants can become when wealthy employers fall.

Maelin is protective of Resha and will not allow strangers to interrogate her aggressively. Teclis has already made a poor first impression by waking her after closing with a magically summoned music box. She nevertheless confirmed that Resha was due to begin work the following morning.

Maelin permits a private conversation if the party explains that they entered House Vaelisar, describes the black orb or shows genuine concern for Resha's safety.

## Menu

- Starflux Black: strong, bitter coffee.
- High Terrace: coffee with steamed milk, cinnamon and honey.
- Night Scholar: sweet coffee flavoured with orange peel.
- Ember Chocolate: thick chocolate with ginger and nutmeg.
- Orchard Fold: apple, pear and almond pastry.
- Servant's Breakfast: bread, cheese, boiled egg and watered coffee.

# Resha Venn

## Overview

Resha Venn is a twenty-six-year-old human woman who served House Vaelisar for six years. She maintained first-floor rooms, assisted during formal meals and knew the manor's service passages and household routines.

She survived the massacre by hiding in a maintenance compartment behind the first-floor linen cupboards. During the destruction of the manor she suffered a severe blow to the head. She now experiences headaches, uncertain memory, disturbed sleep and fear of darkness.

Resha is slight, with pale brown skin, dark eyes and unevenly cut black hair. A thin scar runs beneath her left ear. She speaks cautiously and frequently repeats that the Directorate told her she was confused.

She responds badly to threats. Patient questioning, familiar details from the manor or evidence of the black orb help her reconstruct what happened.

## What She Knew Before the Attack

Resha knew that twelve male members of House Vaelisar took part in private family observances. Servants called them the Dawnmantles, although the term was never explained to her.

She understood that the upper hall was prepared for their ceremonies and that servants were forbidden from entering during them.

## The Attack

Resha was preparing an upstairs guest room when every lamp and fireplace in the manor dimmed at once. The front doors then opened without being forced and without triggering the household wards.

She saw approximately eight black-clad attackers moving through the manor. They carried polished black discs that seemed to absorb light, communicated through brief instructions and appeared to know the layout in advance.

One carried a list of names. Another possessed keys or another means of opening secured interior doors.

The attackers gathered the twelve Dawnmantles and moved them towards the upper hall. Their leader was called the Cantor, a tall woman wearing a black-glass half-mask marked by a single vertical silver line.

Resha remembers the Cantor saying:

> "The line must be witnessed before it is extinguished."

The killings occurred one at a time. With each death the manor grew colder and a low vibration passed through the walls. After the final death, something expanded through the upper hall with enough force to shatter windows, destroy internal walls and tear open the floor.

When Resha regained consciousness, she heard something moving in the ruined manor that did not sound human.

## Sharite Evidence

Resha did not recognise the attackers as Sharites, but remembers:

- All natural and magical light was deliberately suppressed.
- Several attackers carried black mirror-like discs.
- Death was described as extinguishment.
- One attacker said, "May loss make her whole."
- The Cantor described the Vaelisar bloodline as light that had to be removed.

Cassian or another character familiar with Shar can identify these as strong signs of organised Sharite involvement.

## How She Survived

Resha concealed herself in a service compartment behind a linen cupboard. One attacker entered the passage and stopped close to her hiding place, but another called him away, saying:

> "The servants are irrelevant unless they carry the blood."

This confirms that the attack was intended to destroy the Vaelisar bloodline rather than simply murder everyone present.

## The Civic Directorate

Directorate containment officers found Resha several hours later. They covered her head, removed her through a rear entrance and questioned her for two days under the authority of Roderick Valeborne. She never met Valeborne personally.

The Directorate told her that:

- An unstable magical artefact destroyed the manor.
- Any intruders entered after the magical incident.
- Her head injury caused her to mistake ceremonial clothing for attackers.
- The Vaelisar family members she heard were probably already dead.
- Claims of Sharite involvement would cause panic.

She was ordered not to discuss the Dawnmantles, the Cantor, the attackers or anything she heard concerning another family. She signed a statement claiming she witnessed only the aftermath of an accident, received a small payment and was warned that further discussion could be treated as obstruction of a civic investigation.

The Directorate account is false. The attackers arrived before the magical event, family members were alive when taken upstairs, and the officials already knew the term Dawnmantle before Resha used it.

## What the Party Can Learn

### Given Freely

- The destruction was a planned attack, not an accident.
- The attackers knew the manor's layout and security.
- Twelve male Vaelisars were gathered alive for the ritual.
- A masked woman called the Cantor led the attackers.
- The Directorate pressured Resha to change her account.

### Once Resha Trusts Them

- One attacker carried a complete household list.
- The attackers possessed working keys or magical access.
- The ritual occurred around the upper hall where the orb appeared.
- At least one attacker wore what resembled a formal Directorate coat beneath his cloak.
- The orb began as a small dark sphere and grew with each death.

### Deep Memory

Careful questioning, Alekthar explaining what happened to his family or showing Resha an image of the orb causes her to remember the Cantor saying:

> "Cael Tharos was incomplete. There will be no surviving ember here."

Another attacker replied:

> "The first line failed because the child was unprepared."

Resha does not understand these words, but they connect the Vaelisar massacre to the failed ritual against Alekthar's family.

## Physical Evidence

Resha retained part of her maid's uniform. One cuff bears a black stain left when an attacker briefly grabbed her arm. The mark remains cold and slightly dulls reflected light.

Examination may reveal powdered obsidian, black ritual wax, humanoid blood and residue associated with shadow or planar magic.

## Further Leads

Resha can identify:

- The hidden passage behind the first-floor linen cupboards.
- A maintenance hatch behind the upper hall wall.
- A locked compartment beneath the household steward's desk where ceremonial schedules were kept.
- A servants' staircase connecting the kitchens to the upper eastern corridor.
- The location where one attacker was injured, where blood or damaged equipment may remain beneath the stair boards.

## What Resha Wants

Resha wants her testimony preserved somewhere the Directorate cannot alter it. She also wants reassurance that other surviving servants are safe and that the black orb cannot harm anyone beyond the manor.

She may become a continuing witness, a target for the Sharites, and the first person able to connect House Vaelisar to Cael Tharos.
