# Hall of Twelve Dawns

> [!warning] Private DM Notes
> The hidden tome describes all twelve Dawnmantles as living because it was last updated immediately before the massacre.
>
> The Hall remains clean, intact and prepared for ceremony. There are only two visible contradictions:
>
> - Every preserved blood vial has darkened and crystallised.
> - The illuminated name of every current bearer has gone out.
>
> There are no death entries, last words, blood trails, damaged pillars, Sharite carvings or written explanations inside the chamber.

## Summary

**Location:** Directly beneath the billiards room of [[House Vaelisar]]  
**Entrance:** Beneath the large green carpet, through a concealed stone hatch and down the staircase  
**Formal name:** The Hall of Twelve Dawns  
**Family name:** The Lower Hall  
**Ritual:** The Rite of the Carried Dawn  
**Hidden tome:** The Concordance of Twelve Dawns  
**Required Dawnmantles:** Twelve living, fully inducted bearers  
**Bearers recorded by the tome:** Twelve  
**Actual living Dawnmantles:** None

The Hall is the ritual heart of House Vaelisar. Twelve inducted bearers maintained the Vaelisar portion of the wider aasimar lattice binding the old god beneath Lazuril.

The Sharites killed the complete Twelve during an inverted ritual. Their simultaneous deaths destroyed the Vaelisar seal-thread and created the black orb upstairs.

None of this is recorded in the Concordance because no Vaelisar survived to update it.

# The Hidden Truth

## The Aasimar Bindings

The old aasimar houses maintain separate portions of an ancient living prison.

Examples include:

- House Vaelisar and the twelvefold Dawnmantle.
- House Amakiir and the Bound Radiance.
- Cael Tharos and its inherited seal-work.
- Other houses marked upon the Dawnline Map.

If one house-binding fails, the prison weakens.

If all of them fail, the old god is released.

## What Happened at House Vaelisar

Immediately before the incident:

- All twelve Dawnmantle stations were occupied.
- The scattered bearers were recalled to the manor.
- Tarian Vaelisar had recently replaced Rathan Vaelisar.
- Other relatives and household witnesses were present.
- The family believed it was preparing an emergency strengthening.
- Sharites infiltrated or compromised the gathering.
- The legitimate rite was inverted.
- All twelve fully inducted Dawnmantles were ritually killed.
- Other family members were murdered to prevent rapid restoration.
- The complete collapse of the twelvefold bond formed the black orb upstairs.

## What Happened at Cael Tharos

The attack upon Alekthar's family followed the same general plan.

The fully inducted Bound Radiance holders were killed, but the ritual failed to form a stable black orb because Alekthar's sister was included despite not being fully inducted.

Her connection had begun but was incomplete. The attackers therefore did not sacrifice an exact set of fully bound holders.

The result was:

- The inducted Bound Radiance holders died.
- The Amakiir binding was devastated.
- No stable planar wound formed.
- No black orb appeared.
- The attackers later corrected the mistake at House Vaelisar.

# Entrance from the Billiards Room

The large green carpet in the billiards room conceals a broad stone hatch.

The billiards table is positioned slightly off-centre so that it does not stand directly over the opening.

## Finding the Entrance

- **Perception DC 12:** Notices that the carpet rises slightly near its centre.
- **Investigation DC 13:** Determines that something broad lies beneath it.
- **Strength DC 12:** Moves the billiards table far enough to roll back the carpet.
- **Two characters working together:** Move the table without a check.
- **Knocking on the floor:** Reveals the hollow space immediately.

## The Stone Hatch

The hatch is dark grey stone bordered with narrow brass strips. Twelve worn sunbursts surround a recessed lifting ring.

It is counterweighted and intended for concealment rather than defence.

- **Investigation DC 11:** Finds the lifting ring.
- **Investigation DC 13:** Finds the concealed counterweight release.
- **Strength DC 13:** Raises the hatch without the release.

## Read Aloud: Opening the Hatch

> Beneath the green carpet, the wooden floor gives way to a broad slab of dark stone. Twelve worn sunbursts encircle an iron ring at its centre.
>
> The hatch lifts more easily than its size suggests.
>
> Beneath it, a narrow staircase descends into muted amber light.

# Entering the Hall

## Read Aloud

> The staircase opens into a long ceremonial chamber of dark stone, brass and pale geometric tile.
>
> Twelve round pillars stand in two ordered rows beside a central processional path. Four braziers burn with steady golden flames, and every surface appears clean, maintained and ready for ceremony.
>
> At the far end, a carved lectern stands between two broad ritual wells.
>
> Each pillar bears the name of a living Dawnmantle recorded in bright golden script.
>
> Or it should.
>
> All twelve names are dark.
>
> A narrow glass vial is set into each pillar. Whatever blood it once held has hardened into opaque black crystal.
>
> Nothing else in the chamber appears damaged, disturbed or abandoned.

Do not explain the significance immediately.

The room should look as though its owners could return and begin the ceremony at any moment.

# Chamber Features

## The Southern Arch

The staircase descends beneath an arch bearing the Vaelisar words:

> **The Dawn Is Carried, Not Chosen.**

The inner face reads:

> **Twelve were appointed. Twelve must endure.**

## The Processional Way

The central aisle is divided into twelve ritual stages. During an induction, the candidate moved between the pillars and acknowledged each station.

The floor, gold inlay and ceremonial geometry remain intact.

There are no visible Sharite modifications or written signs of the massacre.

## The Twelve Pillars

Each pillar contains:

- The station name.
- The station inscription.
- The names of previous holders.
- The name of the current bearer recorded before the massacre.
- A sealed glass blood vial.
- A hand-shaped recess used during induction.
- A link to the wider ritual geometry.

While a bearer lives:

- His preserved blood remains fluid.
- His engraved name shines with warm golden light.

Now:

- Every vial is dark and crystallised.
- Every current bearer name is unlit.

Everything else remains unchanged.

### Checks

- **Investigation DC 12:** Identifies the current bearer recorded upon each pillar.
- **Medicine DC 13:** Determines that the black crystal was once blood.
- **Religion DC 14:** Recognises that the pillars are ritual functions rather than noble ranks.
- **Arcana DC 14:** Understands that a living bearer should keep his blood fluid and his name illuminated.
- **Religion DC 15:** Understands that the Hall was designed to maintain twelve simultaneous living bonds.
- **Arcana or Religion DC 17:** Determines that all twelve bonds ended within a very short period.
- **Arcana or Religion DC 19:** Understands that the complete Vaelisar bond has failed, but the Hall does not reveal who caused it or how.

## The Eight Ancestral Niches

The niches contain tablets for:

1. The Arkborne Line.
2. The First Caer Oryn Line.
3. The Ashen Dawn and the False Saints.
4. The Compact Line.
5. The Main Vaelisar House.
6. The Vaelis Branch.
7. The Hearthward Vaelisars.
8. The Southgate Cadet Line.

The tablets remain intact and current to the final convocation.

## The Four Braziers

The braziers represent inheritance, witness, sacrifice and return.

All four continue to burn normally. Their flames do not indicate whether the bearers are alive.

## The Two Wells

### The Well of Remembered Blood

Used during induction to compare the candidate's blood with the established line.

The well remains clean and prepared for use.

### The Well of First Light

Used to receive and reflect the first light of dawn during the completion of the rite.

It remains intact and functional.

# The Main Lectern

The northern lectern contains a secret internal compartment.

Its top bears three symbols:

- A closed eye.
- An open hand.
- A rising sun.

Pressing them in that order opens the compartment.

This represents:

1. Knowledge kept.
2. Duty accepted.
3. Dawn carried.

### Finding the Compartment

- **Investigation DC 14:** Finds the fine seams around the front panel.
- **Religion or History DC 15:** Deduces the intended sequence.
- **Thieves' Tools DC 16:** Opens the compartment mechanically.
- **Speaking the house words while touching the lectern:** Causes the correct symbols to warm.

# The Concordance of Twelve Dawns

The tome is a heavy vellum volume bound in dark leather and reinforced with brass.

Its cover bears twelve golden sunbursts arranged around a larger central circle.

The title page reads:

> **The Concordance of Twelve Dawns**
>
> **Being the names, stations, obligations and manner of continuation of those who carry the keeping of House Vaelisar.**
>
> **Let no name be removed because it brings shame.**
>
> **The house may reject the bearer. The burden does not.**

## Critical Canon Rule

The Concordance contains no record of the massacre.

It does not contain:

- Death entries for the Twelve.
- Notes that anyone is missing.
- A description of the Eclipse Rite.
- A reference to Sharites.
- A connection between the Hall and the black orb.
- A final warning written during or after the killings.

The last writer believed all twelve bearers were alive.

# The Rite of the Carried Dawn

The tome provides the complete induction procedure as an established family practice.

## Preparation

The candidate remains within the manor for twelve days and studies one station each day.

He must not:

- Leave the estate.
- Spill blood in anger.
- Deliberately invoke celestial power.
- Enter the Hall alone.
- Speak the complete oath before the ceremony.

Each evening the Keeper asks:

> What do you inherit?

> That which was carried before me.

> What do you surrender?

> That which belongs only to me.

> What do you become?

> A bearer, not a master.

## Opening the Rite

> **Patriarch:** Twelve were appointed.  
> **Bearers:** Twelve must endure.  
> **Patriarch:** Blood has diminished.  
> **Bearers:** The dawn has not forgotten.

## The Circuit

The candidate touches each pillar and says:

> I acknowledge the station.

## The Naming of the Blood

The candidate's ancestry is recited back to Seraphel Vaelisar.

Exiles and cadet branches must be named because the binding recognises blood rather than family approval.

> **Keeper:** The house may cast out the name.  
> **Bearers:** The blood remains.

## The Carried Oath

> **Patriarch:** What stands above?  
> **Candidate:** The house.
>
> **Patriarch:** What stands below?  
> **Candidate:** The keeping.
>
> **Patriarch:** What binds the two?  
> **Candidate:** The blood that carries dawn.
>
> **Patriarch:** Is the dawn yours?  
> **Candidate:** It is not mine.
>
> **Patriarch:** Is the burden chosen?  
> **Candidate:** It was chosen before me.
>
> **Patriarch:** Will you carry what you did not make?  
> **Candidate:** I will carry it until blood, memory and light have failed.
>
> **Bearers:** The station opens.  
> **Candidate:** Then let it know me.

The vacant pillar accepts the candidate, the sunburst mark forms beneath his right collarbone, and his name illuminates upon the stone.

# Session Discovery Structure

## What the Tome Tells the Players

- Twelve living Dawnmantles were recorded.
- All twelve had gathered at the manor.
- Tarian had recently restored the final station.
- The family believed it was preparing an emergency strengthening.
- The Dawnmantle was part of an inherited duty larger than ordinary nobility.

## What the Chamber Adds

Only two facts contradict the tome:

- Every blood vial has crystallised and darkened.
- Every current bearer's illuminated name has gone out.

The chamber does not explain:

- Who killed them.
- How they died.
- Whether the deaths occurred in the Hall.
- Why the black orb formed.
- What role the Sharites played.

Those conclusions require evidence from elsewhere in the manor, the orb, later investigation or outside sources.

## DM Conclusion

The book says twelve living people assembled beneath House Vaelisar.

The chamber quietly shows that all twelve living bonds are gone.
