# Caer Oryn: Arms, Alchemy and Magic Shops

> [!dm] DM-Only City Commerce
> This file provides armament, alchemical and magical shops across the High Quarter, Academy Quarter, Guild Quarter, Foundry Quarter and River Districts of [[Caer Oryn]]. The businesses are presented from highest to lowest prestige rather than grouped by district.
>
> The party is currently level 4 and short of coin. Most shops therefore offer useful mundane equipment, consumables, repairs, trade-ins and low-level magical items rather than shelves of powerful treasure.

## Rules and Stock Assumptions

- This file uses official fifth-edition items from the 2014 *Player's Handbook*, *Dungeon Master's Guide* and *Xanathar's Guide to Everything*.
- No item listed below has homebrew mechanics. Shop names, proprietors, prices, availability and purchasing restrictions are campaign material.
- Mundane equipment uses the normal *Player's Handbook* price unless a different local price is shown.
- Magic-item prices are Caer Oryn retail prices rather than universal rules. They may be altered without changing the item itself.
- Permanent uncommon magic items are scarce. A listed item normally means one is currently available unless otherwise stated.
- Common magical curios, potions and low-level spell scrolls return to stock more frequently.
- Foundry weapons, controlled poisons, military-grade armour and uncommon magic items may require identification, guild sponsorship, a Scriptor credential, temple standing, a military licence or a Directorate writ.

# 1. The Sealed Star

**Quarter:** High Quarter  
**Type:** Prestigious magic-item salon  
**Proprietor:** Seraphine Orrel, human woman, late 50s  
**Access:** Appointment, reputable introduction, recognised office or a convincing display of wealth

The Sealed Star occupies the ground floor of a narrow black-stone townhouse opposite a formal garden used by ministerial families. There is no display window. Its door bears a silver seven-pointed star pressed beneath a layer of clear glass. Visitors surrender weapons at the vestibule and give their full name to a clerk before entering.

Seraphine Orrel is controlled, exact and almost impossible to impress. She does not describe herself as a merchant. She arranges lawful transfers of arcane property between people whose names she considers worth recording.

> [!readaloud] Read Aloud
> The salon is quiet enough to hear the clockwork inside the walls. Dark cabinets stand beneath glass globes, each containing a single object and a handwritten card. Two clerks watch from polished desks while a silver-haired woman waits beside an empty chair, apparently expecting you to explain why you belong here.

# 5. Red Anvil Requisition Hall

**Type:** Crown-licensed military armaments exchange  
**Proprietor:** Inspector-Master Corren Hale, human man, early 60s

The Red Anvil is less a shop than a procurement hall. Weapons arrive in numbered racks, armour is inspected against stamped measurements and every transaction requires a signature. The hall supplies watch units, caravan companies, private guards and approved military contractors.

Corren Hale is a former quartermaster who assumes everyone is attempting either theft or paperwork fraud until proven otherwise.

## Restocking Guidance

- **Mundane weapons, armour and adventuring goods:** restore common stock every few days.
- **Potion of Healing:** roll or choose 1d4 available at Inkglass, Measure and Mortar and Lowwater after each tenday.
- **Other potions and spell scrolls:** replace one or two items after meaningful downtime, a caravan arrival or a completed quest.
- **Common permanent magic items:** replace one item per shop after one to three tendays.
- **Uncommon permanent magic items:** do not automatically restock. Replace them only when the party's level, wealth or story makes the purchase useful.
- **Rare or stronger magic items:** not available through normal retail at the party's current level. They require patronage, auction, recovery, commission or a major favour.
