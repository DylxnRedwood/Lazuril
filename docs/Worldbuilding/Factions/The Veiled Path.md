# The Veiled Path

## Description

The Veiled Path is known across Arkhaven as a thieves’ guild, though even that may be too simple.

On the surface, it is a continent-spanning criminal network of burglars, smugglers, fences, counterfeiters, cutpurses, dockside runners, corrupt clerks, safehouse keepers, and quiet brokers who can move stolen goods across borders better than most legal caravans.

It is spoken of in ports, taverns, prisons, counting houses, gambling rooms, and alleyways where people know better than to say the name too loudly.

No one agrees where it began. Havenor blames the pirate routes. The Crown blames Leridian agents. Leridia blames Crown corruption. Others say it is older than both and simply learned to wear whatever mask each city expected.

That is as much as most people know. It is probably as much as they are meant to know.

## Purpose

The Veiled Path exists to steal, smuggle, hide, move, and sell things that the lawful world cannot or will not touch.

Coin, relics, letters, prisoners, poisons, names, maps, noble secrets, black-market goods, forged permits, and forbidden books all pass through its hands.

To most people, it is just crime made organised.

To criminals, it is something larger and less comfortable than that.

## Rules

Do not steal from another marked member.

Do not expose a safehouse.

Do not speak the Path’s signs to law, crown, temple, scriptor, or torturer.

Pay the veil-tithe.

Never ask who sits at the end of the Path.

A job may be refused, but a debt may not be forgotten.

## Personality

The Veiled Path is charming, theatrical, secretive, and superstitious.

Its members like coded phrases, hidden doors, marked coins, false names, and stories that contradict each other on purpose.

Most people think they are just thieves.

Most thieves hope that is true.
