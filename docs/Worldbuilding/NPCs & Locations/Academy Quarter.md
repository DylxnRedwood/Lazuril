# Academy Quarter: NPCs & Locations

> Player-facing reference for NPCs met and locations visited in the Academy Quarter of Caer Oryn. Updated after Session 4 - The Hart of the Matter.

## NPCs Met

| NPC | First Encountered | What the Party Knows |
|---|---|---|
| Quincy Adams | Inkglass Apothecary | An apothecary and healer who already had a warm working relationship with Teclis. He identified the red dust recovered from Edrin Vaal as veinstone, agreed to examine the sample and supplied the party with two heavily discounted healing potions. |
| Elowen | Inkglass Apothecary | Quincy's assistant. She treated Nakoa's injuries in the back room despite never having provided first aid to a tabaxi before. |

## Mentioned, Not Met

No additional named NPCs were mentioned during the party's recorded visit to the Academy Quarter.

## Locations Visited

| Location | What Happened There |
|---|---|
| Inkglass Apothecary | The party brought Nakoa here for treatment and showed Quincy Adams the red dust recovered from Edrin Vaal. Quincy identified it as veinstone from the Crimson Chain, explained some of its former uses in weapons and magical implements and agreed to study the sample further. |

## Known Open Threads

- Quincy needs more veinstone material before he can conduct a fuller examination.
- The party intended to return after giving Quincy several days to inspect the sample.
- Red veinstone is the most common known variety, implying that other colours and properties may exist.
- Veinstone mining has largely ceased, while much of the remaining material is believed to be held by noble houses and the Crown.
- The precise way veinstone was used on Edrin Vaal, and whether it caused his reanimation and violent surges, remains unknown.
